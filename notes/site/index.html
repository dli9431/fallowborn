Fallowborn

A free “grand” strategy game in your browser. You start as a serf.

Serf → Freeholder → Gentry → Baron → Count → Duke → King → Emperor

▶  Play free in your browser Stable build on itch.io

An English serf who’ll die in the mud he was born in. A blacksmith’s apprentice in Aquitaine. A hedge knight with a good horse and an empty purse. Pick one of seven lives and drag your bloodline up the ladder before it dies out — one funeral at a time, while ~65 AI realms fight their own wars and redraw the map around you.
