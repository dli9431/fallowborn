### Wars now march on the map

Day two. Yesterday the game went up. Today it grew an army.

Until now a war in Fallowborn happened in a menu — you clicked, a number moved, a county
changed color. That's over.

**Wars take the field.** When realms go to war they raise hosts now: columns of spearmen
that stand on the map and march province to province. Two hostile hosts in the same
province fight there, in the open, where you can watch it. Muster your own host (the 🚩
deed, or answer the muster event), hire companies to swell its ranks, then tap the host and
tap a province to send it marching.

The old shortcuts bent to fit. A siege now needs your host actually standing in the county
you mean to take — an enemy you keep off your land can't conjure a conquest out of the war
screen anymore. And the war council's pitched battle is only for an enemy with no army in
the field; if they've got a host, you beat it the honest way and hunt it down.

**Watch the world without you — 👁 Observe mode.** New Game now offers a start with no
character, no events, no choices. You pick it and the centuries just run: realms rise, war,
fall, and redraw the map while you read the chronicle of every war, battle, succession, and
conquest across the whole map — not only the ones near home. It's the world you seeded,
playing itself. Find out what 867 becomes when nobody's steering.

**Renounce your betters — ⚑ Declare independence.** A new deed lets any sworn lord or baron
sitting on 200+ prestige tear up his oath and fight under his own banner. It puts you on a
war footing and calls the muster, same as a lord defending his own dirt. (It also quietly
rescued the barons who used to get stranded as "independent" with nowhere to climb when
their liege's house died out.)

**And so the map reads at a glance:**

- Hosts stand on a disc in their realm's color; two hosts clashing in a province wear a ⚔
  for the day.
- Yours always marches under green, your enemy's under red.
- Markers are bigger and easier to tap, and drawn on the province they actually stand in —
  no more armies adrift in the Channel.
- A shattered host fields only a remnant while it re-forms; a host resting on home ground
  slowly refills its ranks; every leg of a march costs its true time.
- A Settings dialog with a tap-friendly speed chooser, a Changelog button in the menu, and
  a faster fast-forward.

That's v1.10 through v1.14, shipped over one evening. The barley is still real. Now it can
be trampled.
