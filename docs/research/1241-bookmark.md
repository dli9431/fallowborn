Spring 1241 regional first pass

The source tables in data/bookmark_1241_scaffold_b.js hold seven independently declared blocks for a possible Spring 1241 world. They do not register a bookmark, offer a start, initialize an army or change an existing campaign. Their county division and ordered settlement slots come from the retained 1204 inhabited_v2 world on europe_regional_2, as declared in data/bookmark_1204.js and data/bookmark_1204_terrain.js. These are inherited simulation inputs. Development, population shares, district boundaries and generated settlement labels are not a 1241 census or independent local historical research.

Quadrant b: southern Rus, northern Britain and Ireland, Iberia, northern Germany and Italy

The blocks contain 132 inhabited counties: rus_south (7), britain_north_ireland (25), iberia_north_interior (20), iberia_south_east (30), empire_north (10), italy_north (20) and italy_south_papacy (20). Every county appears once. All seven retain their source resident identities, community shares, development and settlement order. Title chains are copied promotion groupings rather than findings of contemporary possession. The source ids remain physical references; a modern fill name does not establish a medieval town foundation. No new culture, faith, institution or physical seed is supplied by these blocks.

Twenty-three courts are declared locally. The only outside owner references are england_1241 and hre_1241, each to be defined once by its owning region in the complete world. The same imperial court covers the inherited Sicilian royal counties. This compresses distinct crowns into one ordinary public-resource owner and does not establish a united imperial and Sicilian administration. It supplies neither a second Frederick II nor proof of his direct possession of every Italian county. A complete composition must resolve both external courts before activation.

Ferdinand III has the working birth year 1201, within a reconstructed 1199-1202 interval around the reference's approximate date. James I uses 1208. Gregory IX uses the working year 1170, within an approximate 1168-1172 interval. These are background ruler inputs, not complete playable biographies. Skills and traits are simulation settings. No historical spouse, child, friend, officeholder or court household is inferred from a ruler's name. Every other locally declared principal is explicitly generated, with simulation birth 1206 within the allowed adult interval 1186-1221. This includes the Scottish, island, Irish, Navarrese, Portuguese, Andalusi, German, Venetian and Sardinian local courts and all four southern Rus placeholders. Their names and exact authority remain unresolved historical questions.

Southern Rus uses bounded Kyiv, Chernihiv, Turov and Volhynian local administrations. Their source-sized jurisdictions preserve resident communities without reinstating the old sovereigns or materializing Danylo, Vasylko, an occupying commander or a tribute collector. No superior occupation or recognition claim is initialized. These generated local authorities are a first-pass gameplay cover, with ordinary territorial powers; they do not establish historical independence after the conquests. Resolving occupation, effective possession, claims and revenue remains necessary for a more exact opening.

Northern Britain and Ireland keep separate Scottish, island, Connacht, Munster and Tir Eoghain groupings. Source English-administered rows reference the one English crown. The generated Ulster court has a provisional English superior. Neither that link nor the inherited collection boundaries establish direct royal ownership of every estate. Scottish and island incumbents, Irish local rights, Norwegian relationships and the source-sized borders remain unresolved. No extra Irish crown, northern island expansion or source-date person is carried forward.

Castile and Leon share one Ferdinand III court, anchored at Burgos as a map convention. Aragon has one James I court, with Zaragoza as its anchor. The Portuguese court is declared here once even though its Coimbra capital lies in the southern block. Caceres receives provisional Castilian-Leonese ownership; Cordoba and Valencia change court after the earlier city conquests. Their source Sunni residents remain Sunni. These city allocations do not settle the surrounding county frontiers or create a conversion. The Mallorca royal jurisdiction has a generated representative and provisional Aragonese superior rather than the source Muslim sovereign. Remaining Andalusian rows are divided between a generated Nasrid court at Granada, a lower Guadalquivir grouping and a Murcia grouping. Their precise borders, Algarve and Badajoz interfaces, actual incumbents and local sovereignty remain first-pass holds. No later Murcia submission, Seville conquest or population expulsion is scheduled.

Northern Germany has generated Saxon and Brunswick courts with provisional imperial links. Mecklenburg and Lubeck reference the imperial/local cover instead of continuing the source Danish expansion. Effective ducal, episcopal and civic rights remain unresolved. Italian source imperial counties reference that same outside court; no second emperor or automatic submission by the communes is supplied. Venice uses the explicitly limited territorial fallback with a generated principal. Its commune label grants no civic council, dogal mandate, inherited quarter right or ordinary republic constitution. Those details require independently dated constituency and office declarations.

Gregory IX heads the Papal court at Rome. Its ecclesiastical_principality reuses the existing papal_temporal_1204 rules, which have no date eligibility restriction, bound to the ordinary catholic office with no imported institution or incumbent roster. The profile id names its original declaration, not a copied 1204 pope. Spoleto and Ancona use provisional imperial ownership reflecting the earlier acquisitions. Sardinia and Corsica retain a generated local-lordship grouping; a unified historical island kingdom, Enzo's claims and exact maritime influence are not established. The papal death, planned council, naval captures and Faenza surrender are not forced events.

Authoring and integration

The blocks are date-owned literals under FBDATA.bookmarkRegions1241. Loading never clones a mutable earlier world or shares its objects. index.html loads the file after data/bookmark_1204_terrain.js and before communal and government rollouts. All existing bookmarks, active settlement layouts, institutions, stories, starts and campaign state retain their definitions. Later assembly must supply the remaining regions, resolve shared courts and publish one complete world. Generated incumbents and coarse territorial constitutions remain disclosed until replaced; this staging does not clear a historical start's biography or household gates.

The existing static scaffold extractor owns province, realm, title and settlement display names for localization. Proper ruler names remain proper names. No generated catalog is part of these declarations. This is ordinary bookmark content with no new capability, technology requirement, runtime JSON root or saved field. Existing government, travel, production, war and technology rules remain authoritative. The focused browser specification is tests/e2e/specs/1241-scaffold-b.spec.js; static extraction coverage is in tools/test_i18n_catalog.py. Whole-world activation and save/restore coverage require the complete bookmark.
