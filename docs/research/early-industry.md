Paper and bloomery production

Catalogue boundary

The construction catalogue offers Paper Workshop only from year 800 in the physical baghdad county, subject to paper_making, development four and an actual supplied site. This deliberately narrow simulation permission is separate from the wider papermaking adoption schedule and from any claim about a named workshop. Bloomery and Smithy has a broader inclusive 650-1300 construction window, bloomery_iron and development one. Neither declaration places a historical asset or assigns a supplier. Existing inherited property is independent of permission to build another plant.

Simulation accounts

Prices are game gold and upkeep is seasonal. The paper plant costs 65 plus upkeep one; metal works cost 35 plus upkeep one. Each has one county place, no construction award and no passive scalar. Their automatic construction priorities are 30 and 31 within the existing finite budget and reserve horizon.

Dimensionless recipes deliberately lose material rather than multiply it: two prepared fiber units and one water allocation become one paper unit; two ore and one fuel become one iron unit; one iron and one fuel become one tool unit. Water uses the actual conserved source owner, independently of material mass. Processing costs are three, two and four gold, with two, one and two real workdays respectively. These quantities, prices and workdays are authored simulation choices, not measured historical yields or employment counts.

The shared finite market adapter prices supplier fiber or ore at base two, charcoal at two and supplied iron at five. Paper, iron and tools have base output prices twelve, ten and fourteen. Current county market multipliers, counterparties, cash, reserve and receipt-backed per-season demand remain authoritative; no profit is guaranteed. An owned fiber delivery has no cash payment but depletes the same real material. Each profile has finite demand and budget. No anonymous merchant, limitless off-map supplier or sale by a receipt alone is supported.

Ordinary authoring examples

tests/e2e/fixtures/authoring/early-industry-paper.json is a fictional geography using the accepted Baghdad county construction scope, with an owned rag depot, finite water and a separate funded buyer. Its explicit fictional staffed rag-recovery enterprise diverts only actual typed production and loses its corresponding ordinary return. It supplies no historical manufacturing claim or local stock estimate.

tests/e2e/fixtures/authoring/early-industry-metal.json is a different upland geography with separate private/public holders, purchased finite ore and fuel, an imported iron alternative, and real iron/tool buyers. Imported iron makes forging possible without pretending that a local mine exists. The examples use the ordinary Mods file import, and each new_works revision removes the plant from opening inventory so ordinary construction must pay for and enroll the empty asset. Neither example ships as a campaign or initializes any historical world.

Evidence limits

The catalogue makes no exact factory date, named workshop, prisoner-transfer story, local ore deposit, furnace inventory or export-volume claim. Named installations and historical supplies require their own source evidence before an owning world can declare them. Knowing a technology, a county name, terrain, development or available gold cannot establish that evidence. No new research slot, national bonus, troop, equipment item or passive tax channel is introduced.
