Seasonal pasture outposts

Evidence

Anna Katrin Matras, Hakun Andreasen and Steffen Stummann Hansen, A Viking-Age Shieling in Skardsvik, Fugloy, Faroe Islands, Frodskaparrit 51 (2003), pp. 200-211. Page 201 discusses excavated Argisbrekka houses with broad Viking-Age/early-medieval dates. Pages 206-207 discuss summer pasture work, dairy and fodder, the relation to the parent settlement and uncertainty in identifying individual structures. These observations support a seasonal agricultural system; they do not establish a particular opening hut, its owner, animal count, output or exact operating year.

Implementation and limits

Attested: Faroese seasonal pastoral activity and comparative archaeological structures. Reconstruction: a bound parent farm, seasonal local work and grazing eligibility. Simulation choices: the inclusive 850-1300 Faroese construction window, 20 cost/1 upkeep, one conserved allocation into at most one provisions unit, two-gold processing price, one workday and ten/thirty-day input/output lifetimes. These units are dimensionless accounts, not archaeological yields. Technology is none for ordinary pastoral work.

The shieling has no temporary population-capacity effect. A canonical receipt diverts an actual staffed parent's existing production and ordinary proceeds once per farm and period. Lawful finite grazing, local summer, available care and paid action work govern preparation. No new people, herd, historical farmhouse, hut, source or opening stock is initialized. Exact farm/herd counts, named sites, historical installations and early dates remain held without separate evidence. Existing crop-farm provisions support the conservative accounting example, without identifying them as dairy products or inferring a livestock inventory.

The two fictional ordinary JSON examples vary geography, farm allocation, local season, work and lifetime policies. Current quote/commit, expiry and matching restore compose the existing production, seasonal, property and account owners. A useful lot can be sold once to real funded demand or consumed; it grants no simultaneous famine protection, population, research or additional ordinary farm proceeds. Autonomous zero-scalar construction and historical source assignments are not inferred from catalogue membership. Existing assets retain their physical identity when dates or operation eligibility change.

Construction uses a required seasonProfile and its opt-in constructionInputs preview. Review checks actual allocated parent output at an existing store, lawful delivery and grazing, available care and construction plus maintenance and paid-work funds without changing real stock or cash. It never predicts a future harvest. The real purchase enrolls one empty asset; input delivery, seasonal opening and preparation remain separate transactions. Both ordinary examples include an empty-site new_works revision. The fictional parent store retains exact producer provenance and stock expiry, with no second farm output or proceeds. Existing property repairs preserve the original stamp outside construction dates. These are simulation rules, not additional historical claims.
