Commercial, administrative and castle catalogue corrections

Scope

data/technology_civic_military.js replaces twelve complete technology records after the base catalogue. Only annual_fairs and siege_engineering change prerequisites. Their explicit costs, attestation envelopes, inherited ten-tradition adoption pairs, effects, unlocks and capability grants remain unchanged. The other ten replacements narrow descriptions. The dates remain simulation defaults, not newly established regional adoption evidence. No physical works, office, equipment, local protection agreement or historical inventory is created by this correction.

Fairs

Attested context: Jeremy Edwards and Sheilagh Ogilvie, What lessons for economic development can we draw from the Champagne fairs?, Explorations in Economic History 49 (2012), pp. 131-148, section 2, especially pp. 132-134. Their analysis identifies public protection and enforcement by the counts rather than a necessary merchant-guild foundation. Source: https://sheilaghogilvie.com/wp-content/uploads/publications/Edwards-Ogilvie-2012-Champagne-Fairs.pdf

Simulation choice: annual_fairs requires urban_markets instead of merchant_guilds and retains trade 0.015. Knowledge does not enact a fair calendar, protection, immunity or jurisdiction. merchant_guilds, guild_charters, notarial_contracts, customary_law, urban_communes and authenticated_seals retain their definitions. Local charters, offices, exact seasons and operating institutions still need their own evidence and consumers.

Siege competence

Reconstruction: Timothy May, The Chinggis Exchange: the Mongol Empire and Global Impact on Warfare, World History Connected 12.1 (2015), opening discussion of siege engineers and the later artillery-transfer and gunpowder sections, distinguishes specialist expertise from evidence for particular engines. Source: https://journals.gmu.edu/whc/article/download/4473/2440?inline=1

Simulation choice: siege_engineering requires geometry, retains siege 0.04, and no longer completes torsion_artillery through prerequisite closure. Torsion artillery and other specific engine branches remain separate. An explicit omission of generic engineering still prevents its hard descendants from completing. No machine, crew or gunpowder inventory follows from generic knowledge, and existing advanced equipment gates retain their requirements.

Castle refinements

Attested context: English Heritage, Castles Through Time, The Romans and The Middle Ages, distinguishes older turf, timber, stone and projecting-tower defenses from medieval castle arrangements. Source: https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/castles/castles-through-time/

Attested context: UNESCO, Historic Areas of Istanbul, criterion (ii), identifies the second Theodosian defensive line in 447. Layered defenses are therefore not a high-medieval invention. Source: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/356/

Simulation choice: ringworks, castle_towers, stone_castles, concentric_defenses and fortified_gates describe maintained strongpoint and castle arrangements. Their unchanged envelopes do not date the first timber refuge, masonry circuit, flanking tower or fortified entrance. The sole fortification-role building, four fort levels and all prices, upkeep, garrisons, requirements and siege mechanics remain unchanged. Exact local phases, conditions and material profiles remain unresolved rather than inferred from these descriptions.

Administrative and care scope

The descriptions of postal_relays, capitularies, mint_assay, physicians and hospitals distinguish optional organized capacity from an operating network, public right, individual qualification or first care. These are interpretation corrections, not new historical date claims. They preserve every graph, quantity and grant, including the existing assayed_coinage capability. No new institution or health guarantee follows. Exact local adoption, postal installations, mint output and institutional inventories remain outside the accepted evidence.

Paid buildings

data/buildings_civic_military.js replaces only guildhall, archery_butts and foundry presentation. Chartered Guildhall and its Italian Guild Hall variant describe guild facilities, not a communal palace or a new public office. Archery Butts is a specialized military range under its existing crossbows gate, without a universal Sunday law. Foundry and Forge Works describes advanced furnace and forge capacity under blast_furnace, distinct from ordinary smithing or older casting. All costs, upkeep, prerequisites, AI priorities, limits and effects are retained simulation values.

Compatibility and authoring

Fresh games use the ordinary historical policy and prerequisite closure. The focused spec compares 867/1066 starting knowledge against the former two edges and checks released world hashes without replacing their fixtures. Existing completion, progress, exposure, active projects and property are not reseeded or migrated. Ordinary JSON tech/buildings replacements use the same consumers and exact mod fingerprints. civic-fair-catalogue.json and survey-siege-catalogue.json under tests/e2e/fixtures/authoring demonstrate independent alternate prerequisite graphs through the Mods file picker. These fixtures are development examples and do not ship.

Technology impact

These graph and description corrections introduce no independently gateable capability or new grant, so no forward-ledger entry is added. Existing building reviews and political authority requirements remain authoritative. The implemented changes grant no commune, monopoly, public revenue, unit pool, fort material profile or equipment.
