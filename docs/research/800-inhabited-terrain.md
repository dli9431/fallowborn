Winter 800 inhabited terrain

Scope and evidence standard

The opening remains winter 800, day 25. The inhabited_v2 world uses the existing europe_regional_2 physical catalogue. Six bounded districts are inhabited: Jenne-jeno, Gao-Saney, Ibrim, Kath, the Kashan district and Tadmor. The other 23 additions and all 21 original scenery anchors remain scenery. There are 527 physical anchors, 483 inhabited counties and 44 scenery counties. This is a conservative dated subset, not a claim that every retained district lacked people. Unresolved candidates below remain research gates, not accepted historical reconstructions.

Each added county has one authored settlement slot. Existing 800 slots are copied without reordering, renaming or replacing them. The shared 65 km district limits, grid width, map scale and dry-route costs are unchanged. Site coordinates below are approximate longitude/latitude positions. Settlement size, development, community shares, generated people and political boundaries are simulation choices, not archaeological measurements. Existing population and carrying-capacity rules allocate residents once at creation. This change adds no population or authority during restore.

Accepted inhabited districts

jenne_district, Jenne-jeno, -4.55,13.89. Decision: new inhabited county, development 3, one town slot jenne_district_800. McIntosh and McIntosh, Results of recent excavations at Jenne-jeno and Djenne, Mali (2001 conference paper), pp.3-5, distinguish the earlier Jenne-jeno occupation from the much later evidence at modern Djenne and identify Phase III as AD 400-950. Confidence: high in occupation at 800, moderate in this compact settlement representation, low in a named language or constitution. River cultivation, craft work and local exchange use ordinary livelihoods. The existing broad Sahelian name pool and headless local-practice profile are simulation abstractions for unnamed residents; no Muslim dynasty, mosque or Mali state is inferred. A generated local representative holds a one-county territorial realm. Its succession and possession rules are explicitly generic gameplay, not evidence for hereditary kingship or an assembly. No external tribute relationship is asserted. https://anthropology.rice.edu/sites/g/files/bxs3341/files/inline-files/djennemuseumsite.pdf

gao_district, Gao-Saney, 0.04,16.28. Decision: separate inhabited county, development 2, one town slot gao_district_800. Cisse et al., Excavations at Gao Saney, Journal of African Archaeology 11(1), 2013, chronology section pp.18-20 and discussion pp.29-33, date the beginning of excavated occupation to approximately the eighth century. Craft debris and imported glass support exchange; later royal burials do not identify the opening ruler. Confidence: high for occupation, moderate for size, low for a specific court or religious affiliation. Millet cultivation, livestock and craft livelihoods use existing mechanics. A generated local representative and the same broad headless Sahelian profile are editorial abstractions, independently assigned to this dated settlement. The county is not a dependency of Jenne, Ghana or a later Songhay empire. No cemetery king, later Muslim court, trade-income bonus or automatic caravan privilege is created. https://www.researchgate.net/publication/269840466_Excavations_at_Gao_Saney_New_Evidence_for_Settlement_Growth_Trade_and_Interaction_on_the_Niger_Bend_in_the_First_Millennium_CE

qasr_ibrim, Ibrim, 31.99,22.65. Decision: inhabited Nile-edge county, development 2, one town slot qasr_ibrim_800, under the existing Makurian court. Larson, Lost Nubia (2006), the Qasr Ibrim photograph discussion, places the cathedral in the later seventh century over an earlier church. The site and Christian occupation are well established; this is not a reason to populate the surrounding desert. Confidence: high in occupation and Christian presence, moderate in the simplified Makurian county relationship. Nubian/miaphysite residents reuse the existing household rules; no named bishop or new religious office is invented. Farming and local exchange use ordinary mechanics. Generic local holders belong to the existing Makurian government, with no separate royal court or duplicated Dongola population. https://isac.uchicago.edu/publications/lost-nubia-centennial-exhibit-photographs-1905-1907-egyptian-expedition-university

kath_district, Kath, 60.75,41.69. Decision: inhabited lower-Amu district, development 3, one town slot kath_district_800. Bosworth, Chorasmia ii, Encyclopaedia Iranica V/5, pp.516-517, opening paragraphs, separates the 712 conquest from later Islamization and ninth-century Samanid relations. Borjian, Kat, XVI/2, pp.119-121, paragraphs on early evidence and religious communities, cautions that detailed urban descriptions are tenth-century and reports an eighth-century bishopric. Confidence: high in the occupied region and local court, moderate in the site reconstruction, low in an exact 800 incumbent or demographic ratio. Irrigation and local exchange use ordinary production. A generated Zoroastrian ruler represents the still-local court through regnum. No named Afrighid genealogy, automatic Abbasid provincial office, Samanid vassalage or Gurganj dynasty is supplied. Persian is a broad Iranian language/name-pool approximation, not an assertion that Chorasmian had disappeared. Zoroastrian and East Syriac households have illustrative 80/20 weights, not a census. Ruler belief and resident belief are separate fields. No later monumental mosque or church office is projected backwards. https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/chorasmia-ii/ https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/kat-city/

central_iran_oases, Kashan district, 51.45,33.98. Decision: inhabited oasis villages, development 1, one village slot central_iran_oases_800. Amanat, Kashan iii, Early history, paragraphs 1-6, distinguishes the surrounding villages from the later city, notes the early conquest accounts and warns about anachronistic claims. Attribution of a foundation to Harun's wife does not date a complete city. Confidence: moderate for village continuity, low for precise urban extent or faith shares. The bounded agricultural district belongs to the existing Abbasid administrative court. Persian Zoroastrian/Muslim weights of 70/30 keep local residents separate from the court's Sunni identity; these are explicit simulation estimates. No later Shiite school, Jewish institution, silk industry or urban buildings are inferred. Existing public appointments supply consequential delegated authority without inventing a hereditary Kashan dynasty. https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/kashan-iii-history/

palmyra_district, Tadmor, 38.27,34.55. Decision: small inhabited oasis, development 1, one village slot palmyra_district_800. Raja and Seland, Palmyra: At the Crossroads of the Ancient World (2024), Historical Outline and Environment, Subsistence, and Demography, describe continued early Islamic occupation and contraction after the Abbasid shift of power. Confidence: moderate for continuity and a reduced settlement, low for exact 800 size and household shares. Local cultivation, herding and exchange use existing mechanics. Abbasid public authority is separate from the editorial Syriac Christian/Arabic Muslim 60/40 resident mix. No revived Roman civic constitution or ancient caravan monopoly is implied. The village label is a conservative simulation size, not a claim to know the exact year of urban contraction. https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10814-024-09203-8

Communities and authority limits

These declarations reuse existing faith profiles without changing their saved meanings. Archaeology establishes neither marriage rules nor a detailed constitution; the existing broad profiles remain gameplay approximations. The Sahelian faith has no universal head or holy-war tradition. No new clergy, institutional offices, holy sites, conversion projects or religious mechanics are authored. Kath's Christian minority and the mixed Iranian/Syrian populations are distinct from ruler religion. Existing dated Abbasid, Byzantine and papal government profiles remain unchanged.

Ordinary farmer and apprentice openings work in every added county. Ordinary baron openings exercise local landed authority, while the existing public-service path at Kashan and Tadmor uses the Abbasid commission system. Jenne and Gao remain independent local possession abstractions; Kath has ordinary royal succession. No assembly, lineage-acclamation or mobile constitution is inferred from sparse habitation, language or religion. Newly researched constitutional rights require their own evidence and working government contract.

Compatibility, costs and acceptance

baseline and governments_v2 retain europe_regional, all 498 original anchors and the original 800 settlement layout. inhabited_v2 retains the new geography without the later papal composition; inhabited_v2_governments_v2 is the fresh default and includes the existing papal temporal binding. World selection precedes map reconstruction. Carolingian story declarations, opening relationships, supported bundled-story migration, grants and completed opportunity ledgers are unchanged. Restoring an old save neither populates the six districts nor upgrades its government.

Technology impact is none, recorded as period_800_inhabited_terrain. The added capability is baseline habitation through existing livelihood and authority rules. No new logistics, market, navigation or seasonal resource-access capability is introduced. The dry routes continue to use ordinary provisions and movement costs; an uninhabited endpoint is not a free through-route for household travel.

The acceptance workload is fresh/restored saves below 4 MiB, a 45-day household checkpoint below 6 MiB, one 90-day natural household journey per inhabited district, and 45-day observer batches below 30 seconds, with a winter-to-spring/year boundary. Record actual boot/map time, site snapping, map cells, fresh/restored bytes and daily/annual cost before accepting the expansion. These are existing ceilings, not measured results or a budget increase. Raster review must check all six sites, retained desert barriers, Kath-Bukhara and Jenne-Gao routes, and unchanged original coast/strait behavior. No runtime, historical, device or distribution acceptance is claimed.
