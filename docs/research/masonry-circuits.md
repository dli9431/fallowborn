Masonry circuit knowledge and construction

Evidence and scope

ENGLISH_HERITAGE_PORTCHESTER_HISTORY identifies English Heritage, History of Portchester Castle, the Roman fort and Saxon reuse sections: https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/visit/places/portchester-castle/history-and-stories/history/

The accepted source record distinguishes the late Roman circuit from subsequent reuse and castle phases. The founding chronology is uncertain; the coin evidence discussed there places construction after about 268. This supports an older masonry circuit category and later reuse. It does not establish a particular campaign garrison, local condition, continuity of every defensive function or a numerical game fort level. This implementation uses the accepted evidence envelope and makes no additional site claim or placement.

The technology attestation range 200-600 is a rounded modelling envelope for organized late-antique circuit works, not the first stone enclosure. Local continuity is reconstruction. Every adoption pair, prerequisite, construction window and numerical fort value is a simulation choice. Masonry Circuit Defenses costs 50, requires Lime Mortar and Fortified Camps and has no national scalar, free structure, garrison or capability grant.

Physical investment

Masonry Circuit is an optional profile of walls with material masonry, supported canonical levels 1 and 2 and no replacement numeric fields. New construction requires the late_antique campaign era and an inclusive year from 476 to 649, plus current authority, real available strategic occupancy and ordinary funded work. Level 1 must be paid and completed before level 2. At level 2 the current canonical values are cost 220 before market pricing, seasonal upkeep 4, duration three seasons, annual AI work 120, garrison 80, siege delay 2, defense 0.10 and completion prestige 25. The level-1 step retains its own existing values. Neither profile nor knowledge adds these amounts a second time.

masonry_circuit_construction has technology impact hard for optional new works, with ordinary fort progression, timber defenses and inherited repairs remaining available. Default fort requirements and all four default levels retain their existing values. Standing and ruined selected property keeps one strategic position. Later supported repairs use the canonical half invoice without a new knowledge or date gate, grant no construction prestige and retain the original identity. Funded commitments keep their completion date through expiry or conquest. A real ancient circuit still needs separately accepted evidence for any supported level and condition.

Authoring and boundaries
