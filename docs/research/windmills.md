Wind-powered milling and post mills

Evidence

Michael Harverson, MILL i. Mills in Iran, Encyclopaedia Iranica II/7, pp. 751-757 (1987, updated 2016), Windmills section, describes eastern mills with a horizontal revolving wind wheel on a vertical shaft and distinguishes western rotating post mills. It identifies Masudi's 947 account of contemporary Sistan milling separately from his retrospective report of a boast in 644. The earlier anecdote does not establish an operating seventh-century installation. Source: https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/mill/mill-i-mills-in-iran/

Historic England, Cross in Hand Windmill (New Mill), list entry 1183064, History section, places English introduction around the middle to late twelfth century and its first certain documentary mention in 1185. This contextual discussion does not date the surviving nineteenth-century mill to the Middle Ages or establish any county's medieval inventory. Source: https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1183064

Simulation choices

The stable windmill technology is displayed as Wind-Powered Milling. Its attestation envelope is 900-1200, with 900 a rounded tenth-century evidence bound rather than an invention year. The existing adoption pairs for all ten traditions and the default remain inherited approximate simulation values. They describe broad knowledge, not proven regional dates for western post mills. Cost 100, water_power prerequisite, enterprise cost effect -0.015, unlocks and empty capability list remain unchanged.

The stable windmill building remains Post Windmill. New construction requires both windmill knowledge and the inclusive applicability.yearMin 1150 permission. This rounded boundary represents a western construction envelope; it asserts neither a documented English installation in 1150 nor absolute absence everywhere earlier. Cost 45, tax 2, development 1, population capacity 0.05, county limit 2 and AI priority 9 remain simulation values. The ordinary terrain/grain abstraction remains; no wind, climate or site suitability model is claimed.

Compatibility and authoring

The supplements data/technology_wind.js and data/buildings_wind.js replace only their respective existing windmill records after the base tables. Player construction, automation, annual realm AI and private barons use the existing shared applicability projection and canonical paid transactions. Repairs and standing effects remain available after eligibility changes. Saved completions, partial research and building records retain their ids; restore neither reseeds knowledge nor creates assets. No eastern facility or historical opening inventory is added.

Technology impact building_windmill retains its soft windmill interaction, with ordinary grinding and watermills as baseline alternatives. The historical construction date has impact none and introduces no separate discovery gate. Ordinary JSON buildings may declare another construction window under the same applicability schema; exact mod fingerprints retain that distinction. Display fields use the existing tech.windmill and building.windmill localization owners through static correction-map extraction.
