AI decision authoring and acceptance

Purpose

AI preferences are declarations interpreted by registered trusted algorithms. The authorable surface is data/ai_decisions.js, the scalar policy selectors and existing government, balance, trait and agency catalogues. js/ai_decisions.js validates complete records, projects current inputs and supplies bounded first, ordered and seeded weighted choices. The schema and exact numeric bounds are in docs/MODDING.md and FB.aiDecisions.protocol(). This document records the live consumer map, example behavior and acceptance evidence required for that contract.

Consumer and field ownership

War: js/government_ai.js goals consumes chance and bellicoseChance. Explicit strategyParameters.ai.warChance wins over both. js/wars.js consumes minimumStrengthRatio, maximumCommitments, aggressionAppetite and bellicoseAppetite. Legacy ruler.personality bellicose retains its existing effect. War supports first mode and parameters only; it has no ranked candidate facts. Expansion owns the actual target choice. The obsolete aiWarChance scalar had no engine reader and has been removed from the bundled balance declaration; the two policy chances are the active consideration controls.

The built-in ordinary_war policy now declares greater consideration/aggression appetite for ambitious and wrathful rulers and lower values for content, kind and patient rulers. Aims adjust only minimumStrengthRatio; no built-in parameter repeats both a trait motive and its favored aim. Exact multipliers and legacy compatibility are in docs/designs/ai-decisions.md. ai-war-preferences.spec.js distinguishes pure projection from seeded declaration, strength-filtered candidates and actual funded muster; it also covers legal/support blockers, two independent ordinary file imports, malformed nonpublication, matching restore and succession. These are authored focused acceptance cases, not evidence of long-run campaign balance or device acceptance. Neutral means no matching adjustment or an explicitly neutral replacement policy, rather than every generated ruler under the bundled war policy.

Expansion: js/wars.js realmCampaignAI and generateVassalCampaigns enumerate at most 2048 distinct border opportunities in stable id order, and js/world.js aiExpansionFacts projects consolidationBand, core, remaining, contacts and development once per deciding territory. The war owner adds aggression and cached defensiveStrength. A binding lawful-first tier precedes every custom policy; zero weight in that tier declines. The default strategic_expansion orders consolidation, core, remaining, contacts, defense and development, with registered expansion-only aimOrders for crown, wealth and peace aims. ordinary_expansion retains the explicit neutral lawful/id baseline. No synthetic score metric is supported. Fresh cause/strength review, connected-package queue traversal and uncached final declaration/permission review prevent stale rights, ownership, frontier or authority from authorizing a campaign. aiExpansionPriority and aiExpansionTarget remain compatibility queries and aiExpansionProvince retains reconstruction/legacy settlement ownership; none is a competing live declaration selector. ai-expansion-ranking.spec.js covers pure ordering, sovereign/vassal outcomes, two ordinary JSON file imports, detached weight changes and malformed nonpublication, bounded scans, stale package rejection, matching restore and actual succession. aiRaidChance, aiRaidTargetDevelopmentWeight and breakawayChance use realm-scoped balance in aiRaidTick and vassalBreakawayChance; raid target weights retain one draw and zero development preference retains uniform sampling.

Tactics: js/armies.js uses engagementMargin for non-player voluntary pursuit and coordinated advances, and objectiveFirst for ordinary objective versus pursuit precedence. Optional county ranking exposes distance squared in map coordinates, objective membership and current attackPower/defensePower; no synthetic score metric is supported. Facts and parameters live only for the current order phase. Forced routs, supply/fiscal recovery, hostile reach, fort progress, actual combat and manual/player/expedition command ownership remain independent. Policy zero weights suppress only the voluntary candidate list supplied to that selector.

Investment: js/world.js aiBuildingsYear consumes capitalPriority, interiorPriority and developmentWeight; js/fortifications.js fortAIYear consumes capitalPriority, frontierPriority, interiorPriority and fortDevelopmentWeight. js/ai_decisions.js buildingPriorities applies buildOrderWeight, benefitWeight, costWeight, upkeepWeight and buildingRoleWeights before canonical annual/baron eligibility and invoicing. Benefit for that parameter is the sum of declared tax, development, research, piety, levy, retinue and population-capacity contributions; it is an authored preference, not a monetary valuation. Its candidate benefit fact covers tax, development and research only. Ordered/weighted cost reads the real county/profile buildCost even with costWeight zero. Frontier checks the actual adjacent county owners against the realm's sovereign; capitalPriority is the candidate's zero/one capital marker. No per-candidate full-map scan is required.

The default ruler_investment adds declared trait role affinities and current-aim benefit coefficients; ordinary_investment retains the neutral baseline. Optional benefitWeights and investment-only adjustment role/benefit maps use the same detached validator and shared scoring helper for ordinary/profile buildings and forts. Forts rank incremental canonical defenses, with work cost rather than a cash invoice, before the existing county ties or authored ordered/weighted mode. Treasury invoices and finite fortWorks remain separate conserved owners. ai-investment-preferences.spec.js covers contrasting affordable choices, real profile payment, reserves and exclusions, finite fort projects, two JSON imports, malformed nonpublication, matching restoration and actual succession. These focused cases do not establish long-run balance or device acceptance.

Evaluation and remaining engine boundaries

Balance selection has one owner, FB.balanceFor: global, era, government form, bookmark/revision and runtime mod. A runtime balance selector masks a government selector for the same family; authors who want a form override must omit that top-level balance key. The collective/global consumers above deliberately have no individual realm override. No consumer selects preferences by historical realm, bookmark, faith or trait id. Policy adjustments reference effective declared traits and aims; fresh reports use the live existing ruler and generation-aware aim snapshot without materializing people or caching a ruler preference across states.

Every meaningful parameter has a live consumer listed above. War candidate ranking was removed from the supported schema because live warfare uses parameters and expansion owns targets. Constant expansion/tactics score and intrigue schemePriority placeholders were also removed. Unsupported facts fail validation. Complete research records must satisfy horizonFloor <= horizonCap. Detached validation conservatively rejects adjustments whose unclamped values could invert those bounds for any trait subset and at most one aim, including sets that would only become equal through saturation at the upper bound. It never assumes two traits always coexist to repair each other. Mutually exclusive aims are checked independently. Reporting keeps the defensive final bound check.

First and ordered choices add no RNG draw. Shared weighted choose/orderCandidates draw once only for a positive total. Weighted retry lists contain only positive-weight candidates; they put the sampled candidate first and retain the remaining input order. A zero total produces an empty list, including building and government adapters. The live expansion owner never retries aggression after an empty lawful tier. Empty voluntary public-selection choices do not invent a vacant/minority classification and do not bypass an already valid constitutional resolution. Court recognition retains its prescribed incumbent choice without an irrelevant weighted draw; other recognition classes may decline every candidate. willing adds no draw for zero/one, and one chance draw for an intermediate preference. Existing chance sites and the research legacy sampler retain their own original draw behavior.

The engine still owns candidate enumeration, legal rights, constitutional action sets, movement and physical topology, battle calculations, mandatory survival, canonical costs, finite stocks, transaction rechecks, workload bounds, retry scheduling and saved commitments. Definitions supply no code, expressions, unrestricted state paths or recursive programs. A policy is an aid to a supported prospective decision, not a scheduler or a new autonomous action. No new technology eligibility, saved policy record, ledger or save version is introduced.

Tactical disposition acceptance

ordinary_tactics now declares brave/craven margin preferences through the existing adjustment schema. js/armies.js shares a pure report within the order phase for primary pursuit, objective advances and coordinated co-located power comparisons. Recovery checks use its base margin, including canonical logistics searches and treasury regrouping. Reports expose the base and a relative risk reason without news or persistence. Default generated rulers retain their existing pool, which excludes both traits; a complete ordinary rulerTraits replacement explicitly opts them in. Unrelated traits and legacy personality imply no bravery. No combat power, technology eligibility, fiscal approval, route legality, siege force or command authority is supplied by the preference.

ai-tactical-risk.spec.js covers pure composition and bounds, matched army decisions, hostile/unreachable/infeasible routes, separated/coordinated hosts, recovery and daily ownership guards, independent bold/guarded JSON imports, rejected malformed policies, matching-save choices/routes/resources and actual succession. Save/replay cases use existing map edges and stamped revisions; analytic route cases use a bounded local graph over real counties. Program/delegated daily cases isolate the existing order-owner predicates; grant and expedition lifecycle coverage remains with its canonical specifications. Runtime, balance, device and performance acceptance remain pending owner evidence.

Example imports

docs/examples/ai-decisions-conservative.json favors fewer better-supported wars, weak lawful frontiers, worship investment and governance/agricultural research. It declares cautious_court with guarded_tactics (margin 1.6) and deliberate_government. docs/examples/ai-decisions-expansion.json combines more frequent wars and compact frontiers with scholarship investment and learning/commerce research. It declares expansive_court with objective_tactics (margin 0.9, objectives first) and active_government. Both are complete independent ordinary Mods file imports. Neither installs a court in an existing realm or grants a building, technology, army or payment. The new government records take effect only when selected through the existing form owner. Existing realm strategies and authority remain unchanged unless explicitly selected. Top-level war, expansion, investment and research selectors apply immediately to later decisions.

Compatibility and acceptance

Audit corrections cover private-baron zero-total investment choices, expansion contacts within vassal territory and partial aim ordering. The seasonal baron builder skips an empty ranking without spending or a choice draw. Expansion facts use the same deciding territory as campaign enumeration, excluding sibling counties even when their sovereign owner matches. Partial aimOrder lists append omitted aims in retained order, affecting equal positive weights without suppressing higher-weight omitted preferences. Bounded regression cases for these paths are included in ai-decision-integration.spec.js.

Ruler input corrections additionally cover player.charId, compact aim projection from existing identity/family facts, invalid saved aims during pure reads and duplicate behavioral-trait contributions. Valid saved aims remain durable for their generation and current full characters remain authoritative even with no traits. ai-ruler-inputs.spec.js covers those cases, both existing ordinary JSON examples, malformed-definition nonpublication, neutral generated/historical war chances and the legacy personality switch through current/v3 restoration and succession. The switch retains its exact existing effect; no generated trait is relabeled bellicose. Research-domain and investiture-resistance channels retain their single-summary-trait consumers, separate from the shared policy input projection. No broad multi-trait conversion of those channels is implied.

Identity lifecycle owners remain js/world.js makeRuler (ordinary pools and historical profiles), materializeRealmRuler (the same compact person), realmRulerCharacterSnapshot (the current succession member), and advanceRealmSuccession/assignRealmRulerCharacter (the incoming person's full record and ruler summary). js/model.js addTrait/removeTrait change the authoritative character. js/save.js inflates core records and runs ensureDynasticState before ensureAgency; policy compilation and input reads neither repair saves nor initialize people. Retained aims, generation identities, exact mod fingerprints and ordinary resource/commitment records keep their existing persistence owners.

Matching current v4 saves recompose the same declared policy stack and retained world revision. Supported legacy v3 migration also uses the existing save owner; policy evaluation introduces no migration or replay step. Pre-stamp saves retain the existing standard-distribution compatibility exception, while CrazyGames retains its exact profile requirements. Changed policy content or government overrides under the same mod name change the exact effective signature; matching-load eligibility must reject that mismatch. Import validation, stored replacement/removal and unrelated-world isolation remain the ordinary Mods and bookmark contracts. No policy compilation cache belongs in serialized state.
