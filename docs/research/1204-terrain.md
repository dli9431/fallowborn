Authored 2026-10-10. This is the evidence and approximation ledger for data/bookmark_1204_terrain.js. It concerns the existing spring opening, year 1204, season 0, day 76. World contract

The inhabited_v2 revision uses europe_regional_2 with 527 physical districts: 491 inhabited and 36 scenery. Fourteen of the shared 29 additions become inhabited; fifteen retain scenery status with explicit unresolved questions. All 21 original scenery anchors retain an uninhabited remnant. A bounded inhabited district represents households around an oasis, river or valley, not possession of its surrounding desert, mountain or steppe.

The baseline remains the independently authored spring-1204 declaration on europe_regional: 498 districts, 477 inhabited and 21 scenery. Its owners, ordered settlement slots, institutions, rulers and story declarations remain available. No 1066 political, cultural, religious or settlement assignment supplies a new district. Sharing physical geography and existing broad identity definitions does not establish their historical presence; the following entries supply the dated decisions.

Development values, demographic shares, generated households, birth years without identified evidence, ranks and constituency policy numbers are simulation choices. They are not historical headcounts, election records or cadastral surveys. New congregations represent resident religious communities through the existing local-institution contract; they grant no temporal county to a religious institution. No later mosque, castle, conquest or imperial maximum is installed by this revision.

Climate uses the shared geography's existing arid and continental zones. The arid Sahel assignment is a coarse heat/dryness approximation, not a reconstruction of Niger floods or West African monsoon timing. More specific seasonal agriculture needs a separately reviewed zone and season-kind declaration; this revision introduces no unreviewed climate mechanic. Local-season and ordinary route behavior are covered for owner acceptance.
