Late spring 1204: historical frame and authoring decisions

Review candidate: data/bookmark_1204.js and data/bookmark_1204_campaigns.js. This is a full-map high medieval snapshot on europe_regional. It remains outside the selectable bookmark catalogue until content and device acceptance.

Chosen instant

The opening is after Baldwin's coronation on 16 May 1204 and before the summer and autumn partition campaigns. The game expresses this as Spring day 76, 1204 in its abstract 90-day seasonal calendar, rather than claiming an exact Gregorian conversion. It is the year of the Fourth Crusade's capture of Constantinople, not the following year.

The Foundation of the Hellenic World dates the city's fall to 13 April, Baldwin's election to 9 May and his coronation to 16 May. It places the Latin Asian conquests in autumn and winter 1204-1205. Its separate Thessalonica account places Boniface's contested settlement and homage later in 1204. Those later possessions are therefore absent from the opening map.

Constantinople and the Greek courts

Baldwin holds Constantinople and the compressed Thracian opening. The city's principal community stays Greek Orthodox, with smaller Latin Catholic communities; the conqueror's faith is a separate realm field. The city loses relative development without a purported numerical casualty estimate. The full partition agreement is not territorial possession, and Venice's share of the city cannot be represented as a second county owner.

Theodore Laskaris leads a provisional Nicaean court, without projecting his later imperial coronation into May. Michael Komnenos Doukas leads the emerging Epirote court. Alexios Komnenos holds Trebizond and the simplified Pontic coast; its western extension represents the wider Komnenian enterprise rather than a separately simulated David. The spring Greek Thessalonican domain uses Alexios III. Leo Sgouros holds the central Greek grouping and Corinth. The remaining Morea and Crete use explicitly generated local rulers. These compressed opening courts and borders require regional owner review.

Latin vassals and Venice

Boniface's Latin kingdom at Thessalonica, Othon's Athenian duchy, the Frankish principality of Achaea and the later island lordships are not already established spring possessions. Generated territorial holders are gameplay vassals, not identifications of these future historical rulers. The conditional Latin Macedonia campaign may win a county through ordinary war but does not manufacture Boniface or his later fief. A faithful reviewed partition and historical vassal-creation rail is an acceptance gap requiring a shared platform capability beyond the current campaign factory.

Venice is independent and territorial, retaining its lagoon seat and the simplified Zadar conquest. It receives neither Greek Crete nor every projected allotment. Its doge is Enrico Dandolo. Territorial government and dynastic succession remain approximations until a reusable city-republic form supports dogal election and dispersed possessions. No republic behavior is added to the engine for this bookmark.

The Levant, Egypt, Anatolia and the caliphate

Jerusalem remains under the Ayyubids while the Latin kingdom survives at Acre. Isabella is the authored Jerusalem ruler and Aimery the Cypriot ruler; their marriage and joint kingship are compressed into two courts rather than one shared character. Bohemond IV holds Antioch. The Levantine Tripoli county is absent from the shared geography; the Libyan tripoli ID must never be used for it.

Al-Adil leads the Ayyubid grouping in Egypt and Damascus, with al-Zahir's Aleppo and the Yemeni branch represented as subordinate courts. Ayyubid family sovereignty is simplified by one parent relationship. Cairo and Fustat remain one stable gameplay county with its existing physical sites. Sunni and Coptic communities replace the inherited Fatimid-era presentation. The Metropolitan Museum's Ayyubid account supports the end of Fatimid government, Ayyubid control and Jerusalem's 1187 loss, but does not establish the authored population fractions.

France, the Angevin realm and the Empire

Philip II rules France; John retains Rouen, Poitou, Aquitaine and Gascony at this spring instant. Normandy's historical summer loss becomes an ordinary conditional campaign. England's continental possessions are placed under one Angevin sovereign rather than incorrectly creating several unrelated John characters. Guy of Thouars stands for Brittany's guardianship government, and Raymond VI for Toulouse. French vassalage is necessarily less nuanced than contemporary homage and cross-Channel obligations.

The Royal Family's John and Richard accounts establish John's accession and the collapse of the continental inheritance. Sean Cunningham's study in Cardiff Historical Papers gives Rouen's fall as 24 June 1204, supporting its retention at the chosen opening. Anjou's coarse assignment and the exact Poitevin frontier remain regional review points.

Iberia and North Africa

Leon and Castile are distinct crowns under Alfonso IX and Alfonso VIII. Navarre has Sancho VII and Tudela; Aragon has Peter II with Barcelona, Zaragoza and the lower Ebro; Portugal has Sancho I with the Lisbon-Coimbra grouping and the simplified Christian Evora-Beja frontier. Silves, Cordoba, the southern and eastern Andalusi groupings remain Muslim. The Balearic court remains an Ibn Ghaniya Almoravid holdout rather than an Aragonese possession in 1204.

The Almohad grouping covers the Maghrib and its Andalusi possessions under Muhammad al-Nasir. The enduring aghmat county uses Marrakesh as its new seat, reusing the existing geo_marrakesh_2542997 physical site; Aghmat remains a distinct second site and the other fill sites remain available. The Met chronology supports the dynasty's Maghribi context and Marrakesh's importance. Eastern Maghribi control, Ibn Ghaniya resistance and the exact frontier are compressed and need review. The 1212 Iberian campaign window does not guarantee the battle of Las Navas, a coalition or later conquests.

Other mapped regions

Scandinavia, the British Isles, the Rus principalities, fragmented Polish lands, the Baltic, the Caucasus and the eastern Islamic courts are all playable. Rostislav's Kievan opening, the Irish lordships, Balkan authority, Sami/Finnic frontiers and precise Georgian gains are provisional regional identifications. Norway's Guttorm and Novgorod's Sviatoslav use their young historical ages. Generated rulers are explicitly marked generated instead of receiving an invented historical name.

The Khwarazmian grouping replaces the vanished Great Seljuk realm. Its mapped Rayy seat stands for western possessions because Gurganj lies beyond this geography. Ildiguzid Azerbaijan and Hamadan, Salghurid Fars, Nasrid Sistan, the Ghurids and western Karakhanid lands remain separate. The Qara Khitai overlord lies outside the map and is not manufactured as a false local sovereign. Iranica distinguishes the Ghurid defeat in 1204 and subsequent Khorasan loss; the precise spring Khorasan frontier remains approximate. The grouping is not evidence that the entire later Khwarazmian empire already existed in May.

Innocent III and campaign history

Innocent III is the Catholic head at Rome. Papacy, excommunication, great holy wars, tournaments, guilds, council and estates use the existing high medieval systems. Eligible crusade calls use current heads, authority, dates and hostile sacred targets. They are conditional simulation calls; the fresh holy-war history cannot claim to reproduce every preceding crusade or issue an exact Fifth Crusade summons on an authored date. A dated holy-war story type and historical campaign-history seeding are deferred platform gaps, not ordinary bilateral wars renamed Crusades.

Nine registered ordinary campaign instances cover the Latin Macedonia and Bithynia claims, Nicaean restoration, Bulgaria's Thracian challenge, later Epirote expansion, Iberian frontier opportunities and Capetian Normandy. Their windows are opportunities, not outcomes. Living courts, possession of an opening seat, target ownership, existing war eligibility and actual campaign results decide their records. Interpretation and acceptance

Stable county seeds and de jure promotion groupings are reused from the shared medieval geography, not traced as exact 1204 borders. Every realm explicitly uses territorial government. Holders are generated through that form beneath the authored liege hierarchy. County identity remains independent of court religion. Communities are gameplay opening partitions; their positive integer shares total 10000 but are not sourced census estimates. Development, uncertain birth years, skill and trait values are editorial simulation inputs. Historical proper names remain outside translation.

Technology uses the existing high_medieval historical knowledge policy for 1204, with explicit court traditions and ordinary item/unit gates. No technology is awarded by a historical campaign date, and no new research prerequisite is introduced. This is content on existing capabilities, not an additional independently gateable mechanic.
