Spring 1066 North Atlantic revision
Date: 2026-10-16. Definition: 1066 / north_atlantic_v1. Physical geography: europe_north_atlantic_2.

Scope and retained worlds

This optional revision composes inhabited_v2_governments_v2_islamic_v1_institutions_v1, including its final story attachments and institution offers. It retains every source county, court, layout slot, opening actor and institution, then adds ten inhabited coastal or island districts and three highland scenery districts. The ordinary fresh selector and every predecessor retain their definitions. No new start, expedition, dated death, battle or political succession is scheduled.

Actual authority and explicit simulation choices

Iceland is one assembly_polity with six coastal counties and a generated representative. The date-owned profile permits no ordinary public revenue, dues or levy and requires no protected public reserve; its mandate has peace power only. Generic opening treasury initialization remains a simulation input. Reserved actions still require the existing collective consent machinery. The three-year term, voting weights, age and skill requirements are gameplay values. This is a bounded Commonwealth representation, not a reconstruction of godord allegiance, the Law Speaker's identity, quarter-court procedure or a centralized Icelandic executive. The delegate has no royal genealogy or private county grants. The three interior districts remain ordinary scenery subject to existing divergent settlement rules.

The Faroese households use the existing coastal_households assembly profile, with community holders, a generated delegate and bounded collective decisions. Norway's relationship is recorded by norwegian_island_demand_1066. The demand is not an accepted tribute contract, tax payment or engine liege. This intentionally models local communal administration and acknowledges an outside crown while leaving the detailed royal/local constitutional division unresolved. In particular, the map's independent local polity must not be read as a claim that the islands were historically sovereign in 1066. No farm becomes an inherited Norwegian vassal estate at initialization.

Orkney, Shetland and the new Caithness district have one territorial court. Paul is its only incumbent with executable political authority. Erlend is materialized through the canonical authored royalCourt genealogy as a living reciprocal sibling resident at Orkney. Their deceased father Thorfinn supplies the common ancestry for ordinary dynastic eligibility, with a simulation birth year and no authored death date. Erlend receives no independent treasury, command, grant share, assent right or veto. This is a representative single-court simulation; it does not implement joint rule. Both men have simulation birth years and martial/trait settings. Opening spouses are explicitly absent rather than generated as supposed historical partners. No children, expedition, battle credit or scheduled death are inferred from them. Ordinary dynastic succession and later player/AI divergence remain engine behavior rather than a historical forecast.

The court has no engine liege, since one realm-level liege cannot distinguish its island and mainland obligations. The Norwegian island demand and scottish_caithness_demand_1066 preserve distinct outside claims. Their 1000-unit weights are arbitrary bounded demand magnitudes, not ten-percent historical taxes. They debit nothing and create no recurring income, service or sovereignty. Geographic title groupings likewise create no outside grant. Exact dual crown jurisdiction and joint decision powers remain outside the representation; historical acceptance must assess this documented approximation.

Demography, sites and travel

All ten inhabited additions have explicit paired Catholic community defaults. Norse is the shared language/presentation approximation for the islands; it asserts neither ancestry purity nor absence of other identities. Caithness uses simulated 6000/4000 Norse/Gaelic weights. Development, populations derived by existing rules, representative ages and farm slot sizes are simulation inputs, not census results. No bishopric, cathedral, church staff, minority census or named estate is fabricated. Existing source religious institutions and exact source-scoped activity/patronage eligibility carry forward unchanged.

The complete source layout retains each existing slot's identity and order. Each new inhabited county has one village slot, at its physical seed. Birsay is a broad Mainland locality rather than the tidal Brough archaeological footprint. The eastern Iceland farm does not assert that Lon falls inside the eastern cell. All named farm markers are aggregation anchors, with final raster membership and visual quality left to owner-run acceptance.

All seven added water pairs are available alongside the source crossings. Bergen/Shetland, Orkney/Shetland, Caithness/Lewis, Lewis/Faroes, Faroes/Shetland and Faroes/eastern Iceland use open; Caithness/Orkney uses coastal. No Atlantic landRouteUnits corridor or direct Norway/Iceland shortcut is introduced. These are simulated graph connections. Existing household edge clocks do not model realistic ocean sailing times, weather, vessels, seasonal closures or accompanying named-family voyages.

Compatibility and acceptance

The new revision owns complete source-field copies, fresh combined climate tables, its layout, opening revision and extended crossing tables. The source and fresh selection remain separate, and matching old saves continue selecting their exact stamped worlds. New saves use north_atlantic_v1 and europe_north_atlantic_2. Restoration does not create missing relatives, delegates or institutions. This is date content reusing existing authority, collective-consent, genealogy, travel and opening-claim capabilities, with no new technology prerequisite, engine capability or save field.

Focused coverage is in tests/e2e/specs/north-atlantic-1066.spec.js. Acceptance still needs owner-run historical review of the bounded institutions and crown/co-earl representation, raster/site checks, phone-size map review and device measurements. Declaring an opt-in revision does not claim that those reviews have passed.
