# Cologne household and independent followings

The protagonist and established family are fictional. The existing opening uses a German Catholic craft worker aged thirty, fifteen private gold, five prestige and no vow or expedition membership. Ordinary craft work, savings, staying and solo pilgrimage remain independent choices. The imperial-ruler profile is a reference to an existing person, granting no friendship, audience or protection.

Peter and Walter are distinct named principals of independent unformed followings. Albert of Aachen's narrative distinguishes their groups; it does not establish a unified command or reliable numerical roster. The narrative's chronology and geography should not be read as a precise operational schedule. The Cologne placement for both preparations is a disclosed playable abstraction, including Walter's unsettled departure chronology. No additional companion, relative or hereditary commander is supplied.
