Shared physical district ledger

Scope

data/geography_expansion.js defines europe_regional_2 with 29 bounded additions to the 498 retained europe_regional anchors. The resulting physical catalogue has 527 anchors. All 21 original scenery anchors remain. Coordinates are approximate district centres in longitude, latitude order; the 65 km maximum radius limits raster competition and is not a surveyed frontier. Neither a physical seed nor its optional site implies occupation, ownership, a population share, a constitution or a religious institution at any date.

The 476 wider_v2 opening inhabits the existing 25 additions and retains the four further districts as scenery. Its 152 completed ordinary counties and 177 regional declarations are unchanged. The 493 baseline contains 217 inhabited ordinary counties and 260 disabled ordinary counties. The 711, 800, 867, 1066 and 1204 baselines each contain 477 inhabited ordinary counties. Every one of those six baselines retains the original 498-anchor geography. These are authored counts, not runtime measurements or historical acceptance.

The physical review groups below account for every scenery id. Group membership identifies the research area, not necessarily the old raster cell under a site's coordinates. For example, a western Sahel location can lie in the raster assigned to a Saharan seed. Raster membership must be read from the actual map before interpreting a split.

New physical districts and evidence

kath_district: lower Amu Darya near Beruniy, 60.75,41.69; optional shared site kath_district_site. Decision: add a bounded irrigated district, with continental seasons. Borjian, Kat, Encyclopaedia Iranica XVI/2, pp.119-121, discusses river relocation, the limited early record and much fuller tenth-century accounts. Confidence is high for the medieval inhabited region, lower for locating any one early city phase. Agriculture and exchange are supported; exact resident identities, shares, rulers and succession rules need the chosen date's evidence. The seed denotes the district, not an excavated palace. A 493 or 711 town cannot be reconstructed merely by copying tenth-century buildings. https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/kat-city/

gurganj_district: western lower Amu Darya near Kunya-Urgench, 59.08,42.32; optional shared site gurganj_district_site. Decision: add a separate bounded irrigated district with continental seasons. UNESCO's Kunya-Urgench description identifies the old centre and its predominantly eleventh-to-sixteenth-century monuments. Bosworth, Chorasmia ii, pp.516-517, places the rival Gurganj dynasty's takeover in 995 and the Ghaznavid conquest in 1017. Confidence is high for a distinct medieval centre, insufficient here for an early dated layout. This declaration gives no court, dynasty, monuments or exact community shares to 476, 493, 711 or 800. https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1199/ https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/chorasmia-ii/

kumbi_district: archaeological district in the western Sahel, -7.97,15.77; optional shared site kumbi_district_site. Decision: add a bounded steppe district with the existing arid-season approximation. The Mauritanian state-party inventory identifies excavated compounds and the mosque, and gives a broad occupation chronology extending into the thirteenth century. Confidence is high in an occupied archaeological site, lower in matching particular phases to a named capital, ruler or conquest. The coordinate is an approximate district centre. Detailed stratigraphy and a dated textual passage are still needed for each opening's authority and institutions. The seed supplies neither Ghana's borders nor a mosque in an early opening. https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/1546

timbuktu_district: Niger bend margin, -3.01,16.77; optional shared site timbuktu_district_site. Decision: add a bounded steppe district with the existing arid-season approximation. ICOMOS's 1988 evaluation, English p.1 (PDF p.1), records a traditional late fifth-century Hijri camp foundation followed by village growth. Confidence is sufficient for a physical early medieval settlement candidate, not a precise foundation year or an earlier city. Keep 493, 711, 800, 867 and spring 1066 scenery without separate evidence; a 1204 presentation still needs its own local authority and community decisions. No later great mosque or scholarly institution is supplied. UNESCO's mapped position grounds the approximate seed. https://whc.unesco.org/document/152016 https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/119/

The four optional site records contain coordinates only. Dates can use a better evidenced physical site within the district and must supply their own name, kind and slot order. Unknown rulers remain a date-authoring decision, never a generated consequence of adding geography. Archaeology alone establishes neither language nor a collective constitution. No exact population estimate is asserted by this ledger.

Routes, geometry and budgets

New dry routes join Kath to Bukhara (8 units), Kath to Gurganj (3), Gurganj to the lower Syr Darya (8), Kumbi to Jenne (6), and the Timbuktu margin to Jenne (5) and Gao (4). Units are simulation costs using the existing household, courier and army movement contract, not claims for historical itineraries or water transport. Desert remnants remain unbounded. No new sea crossing or island seed is added. The inherited 1100-wide raster, map scale, original seed order and original site coordinates are retained; new seeds are appended.

The declared acceptance workload remains fresh and restored 476 saves under 4 MiB, midpoint saves under 6 MiB, and the existing 45-day observer batches under 30 seconds. The existing one-season regional journeys, observer checkpoints and start coverage remain applicable. County/site counts, fresh/restored bytes, boot/map timing and daily/annual costs still require owner measurement before acceptance; no ceiling is raised by this change. No performance result is claimed here.
