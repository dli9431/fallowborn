# Shared technology coverage before 476

The catalogue is authored for 375–1300. Coverage describes the shared technology
tree; an era's supported technology window and each bookmark's world still need
their own declarations. Existing campaigns retain saved knowledge and projects.

## Earlier emergence

Seven existing foundations receive earlier regional emergence. This means
contact with a practice, not universal mastery. Every widespread date, base
cost, prerequisite, effect, unlock and capability list is retained. No node is
added. None of the existing adoption pairs literally began at 476: the earlier
coverage boundary had hidden broad 410–475 emergence schedules from earlier
campaigns rather than clipping their stored values.

Moving these emergence dates together preserves their ordering, including ties,
and therefore the earliest whole-pair selector for mixed traditions. Altering
only Latin emergence could select a later widespread date instead of another
tradition's earlier completion. The unchanged 476 boundary also requires leaving
every previously unexposed pair alone. These constraints explain the selective
regional schedule rather than asserting identical transmission everywhere.

The two-century shift and retained offsets are coarse simulation choices.
They acknowledge earlier circulation without estimating literacy, adoption
percentages, local equipment, or continuous institutional operation. Tradition
labels denote transmission routes: an early Islamic-route window is not a claim
that an Islamic court existed in the fourth century. The ordinary date, identity,
government and authority owners remain independent.

## Evidence and consumers

- **VITRUVIUS_X_5:** [Vitruvius, On Architecture X.5](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Vitruvius/10%2A.html), describes rotary millstones and geared water milling in the ancient Roman technical tradition. This supports earlier contact with rotary grinding, not universal ownership of hand querns or a local working watermill. quern_stones already contributes tax 0.005 and is the retained prerequisite of water_power; neither effect nor dependency changes.
- **CELSUS_VII_4_5:** [Celsus, On Medicine VII.4–5](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Celsus/7%2A.html), describes probes, scalpels, hooks and forceps. The first-century surgical tradition predates the coverage boundary. A technical prescription does not measure access or outcomes. surgical_instruments retains its bloomery_iron prerequisite and its descriptive practice unlock, with no scalar health award or new surgical action.
- **CODEX_THEODOSIANUS_XI:** [Theodosian Code XI.1.2–3, XI.1.14–16 and XI.3](https://droitromain.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/Constitutiones/CTh11.html) preserves fourth-century fiscal receipts, specified quantities, assessment records, office responsibility and land-transfer obligations. Editorial datings sometimes disagree; these enactments establish earlier administrative practice, not universal compliance. The four fiscal technologies retain their written_law/census_records chains, existing tax effects and the administration career unlock. Knowledge grants no appointment, public account, levy right or exemption.
- **AMMIANUS_XXIII_4:** [Ammianus Marcellinus XXIII.3–4](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Ammian/23%2A.html) places artillery with Julian's 363 expedition and describes torsion mechanisms. His retrospective account supports earlier familiarity, not a precise invention year, engine stock or exact regional diffusion. torsion_artillery retains geometry and siege 0.01. The corrected siege_engineering graph remains independent of torsion_artillery.

[Priscus's embassy narrative](https://faculty.georgetown.edu/jod/texts/priscus.html)
also describes villages, grain provisions, carts, imported craftsmanship,
secretaries and correspondence near the Hunnic court in 448/449. It supports a
mixed fifth-century setting. It does not justify granting every institutional or
manufacturing refinement to that court, identify a universal Hunnic technology
tradition, or establish paired stirrups.

kept distinct from widespread adoption. The retained groups below cover the
other ancient and fifth-century candidates, including those whose emergence
falls between 375 and 476.

For unexceptional single traditions, 451 and 476 completion sets differ only in
the retained Baltic-Finnic composite_bow completion at 455. The exposure
differences are pinned separately for all ten traditions. Latin 451 lacks only
mounted_archery, sounding_lead and stone_bridgebuilding exposure relative to Latin 476;
Byzantine exposure is identical. These are shared schedules, not a claim about
one historical court. Specific earlier polities can use ordinary reviewed
complete/expose/omit declarations without changing the shared tree.

Historical timing alone adds no independently gateable gameplay capability and
needs no new technology-impact ledger entry. Current-content saves retain their
actual completion, exposure, queue, progress, priorities and reserve. The focused
coverage specification checks every year 375–475, every existing date and
revision, mixed routes, closure/omissions, independent ordinary JSON schedules
and repeated matching restores. It does not establish visual or play acceptance.
