Spring 867 inhabited districts

Scope and evidence standard

data/bookmark_867_expansion.js authors the opt-in inhabited_v2 revision of spring 867. government_rollout.js also composes inhabited_v2_governments_v2 while governments_v2 remains the fresh default pending explicit acceptance. baseline and governments_v2 retain original geography, settlements and actors. The larger world has 527 anchors, 485 inhabited counties, eight inhabited additions and 21 pending additions, with all 21 original scenery anchors retained. Authored coverage does not establish historical or runtime acceptance.

Occupation, jurisdiction, community identity and government procedure are separate confidence questions. Excavation establishes activity without establishing a royal frontier. Later narrative descriptions are labelled as later evidence. No historical incumbent or genealogy is invented: the three new courts and the Jenne-jeno representative use generated people. Development, cohort weights, fiscal rates, eligibility, mandate length and the 65 km seed caps are simulation choices, not census figures, archaeological boundaries or attested laws. Sources are identified below so each choice can be revised independently before release.

The existing principal identities, development, starts, stories and ordered settlement slots of all 477 ordinary counties are unchanged. No existing county is reclassified as scenery or granted to a new court. One physical site at each accepted addition represents its district; it does not locate a palace, excavated church or exact settlement footprint. No further physical seeds or political content for another date are introduced.

Accepted habitation

jenne_district: Jenne-jeno and the surrounding Middle Niger settlement landscape. Susan and Roderick McIntosh's occupation sequence covers the opening, with substantial growth during approximately 750-1100. This supports an occupied district, cultivation and exchange; it does not subordinate Gao to Jenne. Habitation confidence is high. Political boundaries and the named interests are reconstructions. Source: The Inland Niger Delta before the Empire of Mali: Evidence from Jenne-jeno, Journal of African History 22 (1981), abstract and occupation chronology. https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-african-history/article/abs/inland-niger-delta-before-the-empire-of-mali-evidence-from-jennejeno/111CBA0644CA0D9EE7B3210C7A8F50F6

Jenne-jeno has a generated territorial local ruler as a provisional gameplay model. Archaeological settlement clustering and heterarchical interpretations support occupation and differentiated communities, but do not establish this opening's assembly membership, selection procedure or reserved powers. The proposed assembly constituency is withdrawn pending that evidence. Neither an unknown incumbent nor this conservative model proves kingship or hereditary local rule. S. K. McIntosh, Modeling political organization in large-scale settlement clusters (1999), considers political models rather than supplying a dated election law. https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511558238.006

gao_district: Gao-Saney, represented separately from Jenne-jeno. Excavated occupation approximately 700-1050, food remains and craft material support a settled district at the opening. The published discussion connects early ninth-century accounts with a distinct regional polity. Habitation confidence is high; precise court location and succession procedure are lower-confidence reconstructions. A small direct-holder regnum supplies an ordinary dynastic path without imposing later imperial reach. No later royal cemetery, mosque, named ruler or Mali institution is imported. Source: Cisse and colleagues, Excavations at Gao Saney: New Evidence for Settlement Growth, Trade and Interaction on the Niger Bend in the First Millennium CE, Journal of African Archaeology 11 (2013), 9-37, chronology and discussion; DOI 10.3213/2191-5784-10233. Author-uploaded article: https://www.researchgate.net/publication/269840466_Excavations_at_Gao_Saney_New_Evidence_for_Settlement_Growth_Trade_and_Interaction_on_the_Niger_Bend_in_the_First_Millennium_CE

kumbi_district: an occupied Kumbi-area district, under the deliberately compressed Ghana / Wagadu court. The Mauritanian state-party inventory reports a long occupation sequence beginning before 867. Identifying this excavated site with a particular ninth-century textual capital remains uncertain. Confidence in this date-local political placement is low; the opening does not claim a verified capital, surveyed kingdom or contemporary mosque. The generated regnum holds only this district and does not acquire Jenne or Gao. Source: Mauritania's 2001 tentative-list entry, Site archeologique de Kumbi Saleh, occupation paragraph. This is a research lead supplied by a state party, not a verified UNESCO inscription or court chronology. https://whc.unesco.org/en/tentativelists/1546

qasr_ibrim: Ibrim, a Nubian desert-edge settlement, with the existing Makurian court as its superior jurisdiction. Christian-period occupation and fortification are well supported; precise spring-867 local officeholders are not supplied. The existing Nubian/eastern profile is a broad inherited Christian presentation, not a definition of a new Nubian church or proof of Byzantine religious jurisdiction. Habitation confidence is high, detailed local authority moderate. The town remains distinct from Dongola and Soba, and its narrow district does not assign the whole desert to Makuria or Egypt. Source: UCL Digital Egypt, Qasr Ibrim, Christian-period overview. https://www.ucl.ac.uk/museums-static/digitalegypt/qasr/index.html

kath_district: Kath under a generated Afrighid court. Bosworth describes ninth-century dynastic Islamization and nominal Samanid subordination, without establishing the exact 867 dependency relationship. The opening therefore gives the local court no invented liege or tribute contract; this means local playable jurisdiction, not a denial of wider claims. Sunni court religion does not convert all residents. The Chorasmian Zoroastrian, Sunni and eastern Christian cohorts are broad regional reconstructions with arbitrary 6000/3000/1000 weights. Sources: C. E. Bosworth, Chorasmia ii, ninth-century Afrighids and Samanid relationship. https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/chorasmia-ii/ Habib Borjian, Kat, early history and religious communities. https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/kat-city/

Kath's inhabited oasis and royal association are moderately supported; the detailed spring-867 settlement scale and cohort composition have lower confidence. The later prosperous metropolis, Ma'munid rule and subsequent Turkish conquest are not opening declarations. Chorasmian presentation inherits the existing Persian mechanical tradition; its shared inherited personal-name pools are presentation proxies, not attested ninth-century incumbents or linguistic ancestry. regnum supplies a compressed dynastic court with direct public county holders. Its generic offices are engine affordances, not attested Afrighid offices.

palmyra_district: Tadmor, an inhabited Syrian oasis under the existing Abbasid administrative monarchy. Early Islamic and Abbasid activity supports continuing habitation rather than a recreated classical city. Habitation confidence is moderate to high; exact local public jurisdiction and the Arabic/Syriac Sunni/eastern 8000/2000 community split are reconstructions. Existing regional administrative rules stand in for an unnamed subordinate district; no governor, bishop or future dynasty is fabricated. Livelihoods use ordinary cultivation and exchange. Source: Getty Research Institute, Ancient Palmyra, early Islamic and Abbasid summary. https://www.getty.edu/research/exhibitions_events/exhibitions/palmyra/ancient_palmyra.html Excavation reference: Polish Centre of Mediterranean Archaeology, Palmyra 2005, early Islamic reuse of the church area. https://pcma.uw.edu.pl/wp-content/uploads/pam/PAM_2005_XVII/299.pdf

tayma_oasis: Tayma, a small inhabited oasis with the existing Abbasid court as superior jurisdiction. The Saudi-German archaeological overview reports renewed activity from the eighth century after late-antique decline. This supports modest habitation, not a rebuilt ancient kingdom or a dated 867 governor. Confidence in occupation is moderate; administrative placement and the wholly Arabic/Sunni opening cohort are approximations. Development 1 and a village head avoid importing its earlier monumental scale. Source: DAI/Saudi Commission for Tourism and Antiquities, Tayma archaeological project brochure (2007), early Islamic chronology near the end of the site-history section. https://www.dainst.blog/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2020/09/brochure-2007-10-ebook-min.pdf

central_iran_oases: Kashan oases, located at the shared seed near 51.58 E, 33.98 N. The district represents agricultural townships around Kashan, not Ardestan or the later walled city. Ya'qubi and Ibn Rosta describe surrounding communities; Amanat also records eighth-century heterodox and proto-Shi'i associations. Habitation confidence is moderate. The Abbasid superior claim is a compressed administrative assignment. Persian/Shia, Zoroastrian and Sunni weights of 6000/3000/1000 are illustrative, not a census; the existing Shia profile is a broad presentation for nonconforming Islamic communities, not a claim that later Twelver institutions existed in their current form. Source: Mehrdad Amanat, Kashan iii, early history and religious trends. https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/kashan-iii-history/

Faith, rights and livelihoods

Middle Niger and western Sahel presentation use new dated labels inheriting the existing neutral Sahelian culture. Local traditional practice reuses sahel_traditional_476 without a universal divine pantheon, church hierarchy or imported later jihad. These are broad placeholders for evidenced non-uniform local practice, not claims that everyone shared a single tradition. New religion mechanisms and more precise local affiliation remain separate research work.

All accepted districts have paired culture/faith cohorts and normal population, production, commoner households, home settlements, tenure selection and travel. Peace and revenue customary rights use possession as their basis. These establish simulation public jurisdiction without recording private land deeds or wholesale sovereignty over sand. The household's existing land, buildings and inheritance remain separate. Oasis agriculture and river exchange are represented through ordinary livelihoods; no new crop, pastoral migration, hunting or tribute system is asserted.

Every accepted addition has a bounded seed and an ordinary weighted dry route. No water crossing exemption is introduced. Costs and climate assignments are authoring approximations; arid Middle Niger uses the current climate vocabulary and does not simulate a new flood calendar. Kath uses continental seasonality. Original counties retain their existing climate resolution. Technology impact is none: established habitation and local government do not wait for player research. Existing equipment, production and travel requirements continue to apply.

Pending scenery means a missing date-local declaration, not an assertion of no human presence. The inventory above accounts for all 29 additions exactly once. Unaccepted additions have no owner, inhabitants, settlement layout or generated local actors. These gates can be resolved by further historical/government work without changing the retained worlds.

Supported saves, mods and acceptance

The existing owner-selected 867 inventory is docs/plans/v2-476-phase-0/save-manifest.json. It covers different stations, campaign ages, delegated settlements and dense/stress situations. Original export paths, byte counts and SHA-256 digests remain in that manifest. Exports stay outside version control. The corresponding parity JSON files are checkpoint reports, not loadable saves. The new date spec reads original exports through readCorpusExport and the ordinary exported-save loader, verifies their hashes, switches to the expanded world and restores the retained re-export. FB_PARITY_CORPUS can point at the checkout containing those original saves.

baseline and governments_v2 retain europe_regional, all original county ids and ordered physical slots. Legacy unstamped saves migrate to baseline. Existing wastelandSettlements remain converted scenery in their own world, with the same sites, homes, population and public/private rights; Kath is not inserted during load. Retained serf journeys compare an actual active tenure and established people rather than comparing an absent farmer tenure. The separate household snapshots also compare serialized player, characters, rulers, ownership, development, buildings and population. Restore does not replace local actors, consume RNG or allocate identities.

The normal JSON mod proof replaces the complete public bookmark definition and changes Kath only inside its selected revision. A stored mod fingerprint pins that effective catalogue. Unknown revisions, mismatched geography and changed mod definitions fail closed. Baseline-table mods still apply to the retained baseline; an expanded mod must author the relevant whole-value revision field explicitly. No custom date loader or private mod payload is introduced.

The date fixture records the candidate fresh revision independently of the released catalogue fixture. Existing retained catalogue hashes and parity reports are not regenerated. New district household and observer journeys author one bounded natural season, save/replay boundaries, local government succession or delegation, geography switching and ordinary/CrazyGames flag coverage. Opening save budget is 4 MiB and the seasonal checkpoint budget is 6 MiB of uncompressed JSON. These ceilings are review targets, not measured results. Owner execution must supply timings for activation/raster compilation, ordinary seasons including annual work, save/restore and dense settlement review, plus longer household/court exports. Compare retained and expanded worlds under the same device/browser conditions; the authored counts alone do not prove performance.

Historical acceptance, visual raster and route inspection, touch/phone behavior, actual CrazyGames package review and standard itch/play review remain owner acceptance work. No release, deployment, catalogue recapture or supported government retrofit is implied by this candidate revision.
