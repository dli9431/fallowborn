493 bookmark: Ravenna after the Italian war

Date: 2026-10-05. Status: initial historical research and source inspection; no playable bookmark registered. County ownership, the full court roster and local incumbent coverage still require content review.

CHOSEN FRAME

Use spring 493 after Theoderic has taken Ravenna and killed Odoacer. Spring day 1 on the game's 360-day calendar would be an editorial snapshot of that completed settlement, not an assertion that its season boundary reproduces a Julian calendar date. Do not open with the earlier agreement to share Ravenna: that is a different political situation.

Procopius, Wars V.1.24-29, describes episcopal mediation, the joint settlement, Odoacer's killing and Theoderic's rule over Goths and Italians. The account supplies a narrative sequence rather than an exact daily chronology. Source: https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Procopius/Wars/5A%2A.html

ITALY AND ITS COURT

Theoderic belongs at Ravenna as an established territorial ruler, with a Gothic Homoian elite and surviving Roman civil institutions. He must not be the landless Balkan ruler or migrant host of the earlier start. Odoacer's independent court and the western court of Romulus Augustulus must not remain active sovereigns. A retained Roman civic government with a distinct governing identity is a proposed gameplay representation, subject to review; it does not imply that Theoderic became western emperor or that the Goths converted to Nicene Christianity.

Anonymus Valesianus, latter part 55-64, describes the removal of Odoacer, Gothic acclamation, coexistence with Roman institutions, and later agreement with Anastasius. Its compressed chronology associates Zeno's death with the settlement despite the intervening years; do not use that passage to make Zeno alive in 493. Source: https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Excerpta_Valesiana/2%2A.html

Liberius is a researched candidate for the praetorian prefecture. Cassiodorus, Variae II.16, praises his service under Odoacer, subsequent appointment by Theoderic, financial administration and the Gothic settlement. This retrospective royal letter is evidence for a civil office and negotiated settlement, not a census or a warrant to allocate exactly one third of every county to Gothic residents. Administrative rights, fiscal power, personal household property and military command need separate records. Source: https://www.gutenberg.org/files/18590/18590-h/18590-h.htm

Retain Rome's senate, Ravenna's council and distinct Nicene and Homoian religious communities. Settlement community proportions, development, skills, treasury amounts and army strength will be editorial gameplay values. They must not be presented as historical measurements. Confirm Sicily, Dalmatia, Raetia and Noricum county by county rather than copying the earlier political ownership wholesale. Procopius's later narrative of the 535 campaign establishes Gothic Sicily and Dalmatia then; it does not by itself prove every 493 frontier.

THE EASTERN COURT

Anastasius I, not Basiliscus or Zeno, holds Constantinople. Hugh Elton's scholarly account places his accession in April 491, marriage to Ariadne that May, continuing Isaurian resistance and recognition of Theoderic in 497. Recognition therefore must not be assumed as an opening 493 imperial commission. The emperor's Miaphysite sympathies and the Chalcedonian patriarchate are distinct from the beliefs of every eastern resident. Source: https://roman-emperors.sites.luc.edu/anastasi.htm

Euphemius is the Constantinopolitan patriarch for this snapshot, replacing the earlier Acacius. The Catholic Encyclopedia dates his tenure to 490-496 and describes his Chalcedonian profession and failed reconciliation with Rome. This is a confessional secondary account; use its incumbent chronology without adopting its judgments about rival churches as neutral narration. Source: https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05606a.htm

ROME AND RELIGIOUS AUTHORITY

Gelasius I replaces Simplicius at Rome. His letter to Anastasius in 494 distinguishes priestly authority and royal power and provides a near-contemporary basis for contested jurisdiction. It is later evidence for the relationship, not a dispatch already written at the opening date. It must not activate medieval papal elections, crusades or excommunication systems merely because the Roman bishop exists. Source: https://sourcebooks.web.fordham.edu/source/gelasius1.asp

The current institution catalogue also has historically named sees and vacancies outside these capitals. Each needs its own date review. Generating a representative of the correct local community is preferable to asserting an unverified incumbent, but changing a global historicalName would also change the earlier bookmark. Preserve independent institutional identities and saved representatives across campaign switching and restore.

GAUL AND IBERIA

Clovis is the Frankish ruler; Syagrius's independent Soissons domain is no longer an opening polity. Alaric II is the Visigothic ruler at Toulouse. Gundobad and Godegisel require a Burgundian arrangement that does not invent precisely known borders or erase their rivalry. Gregory of Tours, Histories II.27-37, supplies the fall of Syagrius, Burgundian conflict and the later defeat of Alaric. His retrospective Christian narrative is not a reliable county survey. A pagan Frankish opening is an editorial choice pending review of Clovis's disputed baptism chronology. Source: https://sourcebooks.web.fordham.edu/basis/gregory-hist.asp

AFRICA AND OTHER REGIONS

Do not carry Gaiseric's earlier court, Carthage's earlier vacancy or named Moorish rulers into 493 without fresh evidence. The African succession, Sicilian settlement, Carthaginian incumbent and peripheral frontier ownership remain research tasks. Generated peripheral rulers must be labelled as editorial identities in the research record, with period-appropriate ages rather than unchanged birth dates from the earlier snapshot. The geography remains the existing regional map; this proposal supplies no new playable Persia, Syria or Egypt.
