Agricultural foundations and machinery

Scope

The effective catalogue loads data/technology_foundations.js immediately after data/technology.js. It replaces nine existing technology definitions and preserves their ids, explicit costs, prerequisites, unlocks, capability lists and numerical effects. No building, workshop, equipment, institution, population or regional starting-knowledge exception is created by this file.

Evidence corrections

Seed Selection and Stored Hay now use history.attested [1,900], replacing [500,900] and [550,900]. Columella's first-century On Agriculture II.9.11-12 describes selecting and retaining seed; II.17-18 describes meadow management, cutting, curing and storing hay. The first-century lower bound is a rounded evidence convention, not an invention date. Prescriptive advice establishes knowledge of a practice, not its use by every household or region. Their base research costs remain 50 rather than dropping to the base helper's ancient-foundation tier.

Water-Powered Stone Sawing now uses history.attested [200,1100], replacing [600,1100], and retains base cost 50. The Hierapolis sarcophagus relief supports a third-century witness for mechanized stone sawing. A rounded third-century bound does not identify the wheel at every mill, establish continuous operation or justify a physical installation in a particular campaign county.

Machinery and agricultural meaning

Water Power covers direct rotary drive as well as geared arrangements. Expanded Powered Milling describes a later extension of applications and workshop organization; it does not date the beginning of mechanical grinding. Neither description changes research requirements or cost discounts. Existing water-lifting, undershot-wheel, irrigation and manuring records remain unchanged.

Organized Legume Rotation names the optional coordinated program whose existing prerequisite is three_field. It does not claim that ancient farmers needed that program to grow beans or improve soil. Heavy Plough, Improved Husbandry and Selective Stockbreeding describe systematic refinements rather than the beginning of tillage, herding, pasture use or breeding. Their numeric dates, requirements and effects remain unchanged. No universal northern farming package or inherited ethnic advantage is asserted.

Adoption and saved knowledge

Every default and ten-tradition adoption pair is retained from the base catalogue. These are approximate simulation defaults, not newly verified local diffusion dates. A local early completion or exposure needs evidence owned by that bookmark or revision. The three earlier evidence bounds alter the existing soft research-cost review before regional emergence, while explicit base prices remain fixed. They do not move emergence, widespread adoption or prerequisite closure, and therefore do not change the released dates' starting knowledge by themselves.

Completed ids, exposed knowledge, ongoing progress and existing property keep their meanings. Restore never completes a technology or installs a building merely because its current evidence range is earlier. Research can still begin before attestation at the ordinary premium when its real prerequisites are met. No independent capability or technology-impact entry is added by this correction.

Remaining uncertainty

Exact regional diffusion, continuous local operation, the form of individual waterwheels and all opening inventories remain outside these corrections. Existing overshot-wheel dating is retained rather than inferred from evidence of an unspecified mill. Earlier testimony alone supplies no later county, court or physical asset.
