Boathouses and maintained work

Source and limits

Museum Nord, Boathouse at Lofotr Viking museum, opening three paragraphs, https://www.museumnord.no/en/stories/boathouse/ (consulted 2026-10-18). Attested: late Iron Age shoreline shelters housed fishing boats as well as larger vessels. The discussion of seasonal housing is tentative. The evidence supports a distinct modest shelter/workspace presentation; it does not establish a particular dated facility, owner, inventory, boat count or invoice practice.

Simulation choices

data/buildings_boathouses.js adds boathouse as a shipyard-role building with cost 25, upkeep 1, devMin 0, maxCounty 1, coastal true and AI order 32. New construction has inclusive years 650-1300 and Nordic sovereign learning-tradition scope. These rounded dates, scope, prices and priority are simulation permissions, not first-invention claims or evidence that other peoples lacked shelters. There is no technology requirement and no passive tax, development, population, research, manpower or naval scalar. Existing authority, county occupancy, current construction budgets, reserves and upkeep apply.

The useful consumer is the existing maintained maritime-work adapter. data/production.js declares finite boathouse_timber and boathouse_cordage inputs and boathouse_work output. prepare_boathouse_work consumes two timber units and one cordage unit, six processing gold and one actual action day for one prepared unit. The dimensionless units and mass are conservative accounting choices, not historical yields or material prices. data/maritime_work.js declares boathouse_work: the retained fishing_boat_business, that recipe/output, quantity 1, unitValue 6, invoiceShare 0.1 and termDays 7. Its credit is still limited by proportional actual paid processing value and the whole-gold floor of ten percent of the current ordinary invoice.

The building declares productionRecipeIds:[prepare_boathouse_work] and maritimeWorkProfileId:boathouse_work. New construction requires an actual empty productionSites assignment, the matching maritimeWorkAssignments profile, a lawful co-located launching source, finite owned delivery or paid material acquisition, available work and private funds for construction, upkeep reserves, processing and the remaining eligible ordinary invoice. Scenery, an unrelated sale buyer and knowledge alone cannot qualify. Canonical construction enrolls that actual empty asset once; delivery, paid work and the invoice remain subsequent explicit transactions. Inherited facilities can retain their declared finite opening lots and need not satisfy current construction permission. There is no historical placement, free source, resupply or opening stock from construction. The facility omits settlement art.

Prepare reserves only the completed original household's paid output for one exact pending construction or next-stage invoice. Complete consumes that output and one lawful launching place, then charges the remaining unchanged ordinary price through the existing enterprise transaction. Cancellation releases the reserved output without refunding its processing. Lost authority, expiry, changed prices, changed levels, unavailable launching or insufficient remaining funds fail purely and require current review. No private work is supplied by an independent public treasury. Acquisition and upgrades retain ordinary staffing, coastal/enterprise eligibility and current discovery gates. A bought enterprise can remain idle; no fleet, command, soldiers or proceeds are supplied by the facility.

Technology decision building_boathouse is none: basic shelter and ordinary maintained boat work need no discovery. The maintained_maritime_work and conserved_production_batches decisions remain none. Fishing construction retains coastal_piloting, the decked stage harbor_works and flotilla organization naval_levies, with their existing separate reviews, prices, effects, paid staffing and ordinary full-price alternatives. No technology definition or unlock field changes.

Ordinary imports and continuity

tests/e2e/fixtures/authoring/boathouse-work-shore.json uses the bundled building, recipe and work profile with an explicit fictional coastal site. boathouse-work-river.json retains that building but independently configures one timber plus two cordage units, two workdays, processing cost 4, a river enterprise, unitValue 3, invoiceShare 0.05, term 14 and two launching places per seven-day period. Its declared river landing is in a coastal county, retaining the base building's coastal constraint. Each import includes supplied inherited property and a separate new_works revision with only a finite material depot and an empty declared boat-work site. The river import explicitly selects river_boathouse_work on its complete building definition, demonstrating that construction follows the declared consumer. The examples are ordinary Mods imports, not executable registrations or historical world seeds.

The existing strict version-1 production and maritime receipts retain original asset, worker, batch inputs, payer, invoice price/level, actual contribution and consumed launching claim. New construction retains the existing constructed:true production marker, its actual openedTurn and one asset revision, with no replayed opening goods. No new saved owner or schema is added. Matching pending and completed restores preserve stock and payments without replay. Missing, forged, duplicated or cross-owner contribution provenance is rejected before adoption. Bundled bookmarks, released geography, original enterprise ids/stages, existing assets and all technology definitions remain unchanged.

The new script follows data/buildings_maritime.js in index.html, after the base catalogues and before bookmarks. Existing data-directory distribution allowlists and index-derived offline discovery include it. No assets, packages, loader flags or distribution-specific behavior are added. Tests and authoring examples remain development-only. New display fields use the existing building, productionGood, productionRecipe and maritimeWorkProfile literal extraction owners with imported English fallback.
