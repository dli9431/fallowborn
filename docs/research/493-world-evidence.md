493 expanded world: evidence and editorial limits

Scope and status

The hidden world_v2 candidate in data/bookmark_493_world.js supplies a dated reconstruction for the spring 493 editorial instant. docs/research/493-world-districts.csv identifies all 260 original ordinary omissions and twelve bounded additions separately, with their names, sites, proposed authority, working government, resident identity, source locator and confidence. The register describes implemented candidate data, not accepted historical frontiers. Every unknown local incumbent and genealogy is generated. Numerical community shares, development, skills, recognition thresholds and borders are simulation choices.

The original 217 inhabited districts remain the regional seam. Theoderic, Anastasius, Gunthamund, Clovis, Alaric and the two Burgundian courts retain their existing actors, territories and campaign commitments. Upper Rhine additions meet the retained Cologne and Strasbourg courts; Gepid additions meet Sirmium without transferring it early. The eastern additions join the retained Anatolian provinces. Ras is reconstructed with eastern Dardania; Bosna follows the retained Italian Dalmatian sphere. These frontier allocations need historical review alongside the existing 493 Dalmatian, Raetian and Norican approximations.

Regional government and identity choices remain candidate abstractions, especially the forest, northern, island, Moroccan and Arabian local constitutions. Existing faith profiles are reused without changing any released catalogue or assigning faith by modern national identity. The legacy suffix on a registered identity is its stable catalogue id, not a source of 493 politics. Detailed religious institutions and revised faith behavior require their own evidence and catalogue work.
