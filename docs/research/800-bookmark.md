Late 800 bookmark: sources and editorial decisions

This is a review candidate, not an accepted public start. data/bookmark_800.js owns the full political map independently of the other bookmarks. data/bookmark_800_history.js declares opportunities for the existing campaign factory. No engine changes accompany this content.

Chosen instant

The snapshot follows Charlemagne's coronation at Rome on 25 December 800. Its game date is Winter day 25, 800. The calendar has four equal ninety-day seasons, so this is an editorial seasonal approximation, not a Gregorian date conversion. Barcelona remains outside Frankish control at the opening. Irene rules the eastern empire, Leo III holds the Roman office and Harun al-Rashid heads the Abbasid court.

Einhard's account describes the coronation, imperial rivalry, Frankish campaigns and foreign embassies. It praises Charlemagne and summarizes a whole reign, so its territorial assertions must not all be read as an exact 800 frontier. The Metropolitan Museum's Carolingian survey supplies the broader imperial and cultural context. The Vatican list dates Leo III's pontificate to 795-816. Irene's sole rule is dated 797-802 in the historical biography hosted by Perseus.

Carolingian government and Rome

The Frankish core, Saxony, Bavaria, Burgundy and Austrasia belong to one imperial realm with Aachen as its stable administrative seat. Charlemagne is physically in Rome at the coronation; the capital field is not an itinerary. Louis's Aquitaine and Pippin's Italy are subordinate kingdoms. Charlemagne's birth uses 748, an editorial choice among disputed dates. Personal skills, personality and uncertain birth years are game characterization. Unattested spouses and children are generated; the three courts are not wired into a fabricated saved family tree.

The Papacy has a separate territorial realm, including Rome and a compressed central Italian and exarchate grouping. Frankish protection and overlapping claims are not expressed as ownership of the Pope's office. Benevento under Grimoald III remains separate. Venice uses a Byzantine liege, with Malamocco as its period presentation; the existing displaced lagoon site remains the shared gameplay anchor. Sicily, Crete, Sardinia and the Dalmatian coastal cities are eastern possessions. The Avar basin retains a small representative remnant polity after Frankish victories; it does not depict an intact pre-conquest khaganate. Its exact frontier remains a review issue.

Louis was crowned king of Aquitaine in 781, while Pippin held Italy. These subordinate courts model regional government, not the later partition at Verdun. The ordinary campaign engine resolves external war at sovereign scope, so the Barcelona and Adriatic instances use the Carolingian sovereign as actor and condition their regional seats on that sovereign's ownership.

Iberia and Britain

Al-Hakam I's Cordoban emirate predates the caliphate and still holds Barcelona. Asturias under Alfonso II has its northern core; the Duero frontier is compressed into a single gameplay owner, not a claim of uninterrupted emirate administration. Pamplona is a representative frontier polity without a securely identified ruler. Burgos, Murcia and Almería use Arlanzón valley, Tudmir and Pechina presentations to avoid advertising later urban foundations. Santiago uses Iria Flavia, before the later pilgrimage center. East, Africa and uncertain regions

Harun's Abbasid realm includes Egypt, Syria, Iraq, the Iranian provinces, Khurasan, Sindh and Yemen. Raqqa is an authored court site but Baghdad remains the durable imperial capital. The Tahirid, Saffarid and Habbarid dynasties of the 867 map are absent. Ibrahim ibn al-Aghlab governs Ifriqiya as an Abbasid subordinate. Armenian principalities also use a compressed subordinate relationship. The Cordoban emirate is independent of Baghdad. Rustamid, Barghawata, Sijilmasa and Idrisid territories remain separate; the Idrisid regency uses Walila rather than a fully developed later Fes. Makuria and the Ethiopian highlands keep regional Christian kingdoms.

Communities, settlement slots and traditions

Each inhabited cell has explicit community weights totaling 10000. These are design inputs, not historical census estimates. Selected Iberian towns retain Christian communities under Muslim government; Egyptian and upper Mesopotamian cells retain substantial Coptic and Syriac communities. Ruling allegiance and local majority are separate. Sparse mixed-county coverage is a review limitation. Development is the existing ordinal 1-10 index, with specific earlier-town corrections, not a population reconstruction.

The independent 800 settlement layout owns period presentations for fifty major seats. Other counties use generated slots instead of importing the 867 fill list. Slot order and enduring physical-site slugs remain unchanged. Local names do not imply a relocated county or a recycled site id. Technology traditions record Latin, Byzantine, Islamic, Persianate, steppe and northeastern African knowledge at the relevant courts; the historical policy seeds knowledge against year 800 without granting later inventions.

History and acceptance limits

Barcelona (801-804), the Abbasid Anatolian frontier (806-809), the Danish coast (810-813) and the Adriatic rivalry (810-812) are conditional ordinary-war instances. Capital control, current target sovereignty, living courts, peace and the opportunity window must hold. Starting a campaign grants no territory; actual armies and peace determine the result. The Danish opportunity is a territorial-pressure approximation, not a simulation of transient raiding fleets. Iconium is a regional frontier objective, not a claim that Harun permanently annexed it in 806. Succession can interrupt a started opportunity, and resolved records remain closed after saving.

Ordinary dynastic succession continues, but the shared data factory cannot express the 806 succession settlement, the 817 arrangement, sibling partition pressures, the 843 treaty, Frankish-Abbasid gift exchanges or eastern recognition of the western imperial title. Do not substitute date-forced conquests, invented kinship or fabricated diplomatic outcomes. scripted is deliberately empty. These acceptance gaps keep the start outside FBDATA.bookmarks. Only owner acceptance and an explicit registration change may expose it. Requirement-check tooling, owner-run browser journeys and device review remain prerequisites for promotion.

Review access

Open this worktree's index.html. In the browser console assign FBDATA.bookmarks['800'] = FBDATA.bookmarkCandidates['800'], then use New Game and the normal starting-date picker. The review registration is session-only; reload clears it. The regression specification performs the same explicit registration. Save restore needs the candidate registered in that review session. This is a temporary acceptance workflow, not a hidden public bookmark API or a saved-state migration.

The seven standard starts already declare early_medieval in data/starts.js and need no bookmark-id additions. Normandy, Norway, England and Sicily remain future ambitions because their foundedYear values are later than 800. The Papacy uses the pre-1059 election law and the new 800 regnal seed: Leo III, John VII, Benedict II and Gregory III are already counted; Nicholas and Paschal have not yet appeared. Later bookmark seeds are unchanged. Cardinals and election procedures retain the existing game's institutional abstractions; this candidate does not claim a mature medieval College in 800.

Technology impact: period_800_conditional_campaigns uses none. Regional opportunities reuse the existing political and military capability; ordinary troop and equipment technology requirements still apply. All other changes are world data, period presentation or historical seeding.
