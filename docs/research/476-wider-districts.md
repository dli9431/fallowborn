Historical interpretation

County ids are durable map anchors. A district name at a medieval city id does not assert that the later city existed in 476. New local rulers and representatives are generated. Their birth years, households, institutions, political boundaries, development and population shares are editorial simulation choices. Broad archaeological evidence supports habitation more readily than a precise political constitution. Local assembly and lineage models are abstractions requiring historical review, especially in eastern forest districts.

Egypt retains Roman civil administration and a population distinct from imperial profession. Alexandria relocates its existing episcopal institution onto the inhabited map rather than creating another Timothy. Armenian Christian magnate households receive hereditary local interests within Sasanian government. Peroz and the existing royal genealogy remain in place. Settled Bactrian courts use regnum; moving Pontic followings retain their separate mobile population and access agreements.

Independent local faith roots explicitly declare their shared mechanical defaults. They do not inherit Norse priest titles, holy war doctrine or an assertion that unrelated local traditions belong to one church. Buddhist and Brahmanical profiles are minimal regional presentations with explicit marriage and clergy rules. These declarations do not constitute a complete historical religion simulation.

Physical interpretation

Each new physical seed has a 65 km Mercator-local radius limit. This is an editorial map extent, not an excavated frontier. The existing scenery seeds remain in place. Twenty scenery cells have bounded inhabited districts associated with them; w_registan retains its scenery representation. Retention means the unmodelled off-route landscape remains outside ordinary ownership, not that nobody ever lived or travelled there.

Overland links are explicit physical routes with travel-unit costs. Households, couriers, games journeys and armies pay their appropriate ordinary time and provision costs multiplied by those units. They do not receive water transport capacity. Long Saharan links are coarse travel abstractions and require historical and map review before release; their existence and exact weights are not claimed as measured 476 itineraries.

Acceptance

The corrected opening separates Saragur and Oghur mobile followings from river residents and gives Taman a Bosporan coastal court. Ras follows the existing eastern Roman Scupi side; Rosetta has a generated civil delegate. Inland constituencies describe river and lake households. The reusable startLocations field restricts the Colonus beginning to declared estate regions; later travel and restored tenure are unaffected.

Bounded regression specifications cover regional starts and borders, succession, death, mobile defeat, occupation, mixed-government consent and levies, cross-map restores, and revision-only authoring references. Multi-year evidence has a separate role: it evaluates continuing political and demographic behaviour beyond those interruption cases.

The data and generic engine contracts are implemented for review. Site chronology, exact local political groupings, faith shares, the northern court aggregates already present in the baseline and long overland links remain historical acceptance points. Shared geography and route constraints require visual raster inspection. Save budgets remain 4 MiB fresh, 6 MiB household-year and 8 MiB observer, with 30 seconds per 45-day observer slice.
