Government assignment ledger

Scope and confidence

This ledger accompanies data/government_rollout.js. It distinguishes sourced institutional authority, deliberately reduced simulation policies, retained approximations and pending consumers. An implemented assignment is not historical or gameplay acceptance. Exact county boundaries, constituency weights, ages, fees, stipends, terms and service fractions are simulation choices. No new named historical officer, genealogy, private estate, territorial grant or religious election is asserted. Source review: 2026-10-10.

Each assignment applies only to its named court and actual jurisdictions. Province ownership, communities, independently declared subordinate realms, private property, religious offices and stories retain their own declarations. An imperial title or religious claim does not make every nominal dependency directly administered. Religion, culture, terrain and unknown rulers never select a form automatically.

Shared behavior contracts

Administrative assignments use administrative_monarchy: public_offices holders, public_accession succession, fixed territory, delegated consent, public_service levy, public_receipts revenue, appointed_council, territorial_diplomacy, public_administration AI and public_careers progression. Existing authority appointments grant revocable jurisdiction and source-backed pay, not private ownership. Recognition reads effective officers and canonical succession. Households inherit privately. Profiles specify designation/recognition, minority, officer candidature, interests, confirmation and fiscal/service terms. AI pays at most one new appointment per court per season from its actual treasury.

Papal assignments use ecclesiastical_principality: institutional_holders, religious_office_bound succession, institutional_territory, ecclesiastical_consent, ecclesiastical_service levy, ecclesiastical_receipts revenue, ecclesiastical_council, ecclesiastical_diplomacy, ecclesiastical_decisions AI and ecclesiastical_careers. Each binds officeId catholic to the canonical Roman claimant. Continuing public property, receipts, treasury and debt use existing ledgers. Vacancy administration creates no second election or college. Temporal profiles do not define election law. layRecognition false means no additional government-layer confirmation, not an absence of external political influence.

Technology impact remains none under existing public_service_appointments, public_fiscal_assignments, public_military_obligations, public_sovereign_recognition and ecclesiastical reviews. These are additional consumers, not new equipment, financial instruments or logistical powers. Existing research conditions remain effective.

711: byzantium_711, byzantine_711

Basis: public imperial authority with appointed provinces and competing civil/military acceptance. PBE Philippikos 1, citing Theophanes AM 6203-6205 and Nikephoros, describes the 711 rebellion, proclamation and later displacement. This supports separating imperial office from hereditary property, not a lawful majority ballot. The opening remains Justinian II; no coup or historical successor is scheduled. Confidence: high for public authority and contested military recognition; moderate for county commissions.

Policy: adult recognition may include effective officers; civil weight 1, provincial weight 2 and army weight 1. Commissions require sovereign reaffirmation, salary is capped at 4 and 10 percent of its actual source, and funded service is 50 percent. Those quantities are game values. Direct public holders replace generated vassals, public careers replace feudal service, private property stays separate and Orthodox headship retains its owner.

Acceptance boundaries

The rollout specification covers current/baseline assignments, public holders/careers, canonical election laws, pure reads, appointments, payment/replay conservation, malformed-state rejection, JSON mod reuse and one-season save/replay journeys. Existing form specifications own deeper succession, multi-magistrate, coalition, household and reform cases. Historical acceptance, tuning, measured budgets, device and distribution review remain separate. Released-catalogue hashes belong to the compatibility suite and are never rebaselined by assignment changes.
