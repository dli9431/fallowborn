Administrative monarchy: dated profile basis

Scope

The 800 Abbasid and Byzantine profiles are separate abstractions of appointed public authority. They do not assert a shared constitution or uniform provincial institution. Fees, county-scale jurisdictions, source shares, terms, service percentages and recognition weights are gameplay values. The existing generated compact court remains simulation background unless royalCourt data explicitly declares a historical relative; it is not a researched roster. Neither profile automatically grants iqta, pronoia, freehold estates or hereditary counties.

Abbasid administration in 800

Jürgen Paul's fiscal survey distinguishes central accounting, assessment, collection and local obligations. Its Islamic-period discussion identifies amil and provincial financial governors and describes local expenses being met before surplus is sent to the centre. These passages support separate public assessment/remittance and a revocable office rather than giving a collector personal ownership of a whole county. They span multiple periods and do not establish the game's exact stipend or a universal 800 fiscal arrangement. The profile therefore uses source-capped public remuneration, local expenses before remittance, real arrears and audit/removal. It permits dynastic designation and minority while keeping the existing caliphal religious claim as a separate canonical office. https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/fiscal-system-iii-islamic-period/

The eqta survey distinguishes types of concession and the later development of military revenue assignments, including the tenth-century Buyid context. It supplies a boundary, not evidence for issuing military iqta in every 800 province. The Abbasid profile uses salary and ordinary funded hosts, with no automatic military land or revenue grant. https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/eqta/

Byzantine administration in 800

The King's College Prosopography of the Byzantine Empire's Eirene 1 record cites Theophanes AM 6282-6283 for army acceptance/refusal during the 790 crisis, AM 6291 for rival advisers in 799, and AM 6295 for high officials supporting Nikephoros in October 802 and Irene disclosing imperial finances. These dated events support treating the supreme office, military/civil interests, household property and public finance separately. They do not establish a formal majority ballot or a fixed universal succession constitution. The game's recognition rule is an explicit abstraction of contested public acceptance around this reign; it does not schedule Irene's overthrow or the historical successor. https://pbe.kcl.ac.uk/data/D23/F09.htm

The PBE career index distinguishes fiscal logothetes of the genikon from military and communications offices and lists genikon evidence in the eighth/ninth-century window. It supports using an independently dated fiscal office label rather than importing the game's late-antique Roman catalogue. A strategos is represented as an appointed provincial jurisdiction at county scale, and an exact commissioned host remains a separate authority scope. Stipends are source-capped; renewed sovereign confirmation and a funded commissioned service share are gameplay constraints, not a reconstruction of a complete thematic recruitment system or historical payroll rates. https://pbe.kcl.ac.uk/data/off/idx-281.htm

Fictional reuse and rollout

public_court supplies generic governor, fiscal officer and commander offices, revenue/service remuneration and adult recognition. A data-only fictional form/profile exercises the same APIs without historical names in engine dispatch. Only the explicitly selected 800 courts change in fresh campaigns. Existing 476, 867 and 1066 declarations and old saved government selections remain unchanged. Broader dated rollout requires its own profile and evidence.
