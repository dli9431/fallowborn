Historical context

The accounts distinguish the northern land approach, Raymond's overland Adriatic approach and Bohemond's crossing. The declaration supplies a short conditional staging road for each, rather than claiming a reconstructed continuous itinerary. Godfrey's first camp destination is Trier county, Raymond's is Narbonne county and Bohemond's is Bari county. These three initial legs and their requested local county scopes are simulation choices, not attested muster sites, travel dates, riverbanks or embarkation ports. Onward northern, Danube, Adriatic and eastern movement requires further ordinary reviewed access and transport. No accepted first leg proves a safe road to Constantinople.

Simulation choices and conserved resources

The potential formations contain Godfrey 320 fighters and 320 civilians, Raymond 320 and 480, and Bohemond 240 and 240. These are bounded demonstration rosters, not historical headcounts or a scale conversion. Each single external source includes its named original principal. Its committed fighters are allocated once to the ordinary levy class, an explicit simulation allocation with no historical cavalry ratio. The remaining people are civilians; there is no second reserve army, demographic withdrawal from the home county, free equipment or automatic family enrollment.

Public grants and voluntary private gifts use different existing accounts and create no consent. Formation pays the ordinary training cost and buys a fourteen-day target derived from actual current camp and field ration use, separately rounded within carrying capacity, from finite local market stock. The receipt retains that original purchase after conditions change. No property sale, extraordinary collection, historical treasury figure or automatic subsidy is implied.

The preparation deadline is campaign turn 270, and the original mandate and program end at turn 720, exclusively. These are finite gameplay windows, not historical departures or deaths. Optional early formation is possible; a seasonal agenda date does not impose an earliest muster. The limited-assurance records window is turns 360 through 719, simulation review pacing independent of an asserted arrival. Waiting, refusing, stopping or losing original authority neither sells home possessions nor creates a future crown.

Each local request asks the actual current direct holder for capacity equal to the original whole roster, no recruitment right, a price of one game gold and a term of 360 days. Those quantities and requested areas are simulation terms. An offer supplies no passage. The original requests never renew or retarget themselves.

The one optional supply checkpoint is independent of route progress and buys only an actual affordable deficit. A full target need not block the road. Programmed followings receive no recurring purchasing subsidy; ordinary reviewed contributions, current markets, pasture and camp-to-field refill retain their own costs and locations. Fourteen days of purchased food do not establish whole-route sustainability. A field-only manual order can leave the civilian camp behind. Ordinary manual redirection, pause, waiting and stopping remain available, without teleporting a return or cancelling a separate vow or war.
