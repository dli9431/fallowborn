Autumn 634 staged declarations

Scope

The current implementation stages identity and western regional data. Loading these inputs registers no 634 world, active court, community, start, religious office or historical relative. No existing date is modified. A later complete world composition owns publication and the physical distribution of communities.

Identity decisions

early_islam_634 is a direct child of muslim. Its earliest eligible year is 622, its opening selector is 634, and it has no closing year. No Sunni school, Abbasid head or inherited religious career is selected. Muslim legal dispatch and spouse capacities remain disclosed simulation settings; optional divorce is withheld. A political ruler title is independent of a religious-head office.

zoroastrian_sasanian_634 is a distinct dated child of zoroastrian, rather than an extension of the 476-493 court profile. Its monogamous household mechanics and existing sasanian_custom_476 dispatcher are explicit compatibility abstractions. No ritual sibling marriage, priestly qualification or exact seventh-century remedy is asserted. Its neutral learner and community-keeper labels confer no ordained rank.

west_syriac_lay_634 is a bounded lay child of miaphysite. It grants no Alexandrian head, ordained profession, automatic institution membership or paid military service. Customary law, monogamous capacity, same/in_fold family eligibility and withheld optional divorce are game choices, not a reconstructed canon-law code or a newly asserted denomination.

All three permit the ordinary eight identity consumers only within the 634 bookmark context. Their continued eligibility after the opening is a campaign convention, not evidence for later historical institutions. Head, holy-war tradition, raiding tradition and religious paths are explicitly absent; Early Islam also removes the inherited tournament tradition. There is no automatic conversion, religious replacement or award.

Source-faith availability

The fifteen additive selectors retain the earlier regional presentation pools. Adding 634 to a bookmark selector establishes technical eligibility only. It does not establish unchanged populations, majorities, political borders or religious continuity. All other restrictions and earlier permissions must survive publication. The older Sasanian profile, Mesopotamian traditional profile and 711 caliphal profile receive no extension.

Evidence boundary

These identity settings are first-pass simulation contracts. They establish no named person's age, residence, family, jurisdiction or local institution. Historical background for the period is discussed by Fred M. Donner, The Early Islamic Conquests (Princeton University Press, 1981), and Walter E. Kaegi, Byzantium and the Early Islamic Conquests (Cambridge University Press, 1992). These bibliographic references supply context, not evidence for the specific mechanical settings or a claim of newly inspected passages. Exact local law, specialist institutions and communities require their own evidence before declaration.

Western regional first pass

data/bookmark_634_scaffold_a.js supplies b01 through b07 and scenery_a. Its 126 inhabited county anchors span Iberia, Gaul and the Rhine; six additional cells represent broad desert and Sahel scenery. The blocks are independent literals using the physical geography europe_regional_2. They borrow the 568 regional partition, ordered settlement presentations, resident mixtures, development and customary possession defaults as provisional simulation inputs. No runtime copy of the 568 world is made. Population weights are game parameters, with no claim to a seventh-century census or unchanged religious majorities.

The Visigothic court is represented once at Toledo. The former Suevic kingdom, Corduban civic enclave and mainland imperial dependencies are compressed into its territory; Cantabrian and western Pyrenean local courts retain their provisional separate treatment. The court is Gothic and Nicene, while copied residents include distinct Hispano-Roman, Suevic, Basque and Homoian communities. Sisenand's reign is accepted background, but the available identity record supplies no supported birth range. The campaign therefore uses a generated adult representative, born 599 as a simulation choice, without Sisenand's name or family. The Balearic attachment remains a provisional Eastern Roman map choice, resolved through the external byzantium_634 court during complete composition.

Dagobert I has the sole principal Frankish court at Paris, with working birth 604 between the reference traditions of about 603 and 605. Austrasia has its own court at Metz under the child Sigebert III, working birth 630. The former Neustrian and Burgundian royal partitions share Dagobert's court. Austrasia references Francia as its superior; both retain the rank-3 royal scale. This is a deliberately reduced political relationship in the existing realm model, not a newly implemented regency, named mayoral administration or exact account of the relationship between the two kingdoms. Ordinary court actions represent collective government. No player start for Sigebert is introduced. Both named courts explicitly contain no spouse or additional relatives.

Armorican and Frisian coastal courts, together with the Cantabrian and western Pyrenean courts, use generated representatives born 599. Their source capital anchors, borders and local identity templates are provisional. No Judicael, named Frisian ruler or inherited 568 incumbent is asserted. The royal and local courts use the existing regnum abstraction. Local autonomy in Aquitaine, southwestern and Septimanian seams, eastern Frankish dependencies, precise Balearic jurisdiction and unmeasured community shares remain open historical questions. These provisional rows do not settle them.

Each court is declared in the block containing its capital. Date-owned title keys include their batch, preserving distinct pieces of source districts without duplicate title ownership. Physical site ids may retain older suffixes because they identify locations, independently of the date-owned court and settlement presentation. Layout order and seed coordinates are preserved. All opening actor, appointment, claim, migration, story and institution collections are empty. Complete composition must disable inherited institutional systems as appropriate; these blocks supply no dated clergy or religious-head office.

Western Sahara, Great Sahara, Libyan Desert, Sahel, Nubian Desert and Empty Quarter remain the six assigned scenery cells. Scenery expresses map scale and impassability, without asserting an absence of inhabitants, pastoral use, movement or political claims. The arid and steppe climate assignments are simulation settings. No route, civilian movement, new settlement or economic award is introduced.
