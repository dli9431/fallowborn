Autumn 634 staged declarations

Scope

The current implementation stages identity and western regional data. Loading these inputs registers no 634 world, active court, community, start, religious office or historical relative. No existing date is modified. A later complete world composition owns publication and the physical distribution of communities.

Source-faith availability

The fifteen additive selectors retain the earlier regional presentation pools. Adding 634 to a bookmark selector establishes technical eligibility only. It does not establish unchanged populations, majorities, political borders or religious continuity. All other restrictions and earlier permissions must survive publication. The older Sasanian profile, Mesopotamian traditional profile and 711 caliphal profile receive no extension.
