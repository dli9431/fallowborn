Spring 711 inhabited districts

Scope and evidence boundary

The terrain_v2_governments_v2 opening uses europe_regional_2. It preserves 477 previously inhabited counties, adds eight inhabited districts and leaves 42 scenery entries: the original 21 plus 21 unused additions. There are 527 authored anchors, 485 inhabited declarations and four additional generated local courts. These are declaration counts, not measured raster coverage. All seven scenery groups and all 29 shared additions have a disposition below. Retaining scenery records a limit of this implementation, not proof that the historical landscape was empty.

Coordinates refer to the shared bounded physical districts, in longitude and latitude. Their 65 km radius and dry-route costs are simulation choices. County titles are spatial groupings, not claims that medieval duchies or empires existed there. No accepted district creates a new behavior or religion. The source remains independently authored for 711; the 476 political map supplies no authority or religious evidence.

Implemented inhabited districts

ghadames_oasis (9.50,30.13): add county, Ghadames head, town, development 2, arid climate, generated local ghadames_711. UNESCO's description of the occupation sequence includes Christianity and the Islamic conquest. Jacqueline Passon, Places of Trade, Communication and Religion, section 6.3.1, dates the Arab occupation to 667 and distinguishes the later eighth-to-tenth-century Ibadi phase. Local territorial authority is an editorial conservative boundary: conquest and religious allegiance do not prove permanent direct caliphal administration in 711. Berber Muslim and Christian weights 5000/5000 are explicitly uncertain simulation inputs, with no census claim or imported Ibadi institution. Its ordinary garden/craft households connect to Kairouan through the existing seven-unit dry corridor. https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/362/ https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-00145-2_6

gao_district (-0.04,16.26): add county, Gao Saney head at (0.02,16.30), town, development 2, arid climate, separate niger_bend_711 local authority. Mamadou Cisse's excavation report abstract, Recent excavations at Gao Saney: New evidence for early trade, describes early deposits radiocarbon-dated AD 700-1000, with manufacturing debris and exchange goods. Inclusion at 711 uses the early end of this broad range, with medium confidence, not a precisely dated foundation. The local Sahelian naming and practice proxies have the same limitations as Jenne-jeno; no Songhay empire, legendary king, mosque or later palace is generated. https://www.panafprehistory.org/en/resources/entry/recent-excavations-at-gao-saney-mali-new-evidence-for-early-trade

The inhabited Niger route is Jenne-jeno to Gao (5 units), Gao to Ghadames (22), and Ghadames to Kairouan (7), using the pre-existing shared corridors. All endpoints are independently inhabited; there is no travel through an uninhabited county pretending to be a town. These distances and the route abstraction remain physical gameplay inputs, not a claim of a single documented 711 caravan itinerary or unified sovereignty. Desert remnants stay impassable and normal provisions still apply.

The existing sahelian_476 naming pool and sahel_traditional_476 headless local-practice profile are broad presentation/gameplay proxies selected for this date, not archaeological determinations of ethnicity, marriage rules or priestly offices. Its single community weight of 10000 is an abstraction. Ordinary household cultivation, craft and exchange remain available; no historical incumbent is named.

qasr_ibrim (31.99,22.65): add county, Ibrim head, town, development 2, arid climate, makuria_711. S4 supports occupation. kath_district (60.75,41.69): add county, Kath head, town, development 3, continental climate, independent khwarazm_711. S2 and S3 support the pre-conquest Afrighid court and Zoroastrian community. regnum uses direct county holding and dynastic succession, avoiding an invented electorate or provincial aristocratic contract. Compatibility and acceptance

The government composer retains the government-only revision from baseline fields. Existing campaign stories, religious offices, opening actors and dates are inherited unchanged. The Iberian targets remain Malaga, Toledo, Narbonne and the Asturian uplands. No added county satisfies those ownership predicates or creates a future realm.

The date-specific fixture covers all eight accepted districts, all 21 retained additions and the 21 scenery ids. Regression journeys exercise local household starts, current courts, dynastic succession, public delegation, climates, dry connections, bounded raster/sites, one natural season, observer AI, both distribution flags and cross-revision saves including a legacy frontier conversion. Existing released-catalogue tests remain the compatibility gate for 867/1066 and shared definitions.

Acceptance workload: use the ordinary early-medieval population and court settings, fixed fixture seeds, one 90-day season, midpoint/final save and replay. Starting budgets remain 4 MiB fresh and 6 MiB progressed serialized saves; there is no budget increase. Owner measurements must record boot/map time, per-day and annual work separately, fresh/restored bytes, record growth, raster connectivity, household/army travel and small-device behavior. Historical uncertainty, withheld regional candidates, device review and runtime acceptance remain explicit; declaration counts are not acceptance measurements.
