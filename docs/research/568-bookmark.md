Evidence and presentation

Paul the Deacon, History of the Langobards, translated by William Dudley Foulke (1906), Book II.7 describes departure from Pannonia with households; II.26 describes a following comprising several peoples. This later narrative supports keeping a following's political label separate from each person's culture and faith. It does not establish population shares, a uniform appearance, precise name frequencies or a common religion. The accessible translation and its editorial apparatus were consulted separately on 2026-10-17. https://elfinspell.com/MedievalMatter/PaultheDeacon-PaulusDiaconus/HistoryOfTheLangobards/PaulTheDeacon-Book2.html

langobard_568 is a standalone early Langobard presentation. It has no cultural parent and does not inherit the existing later Romance lombard profile. Its four male and three female forms reuse the generic Elbe Germanic presentation pool in data/bookmark_476_wider_identities.js. Its west_european affinity, of_place dynasty naming and portrait palette are simulation defaults, not a linguistic genealogy or a claim about a historical person's face. It declares no military, raiding or conversion bonus and supplies no historical relative or incumbent.

avar_568 is a bounded Avar court presentation with no cultural parent. The four male and three female forms borrow the existing generic Central Steppe campaign pool as openly reconstructed names. The portrait palette is a presentation default. Neutral other affinity and of_place dynasty naming avoid deriving political, religious or military rules from a proposed ancestry. This declaration does not equate Avars with Magyar, Khazar, Hun or Turkic identities. A dated local roster and any named historical ruler remain separate evidence work.

Neither generated pool contains Alboin, Rosamund, Rosemund, Albsuinda or Bayan. Naming a generated character from either pool identifies a campaign person, never a replacement for one of those historical people. Personal faith, kin, rank, residence, political rights and historical biography are not supplied by either culture. The ordinary culture-default machinery remains authoritative; no new technology-dependent capability is introduced by these content declarations.

The principal source is the public spring 493 baseline with its world_v2 expansion in data/bookmark_493.js and data/bookmark_493_world.js. These district ids imply neither medieval towns nor later states.
