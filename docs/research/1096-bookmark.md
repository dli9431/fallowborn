1096 opening research note

Quadrant c, first-pass regional declarations

data/bookmark_1096_scaffold_c.js stages five independently replaceable regional payloads for a Spring 1096 opening: britain_south (33 physical ids), nordic_baltic (34), central_danube (28), rus_steppe (32), and jazira (5). Together they contain 132 physical ids, including 122 inhabited counties, five unresolved potential districts and five broad scenery areas. These declarations do not register a bookmark, alter earlier worlds, add starts or install institutions. They require complete world composition before campaign use.

The mechanical source is the 1066 inhabited_v2 declaration on europe_regional_2 at game commit 0f5f442278f91978038f181f6f9300a641f14ee5. County partitions, coordinates, terrain, development, resident culture and faith pairs, numerical community weights and ordered curated and fill settlement layouts are literal copies. The date owns independent objects. Its court names, rulers, capitals and listed ownership exceptions are redated. Shared physical settlement ids keep their existing coordinates and slot order. First-pass layouts do not independently establish that every settlement name, town rank or local community remained unchanged in 1096.

Evidence boundary

The four named ruler identities below follow standard-reference biographies. Their use establishes limited incumbent and birth choices, rather than a dated county survey, household genealogy or precise royal itinerary. Capital anchors, source-sized jurisdictions, broad court cultures and faiths, population weights, ranks, skills, traits, military and economic quantities are simulation choices. A court's identity never converts its residents. Every other ruler in these five payloads is explicitly generated, without a historical name or authored royal genealogy. Generated rulers use the simulated birth year 1060, inside the adult range 1041-1076; neither that year nor their generated families are historical claims.

Southern Britain

england_1096 has William II Rufus, fixed birth 1060 within the approximate range 1058-1062, a Norman Catholic court and the London anchor. The copied English resident communities remain English, including the source's northern-language mixtures where present. England and Normandy are separate courts; no later pledge or overseas conquest is seeded. No 1066 Welsh incumbent is retained. This payload owns England's sole court definition; northern English counties in another payload reference it.

Composition and replacement

Each FBDATA.bookmarkRegions1096 payload exposes provinces, realms and layouts. A literal FBDATA.bookmarkRevisionContent_1096_<region> table owns the same payload, with bookmarkId 1096 and baseline revision, so the existing static localization extractor can find its county, realm and settlement labels. No new authoring root, shared runtime behavior, technology capability or saved field is introduced. The complete composer must install each payload's layouts and resolve the three foreign courts after all regions are available. Replacing a regional payload must preserve its exact physical allocation and remove its first-pass definition so that composition has one owner for every county and court.
