Civic republic profile evidence

Reviewed 2026-10-08. The rollout changes fresh 1204 Venice only. Earlier Venice openings retain their existing government. Genoese and fictional profiles are independent reuse examples and do not change the dated political map.

Venice

Treccani describes the twelfth-century narrowing of ducal power and growth of councils. A council of six ducal advisers is attested before 1204. Quarantia and Pregadi belong to later thirteenth-century institutional development, while the hereditary closure associated with the Serrata follows 1297. The 1204 policy therefore represents ducal advisers, civic council interests and ducal electors without importing those later bodies or automatic patrician ancestry. https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/venezia/ https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/doge/

The Palazzo Ducale museum's doge chronology places Enrico Dandolo's rule in 1192-1205. The opening retains his authored name, sex, birth year and martial interpretation, with no hereditary city succession. A continuing lifetime office is modeled alongside lawful resignation and removal; the simulation does not prescribe the later forty-one-elector machinery. https://palazzoducale.visitmuve.it/wp-content/uploads/2020/09/DOWNLOADS-IL-DOGE-ITA-ENG-2020.pdf

Treccani distinguishes Venetian sovereign territories from commercial establishments inside another authority's territory after the Fourth Crusade, including Constantinople. The city's Latin political holder stays separate from the Venetian commercial presence. The game represents that presence by a zero-receipt quarter right attached to the existing Constantinople site. It does not assert a surveyed boundary or a numeric share of port income, and it does not turn the whole county into Venetian territory. This is a coarse site reference for historical presence, rather than a reconstruction of every division of the city. https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/venezia_%28Dizionario-di-Storia%29/ https://risorse-esterne.edizionicafoscari.it/lemmi/Constantinopoli.html

Genoese tradition

The published 1143 consular oath provides one-year consular service and the relationship between civic office and the sworn compagna. It supports a separate membership requirement and annual tenure. Two modeled consular seats and unanimous external decisions are a reduced game interpretation, not a claimed reconstruction of the full historical college. https://www.rm.unina.it/didattica/fonti/patrone/sez1/par6.htm

Annals describing the transition to a podesta in 1190, and Treccani's account of alternating consular and podesta arrangements, prevent applying this 1143 interpretation automatically to Genoa in 1204. The profile is independently usable through a declaration or deliberate founding review, with its evidence date visible in its display name. https://www.rm.unina.it/didattica/fonti/bordone/sez4/cap25.htm https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/podesta-del-comune-italiano_%28Federiciana%29/

The State Archive of Genoa publishes the 1190 Tyre grant, including civic jurisdiction and a fraction of port revenue. This documents why an institutional foreign right needs a different owner from a county sovereign. No Tyre opening right is awarded here: dated continuity to 1204 is not established by that earlier grant alone. Public revenue contracts created in play use the existing actual source and explicit negotiated share. https://archiviodistatogenova.cultura.gov.it/valorizzazione-mostre-ed-eventi/servizi-educativi/attivita-culturali/mostre-virtuali/i-genovesi-e-la-terrasanta/1190-tiro-1

Simulation boundaries

Qualifications, influence weights, nomination fees, salaries, support calculations, recall thresholds, reserve amounts, contract prices and the reduced collegiate size are game parameters. The public-service city is fictional: either sex may qualify, service is required, terms differ by office and two of three magistrates can assent. The shared engine reads these declared policies and never selects a historical constitution by a place name, culture, wealth or missing family information.
