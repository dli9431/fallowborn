Paper and royal-order transmission

Scope and classification

Five emergence/widespread adoption pairs change in three existing technologies. These inclusive game-year schedules are reconstruction and simulation choices. They do not date invention, measure diffusion, guarantee local manufacture, or establish an operating royal office. Attestation envelopes, source codes, descriptions, prerequisites, explicit research costs, effects, unlocks and capability grants retain their previous values.

Papermaking retains the 650-950 attestation envelope and BLOOM/SINGER source metadata. Its Persianate pair changes from 700/950 to 650/800; its Islamic pair changes from 690/940 to 750/950. Every other pair remains inherited. data/technology_transmission.js provides one complete literal correction, including the unchanged Papermaking display fields. Paper knowledge supplies its existing research refinement without creating a workshop, materials or inventory.

Paper Scholarship retains the 750-1050 attestation envelope and GRANT/LINDBERG source metadata. Islamic 760/1010 and Persianate 770/1020 both become 800/1000 in the existing data/technology_learning.js correction. Paper Scholarship still requires paper_making and scriptoria and supplies research 0.25. Organized manuscript learning and physical Library construction remain available through their own manuscript requirements.

Royal Capitularies retains the 650-900 attestation envelope and BLOCH/BERMAN source metadata. Its Latin pair changes from 710/870 to 700/800 in data/technology_civic_military.js. The retained written_law and diplomatic_correspondence prerequisites and levy 0.01 effect grant neither a public appointment nor authority to issue orders. All other regional pairs remain inherited.

Fresh and retained campaigns

The existing selector chooses the whole pair with the earliest emergence, then the earlier widespread year on an emergence tie. Combining Persianate and Islamic traditions therefore selects Papermaking's 650/800 pair; it does not construct a new pair from independent endpoints. Paper Scholarship has equal 800/1000 pairs. Research-selection culture restrictions remain separate from historical knowledge and explicit complete/expose/omit exceptions.

For single-tradition historical seeding at 867, Persianate knowledge gains paper_making and Latin knowledge gains capitularies. Every other existing completion and exposure set remains the same. At 1066 no tradition changes its existing completion or exposure set. Real sovereign exceptions and prerequisite closure can already supply those ids, so these nominal differences must not be added blindly to every realm. National caps still bound the existing effects.

Retained completed, exposed, active and partial-project records remain authoritative, together with research reserves and standing or ruined property. Matching restore never reruns historical seeding, adds a prerequisite, or installs a physical asset for these schedules. There is no new save field or schema.

Authoring and review

The ordinary tech JSON root accepts independent complete definitions under the same pure validation and historical consumers as bundled records. tests/e2e/fixtures/authoring/transmission-paper.json uses different paper windows and an equal-emergence scholarship tie; transmission-orders.json delays Latin royal-order adoption and makes an earlier Islamic paper emergence select a later completion than its Persianate counterpart. Neither example claims a historical site or date. Source extraction discovers the transmission correction through the existing supplemental convention and retains tech.paper_making display owners.

tests/e2e/specs/early-knowledge-transmission.spec.js covers complete-definition preservation, the five boundary changes, mixed traditions, omissions, the entire existing catalogue's released-date differences, baseline/current save retention, real Mods imports and pure rejected schedules. Existing composition and learning expectations account only for the declared window changes. No geographic or political fixture is regenerated. Adoption timing is a numeric catalogue correction and introduces no new technology-impact review, national cap, institution or physical facility.
