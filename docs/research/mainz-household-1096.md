Mainz household in Spring 1096

The playable protagonist and established relatives are fictional Ashkenazi Jewish craft workers. Their ordinary work, household resources and generated kin have no claim to reconstruct a named medieval family. There is no special wealth, communal purse, trade advantage or survival reward. The existing resident community and lay congregation retain their own identity and voluntary participation.

Franz Staab, Ruthard, Neue Deutsche Biographie 22 (2005), pp. 303-304, supports the archbishop's office and the 1096 protective context. Ruthard is one character at the Mainz see. Born 1050 is an explicitly chosen simulation age, not a sourced birth. No named spouse, children, guards or personal fortune is inferred. https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd137976194.html

The residence site near the existing Mainz anchor is a disclosed simulation locality. Its coordinates do not identify surviving masonry, a particular entrance or a reconstructed palace plan. The existing Mainz town presentation becomes an authored town for local household presence; other settlements and the communal institution remain separate. The new site is neither Speyer nor Worms, and those places acquire no equivalent shelter contract.

Civil permission uses a generated local custodian under the imperial realm, represented through the ordinary territorial form. This is a disclosed county-scale abstraction of the separate local authorization needed for the service, not an attested new historical ruler, a claim over every archiepiscopal estate, or a second Ruthard. The archbishop is the named institutional protector; his offer and the custodian's civil authorization are separate real transactions. Neither the distant emperor nor spiritual office alone supplies local consent. The generated custodian's birth 1060 and ordinary court statistics are simulation values.

The declared eight person-places, fifteen-day response window, thirty-day protective term and zero per-person service charge are simulation terms. They reconstruct no room count, garrison, historical price, deposited property, communal payment or probability of safety. Each adult separately agrees to refuge; an actual consenting parent decides for a dependent. Exact people must already be halted in Mainz before a separate explicit site entry. Protection reserves finite places without creating food, guards, a journey, new residence or immunity. Refusal, unavailable people, lost permission, provider replacement and expired terms remain actual outcomes.
