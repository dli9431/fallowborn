# Composed Spring 1096 worlds

The fresh `pilgrim_followings_v1` revision extends `western_expeditions_v1`, retaining the Mainz household world and the regional baseline. It adds Peter's and Walter's independent, initially unformed followings and the Cologne household's admission and named-party tracks. Earlier revisions remain available to saved campaigns. The seven starts covered together by `tests/e2e/specs/1096-integration.spec.js` are the Cologne pilgrim household, Mainz Jewish household, Godfrey, Raymond, Bohemond, Alexios and Kilij Arslan.

The four regional scaffold files supply the 23 blocks consumed by `data/bookmark_1096.js`. The composer rejects duplicate county and court identities and conflicting shared title parents. The regional evidence and unresolved locality distinctions remain in [1096-bookmark.md](1096-bookmark.md). Later nonterritorial following declarations do not replace county owners or add territorial holdings. The western and pilgrim additions have separate resource and historical limits in [western-expeditions-1096.md](western-expeditions-1096.md) and [pilgrim-household-1096.md](pilgrim-household-1096.md).

`index.html` loads the regional sources before the bookmark composer, then the campaign declarations and world stories, Mainz revision and start declarations. The later personal story sources precede the western expedition revision, followed immediately by the pilgrim revision. All remain ordered deferred classic scripts. Source staging captures declarations before complete-engine validation; the common instance catalogue is not a second owner for these later dated batches. Story dependencies and registration order determine dispatch and successful attachment order, which need not equal the bookmark's declaration-array order.

There is one `eastern_undertaking_1096` holy-war call. The current canonical campaign owns participation, muster, contributions and settlement. The Mainz refuge, western preparation and road programs, limited assurance, pilgrim preparations, civilian admissions and family parties retain separate records and transactions. Personal agendas and household priorities cannot grant passage, pay formation costs or enroll relatives. Later sieges, crossings and historical outcomes remain omitted where the evidence or executable declaration is absent.

Fresh saves retain the existing schema-1 `state.start.storyAttachments` ledger and paired `storyAttachmentSchema` envelope marker. The ledger records successful attachments in registry order, including terminal histories. A missing recorded story, duplicate track or unpaired marker rejects validation. Legacy saves with neither field remain unstamped. Revision activation precedes restore; restoring an earlier world does not attach the fresh pilgrim programs, add people, replenish accounts or replay choices. The save envelope remains v4 with platform schema 2 and authority schema 4. Preparation, program, admission, party and mobile receipt validation stays with the existing owning engines; this composition introduces no optional save schema or new engine protocol literal.

The integration spec validates every retained revision without mutating data, RNG or uid, starts all seven roles in the fresh revision, checks exact world and personal attachment closure, compares saved people, resources, holdings and armies after restoration, and rejects malformed attachment provenance without mutating the candidate or live state. A separate earlier-world case restores across fresh activation and checks genuine legacy absence. These cases supplement the focused world-story, ruler, Mainz, pilgrim, corridor and lifecycle specs; they do not simulate a multi-year journey or establish historical or device acceptance.
