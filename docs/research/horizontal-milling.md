Horizontal milling catalogue

The horizontal_mill profile is a simulation alternative on the existing mill owner. It represents a simpler horizontal wheel, with no new technology id, ethnic requirement, historical installation or inferred source. The catalogue accepts a mechanism class; a world author still needs evidence for a named site, source, period and actual inherited machine. Ancient conduits, county terrain and cultural identity alone do not establish that inventory. The public technology-and-buildings reconciliation remains the combined catalogue owner.

The simulation values are cost 30, upkeep 0, minimum development 1, tax 1 and population capacity 0.03. Development, population and prestige construction awards are zero. Effects replace the base mill values of tax 2, development 1 and capacity 0.05. The inclusive new-construction window is 476-1300. Water Power is a hard requirement for this optional organized machine; ordinary hand and animal grinding are the ungated alternative. Public annual AI priority is 18. These values model gameplay scale, not archaeological output, a universal mill type or measured diffusion.

Actual operation requires the exact lawful physical site, declared source and gradient, and a paid finite service. The bundled source profile offers four water units per seven-day boundary; one allocation consumes one unit and pays one gold of work for the bounded term. That single receipt supplies both existing effect fields. No construction bonus, second development award, extra tax, research, output stock or population is created. Current projection stops with ruin, lost permission, custody or term expiry. Already consumed water and paid work are neither refunded nor replayed.

Unprofiled mills retain all original values and gates. Inherited variants remain property outside the new-construction date or technology window, including ruins. Repair retains the selected definition and original identity, uses its ordinary half-price invoice and creates neither a source nor an operating receipt. Matching saves preserve depleted resources and dormant or removed assets; malformed or changed profile provenance rejects.

The two ordinary JSON authoring examples are fictional complete worlds. regional-milling-river.json uses a declared local flow and the bundled horizontal alternative. regional-milling-catchment.json uses a differently priced wheel, actual empty storage, paid filling and revocable civil source permission. Both provide explicit empty, standing and ruined revisions. Neither is a historical asset inventory or a grant of authority to the player. Mod imports use the same validators, transactions, current projections, AI and save contracts as native declarations.
