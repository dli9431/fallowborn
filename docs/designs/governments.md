Government dispatch

Forms and defaults

FBDATA.governmentForms composes ten behavior slots: holders, succession, territory, consent, levy, revenue, council, diplomacy, ai and ladder. Vocabulary is retired; government wording belongs only in the governments layer of FBDATA.lexicons. A form naming vocabulary fails validation with a migration message. The five bundled forms name every slot explicitly. The optional slots of older JSON mods still resolve engine_default, a deprecated alias for a named implementation, never an empty object.

The named defaults are feudal_levy, demesne_revenue, royal_council, territorial_diplomacy, realm_decisions and inherited_ladder. Territorial and regnum forms inherit their era ladder; roman_civic and federate_polity keep their existing overlays. Existing federate_contracts and roman_offices AI strategies compose the common realm decisions with their contract and office transactions. Office and acclamation succession explicitly delegate to dynastic. They describe existing frontier succession, not a newly implemented election or appointment system.

Interface and installation

FB.governments.call(state, realmId, slot, method, context, resolved) dispatches a named method. Context has named fields documented below. The optional resolved argument carries the already-resolved form into batched county calculations. Read methods return plain records, arrays, numbers or booleans and must not change state, RNG or uid. Existing state-changing transactions still own grants, payments, appointments, recruitment and accession. Engine-default adapters retain their existing function bodies and evaluation order through FB.governments.bind. The government_*.js modules install after fiscal.js, before presentation consumers, after all their underlying engine overrides exist. They do not add a second scheduler.

Register with FB.governments.register(slot, id, implementation). An empty registration is rejected immediately. FB.governments.contract declares the required methods for a behavioral slot; resolution and validation reject incomplete or unknown implementations. A direct delegate may name one nondelegating strategy in the same slot. Delegate cycles and unresolved targets fail resolution. Ladder remains a validated data overlay, not a function interface. Trusted strategy source is registered at script load; JSON mods only choose existing ids and typed parameters. Never select algorithms by bookmark, realm, culture or faith ids.

Runtime cache and saved state

FB.governments.forRealm reads authority.governments[realmId].formId, otherwise start.defaultGovernment, otherwise the legacy territorial default. A transient WeakMap caches the resolved strategy set per state and day. A changed saved form id, replaced definition, changed definitions table, explicit invalidation or new state forces resolution. Mod application invalidates after changing a form in place. Same-day changes are visible immediately. County settlement batches retain their resolved form within the synchronous projection. No resolution, cache or parameter copy is serialized, and reads never create authority records. A missing/dead realm uses the named shared fallback at legacy household and invalid-input boundaries; an invalid named form on a living realm fails closed. Profiling counts Government strategy resolutions through the existing opt-in profiler.

Consumer inventory and interfaces

Levy: world.js owns playerCompositionBreakdown (composition, context baseline) and aiBaseHost (fieldHost, realmId, recruitment, countyInputs, context). PlayerLevy and playerMaxLevy total that composition, so projections, host creation and displayed capacity share it. Actions.js owns domainCountyLevyBase (countyBase, provinceId), vassalLevyRate (subjectRate, realmId) and vassalLevyContribution (subjectContribution, realmId and recruitmentRealm). The latter captures wars.js's final hostile-service override. Rebellions.js supplies rebelLevySize (rebelSize, provinceId). Treasury.js supplies hostFieldUpkeepParts (upkeep, host and batch). MayCall returns permission to muster; both player quotes and committing player/AI host creation read it. SettlementBase receives provinceId, settlement, share, population, modifier, buildings and support; service receives amount and share. Lordships.js calls these for personal recruitment and delegated service before the existing capacity and aggregation steps. Regiment composition, physical population, occupation, supply and garrison facts remain generic inputs; numerical rates remain balance and charter data.

Revenue: actions.js supplies playerTaxParts (playerLines, empty context), countyTaxBase (countyBase, provinceId, rate and localTaxBonus), and vassalTaxContribution (subjectContribution, realmId). Lordships.js supplies settlementTaxBase (settlementBase, provinceId, settlement, rate and shares). The playerLines record retains the named rents, dues, tolls, national, council, positions, monopoly, papacy, bishopric, abbey and liege amounts and their authoritative total; countyLoss and settlementLoss are explanatory subdivisions, not extra receipts. TreasurySnapshot calls realmIncome with tax, buildings and technology once per realm, then remittance with amount, share, recipientId and kind. The same remittance method handles player county dues, vassal dues and delegated settlement dues; kind distinguishes realm from settlement, with provinceId and settlement on the latter. LoanAssignment receives amount, share and loan in both financeAssignedIncomeCost and collectAssignedRevenue. FiscalAssignment receives surplus through fiscalAssignedIncome. Existing transactions collect each source once; private work, gifts, trades, purchases and transfer bookkeeping are not government income algorithms.

Council: seats returns the ordered active catalogue through councilSeats; optional strategyParameters.council.seats filters that catalogue by existing seat ids while retaining original catalogue order, system gates and station rules. An empty list disables the council. Eligible receives seat, candidateId and appointment through councilHolderEligible. Vacancy receives seat and a detached ordered candidate list and returns a candidate id or null. CouncilEnsure and councilRecommendation use that selection, while the existing transaction writes the appointment. Summary, bonus, picker, appointment and confirmation consumers all obtain seats through this interface. Today's council record belongs to the player; a strategy does not create an AI council ledger.

Diplomacy: warGoals receives includeBlocked and readOnly through warCauses. GoalAllowed receives a cause and filters the displayed catalogue and declaration eligibility. TerritorialRights and customaryRights receive realmId and provinceId; declaration receives realmId, causes and inspection through warDeclarationPreview. PeaceTerms receives warId through warPeaceOptions; negotiatedTerms receives custom through warPeaceTerms; termsCost receives war through warTermsCost. SubjectRelation returns kind and liegeId, consumed by vassal war law. Existing claims, territorial adjacency, religious authority, fiscal eligibility and treaty transactions retain their sources. Ordinary peace transactions consume their dispatched options, and legacy tribute, withdrawal, bought peace and submission use their dispatched gold and prestige. Submission standing remains the existing oath effect; its quoted standing is an explanatory capped projection. The readOnly war-goal mode is pure; the legacy false mode retains its existing claim/pact housekeeping at the caller's mutation boundary.

AI: goals returns warChance and allianceChance for seasonal campaigns and annual alliance openings. StrategyParameters.ai.warChance may supply a finite number in [0,1]; omission keeps the original ruler-personality chance. Campaign receives realmId and runs the existing per-realm campaign transaction; aggression receives realmId, provinceId and territory; expansionPriority receives realmId and provinceId; expansionProvince receives realmId, loserRealm and the existing allowed predicate; expansionTarget receives realmId, defenderIds, defensiveStrength and allowed; raid receives realmId, realm, balance and knownPeaceful; breakawayChance receives realmId. MotionScore receives bloc, motionId and courtEconomicPowerAverage in the polity's context. Construction receives actor, provinceId, settlement and buildingId through aiCanBuildAt. CanNegotiate reports contract participation; consentContract receives contract, proposeMigration receives migration, and offices uses the realm id. These last three preserve the existing authority/federate scheduler and transaction order. Demography, mortality, technical research, supply pathfinding and specialist religious/institutional schedulers remain generic system behavior rather than government AI goals. Data continues to own building priorities, policy interests and numerical balance.

Holders: assign is the existing creation transaction, called with state and realmId. Conquest receives provinceId and previousHolderId and returns the direct holder for transferProvince. Vacancy receives provinceId, previousHolderId and proposedHolderId and chooses the escheat recipient after existing lawful grant selection. Both defaults preserve direct conquest and existing escheat results. Transactions accept alternate holders only while alive and within the receiving sovereign realm. Explicit voluntary grants, protagonist realm remapping, absorption and already-awarded settlement property execute their reviewed transaction rather than a fresh holder lottery.

Territory: profile returns movingCourt and holdsCounties. Federate mobility, settlement-form validation and province transfer consume it. Defeat receives counties, provinceId and conquerorId and returns dissolve; transferProvince applies the existing extinction and reattachment transaction. Migration receives spec at federateBeginMigration's mutation boundary; settlement receives migrationId and returns the pure federate settlement quote. Fixed territory rejects moving-court operations. Non-territorial courts retain existing temporary conquest possession and migrate through the existing reviewed contracts and settlement transaction. Existing nonTerritorial saved markers retain their meaning.

Succession: candidates receives succession and frontier and adapts the existing eligible/order policy. Vacancy receives cause and returns allowed, cause and opening rulerGeneration; callers may identify death, abdication, term_end or lawful_removal without inventing saved progress. Accession receives succession and cause and returns realmId, memberId and rulerGeneration. AdvanceRealmSuccession validates the chosen living frontier and generation before applying the existing character/title transition. Refresh returns whether annual eligibility needs rebuilding. Dynastic retains frontier ordering; agnatic_seniority retains founder validation, adult male-line ordering and annual refresh. Office and acclamation delegate explicitly to dynastic, preserving current gameplay. Existing religious-head elections and protagonist inheritance retain their canonical owners instead of manufacturing competing government vacancies.

Consent: local determines whether territorial authority can satisfy county Consent. Decision receives actor, operation and scope. It supports existing extraordinary_tax and federate_contract rules and the levy, war, treaty and grant transaction hooks. Constituency receives the same context plus the decision and returns kind, realmId, operation and members, a reference to the existing required-consent ids. The facade uses members as the decision's required list. The default adapters add no constituency ledger. Assembly strategies use the shared typed membership, weight, selection and mandate contract owned by FB.governments.support; see docs/designs/assembly-polity.md.

Ladder: inherited_ladder is an empty bands overlay delegating to the campaign's era ladder; roman_civic and federate_polity retain the existing bands. Station queries consume bands, ranks and social fields under the station validator, including explicit false permissions.

Boundaries and verification

The original dispatch platform is internal infrastructure with no technology gate. The assembly consumer adds reviewed capabilities and a bounded saved contract, documented with its technology decisions in docs/designs/assembly-polity.md. Presentation continues to use the existing public projections, localized error patterns and transaction reviews. Source ratchets apply to every new strategy module. Numerical/list variations belong in data, with only council seat selection and AI war chance added as typed parameters here; unsupported parameter slots and keys are rejected.

The government-dispatch specification covers registration and form validation, compatibility aliases, test-only alternative strategies, data-only composed forms, bounded seasonal replay and restore in all three baseline bookmarks, read purity and daily cache invalidation/cost reporting. Existing parity-fresh, authority, federation, council, economy, war, station, mod and save suites remain the behavior references. Fixed-seed equality and elapsed-time comparisons must be established by the owner-controlled harness and profiler, not inferred from source inspection.
