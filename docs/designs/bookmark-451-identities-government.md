# Spring 451 identities and government catalogues

The hidden Spring 451 world uses the ordinary culture, religion, government,
office and mobile-profile validators. Its supplemental declarations live in
`data/bookmark_451_identities.js`, `data/bookmark_451_governments.js` and
`data/bookmark_451_presentation.js` and are published by the dated composer.

`hunnic_451` denotes a heterogeneous campaigning court. It has its own neutral
portrait palette and openly reconstructed generated names. It asserts neither
a modern linguistic ancestry nor the identity of settled county inhabitants.
`hunnic_court_practices_451` is available only in this bookmark. It has no
universal head, holy-war entitlement, raiding tradition or religious profession
path. Its three-spouse male household capacity is a bounded simulation choice,
not an attested statutory maximum or a complete account of court households.
It supplies no personal confession for a named Gepid or Alan principal.

The existing mobile-government lexicon describes member followings and their
council through the normal layered resolver. Portraits use the
existing late-antique wardrobe of tunics and mantles. The supplemental ambition
list is empty: a date declaration confers no foundation or territorial reward.

The Salian and Rhenish Frankish, Basque, Cantabrian, Armorican and Alamannic courts explicitly
use `regnum`, representing settled local authority rather than a mobile host.
Rome's opening see names Leo I through the ordinary local-institution owner.
Constantinople uses the broad Nicene presentation with Anatolius; Alexandria
retains Dioscorus. No autumn council result or new religious jurisdiction is
installed in spring. The local see owner supplies the actual religious-head
pointers; an additional direct actor would duplicate the incumbent.
Its dated `constantinople_nicene_451` office keeps this local Nicene see separate
from the shared Chalcedonian central office; the institution and physical seat
remain `constantinople_see` and Constantinople.
Institutional appointments and spiritual succession retain their existing
transaction and save owners.

`hunnic_coalition_451` opens countyless at Sirmium, using the independent elected
coalition contract. Attila binds once as its incumbent principal; Valamir binds
once as the qualified reconstructed Gothic principal. The Gepid contingent uses
an ordinary generated representative, with no named Ardaric or asserted personal
confession. The opening does not offer a named Gepid start. Ages are approximate
gameplay seeds, and the empty compact household adds no invented kin.
Attila's court-practices identity and Valamir's qualified Homoian convention
are simulation presentations, not recovered personal confessions. The personal
faith evidence for Attila, Valamir, Ardaric and Sangiban remains incomplete.

The separate Sirmium custodian grants ninety days of previously accepted access
for all 1,300 people, without recruitment, political sponsorship or a new payment.
The actual local holder, living authority chain and population-derived capacity
are rechecked by the canonical opening preflight. This is a logistical simulation
concession, not an attested treaty or an award of Sirmium to the coalition.

The one-time opening allocates 1,170 food units, 240 fighters and 60 additional
public coin through the existing owners. The muster partitions into ninety
archers, sixty cavalry and ninety levy; each contingent stays within its pledge.
The supply owner puts at most seventy-two food units on the host and leaves at
least 1,098 in camp stock. At the declared 0.01 food per person per day, these
opening stores represent ninety aggregate base-ration days. Actual field use,
grazing, purchases and travel costs determine the live runway. The thirty-day
field target is bounded by the host's normal carrying capacity; later supply
requires actual co-location, access and conserved stock. No private start purse
is granted by these public allocations.

`danubian_coalition_451` supplies the mobile policy. Its three member
pools have 600, 400 and 300 people, weights 4, 3 and 2, and recruitment shares
of 0.25, 0.20 and 0.15. These are game-scale quantities rather than an army census.
The supply policy targets thirty days, retains a public cash reserve of 20 and
limits replacement to thirty fighters per review, using existing levy, archer
and cavalry classes. No later horse-archer equipment is substituted.

The profile explicitly bounds demands, raids, service templates, scarcity,
idle support loss, Standing effects, distribution and departure thresholds.
Sirmium and Mursa are named provisioning grounds with reciprocal eight-day
minimum journeys, finite capacity, off-season yield and separately priced
ninety-day access terms. Spring/summer and autumn/winter suitability are
simulation choices, not a reconstructed itinerary. The canonical climate,
route, shared depletion ledger, outside consent, public account and actual camp
stock remain authoritative. The ground access prices are offers for future
review; they do not renew the opening concession or grant access to Mursa.

`senior_command_451` defines a finite senior field-service mandate with command,
service negotiation and payment, and appointment proposals. It uses court
Standing, a 360-day cap, reaffirmation on succession and a ninety-day dismissal
response. Conditional acclamation retains ordinary independent-source and force
requirements. The field-command definition creates no commissioned
host and grants no county or private estate. An effective host appointment must bind
the original person and an actual court force through the ordinary authority
owner before any delegated operation can succeed.

Aetius's separate `senior_service_451` warrant is effective at boot for western
court business: service negotiation, public payment and third-person appointment
proposals. It has the same favor threshold, 360-day tenure and reaffirmation rule,
with no Ban or field-command power. His honorary `patricius` appointment adds no
governing power. The court retains Valentinian and its own public account;
Aetius receives no private purse, estate, county or duplicated character.
`opening.actors.aetius_451.ref` names `royal_court_person`, realm `italy` and
member `aetius`, binding the original authored court member. The field-command
commission needs the story's real opening host. Active federate service likewise
requires the story's declared host and consensual contracts; neither a warrant
nor the availability of a contract template implies an active service agreement.

The `royal_court_person` opening reference works equally in native declarations
and ordinary JSON bookmarks. Its only fields are `kind`, `realmId` and `memberId`;
the named member must exist and be living in that bookmark's authored court.
Profile overrides and missing/dead members reject during pure candidate review.
Fresh initialization uses the canonical royal-member materializer and rejects
duplicate actor bindings. Restore checks the saved original character identity
without creating another person or replaying appointments. Hosted access also
checks its original custodian by the canonical opening ruler identity, including
generated rulers, rather than comparing personal names.

The Sasanian realm explicitly chooses `sasanian_court_451` and
`sasanian_provinces_451`. The former retains four generated provincial interests
at Ray, Ecbatana, Tushpa and Dvin, and the generated Ctesiphon governor, using the
existing actor and family identities. No 476 ruler or later named magnate is
introduced. The latter explicitly declares the existing shahrab, revenue and
host offices, public receipt shares, finite terms and recognition policy.
These remain mixed provincial authority and royal recognition, with Armenian
communities, household faith and private property owned separately.

These are configurations of existing capabilities. Their existing technology
decisions apply; the content adds no independently gateable mechanic or save
schema. The shared bookmark validation specification registers literal fixture
checks for the dated identities, policies, offices and local corrections, while
retaining its canonical world, earlier-date fingerprint and save-replay checks.
Additive cases reject malformed bindings, openings, policy groups, mandates,
profiles and identities without candidate, live-state, storage, RNG or UID
mutation. The native camp journey checks actual daily provisions and repeated
restore with a generated custodian. The court journey commits a real appointment
proposal under the original Aetius warrant, and a separate ordinary JSON world
binds its own court clerk through the same reference and repeated restore.
