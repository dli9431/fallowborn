Administrative monarchy

Scope and consumers

Additional dated consumers use umayyad_711, byzantine_711, byzantine_867, abbasid_867 and byzantine_1066 through fresh world revisions. The original 800 profiles below remain independent. See docs/research/government-rollout.md for evidence, policy differences and pending courts; these assignments use the existing contracts without changing old saved forms or public-service records.

administrative_monarchy governs populated counties through the sovereign's direct holder and revocable public commissions. It does not generate hereditary counts to fill an empty jurisdiction. A commission grants its declared county courts/receipts or exact existing host command; it grants no settlement property, household estate, enterprise, hereditary county or sovereign accession. Independently declared subordinate courts retain their own realm records and local holders.

Fresh 800 starts select abbasid_800 for the Abbasid court and byzantine_800 for the Byzantine court. Other administrative consumers are explicitly assigned by the dated rollout above. Restored campaigns retain their saved forms. public_court is the reusable fictional profile and the form's default. Profiles are selected only by explicit data or saved administrationProfileId, never by religion, language, imperial title or an engine historical-id branch. Original 800 scope and evidence are in docs/research/administrative-monarchy.md.

Interfaces and canonical records

The form names every dispatch slot. public_offices holders use direct assignment, sovereign conquest and sovereign vacancy continuity. public_receipts and public_service compose the existing tax, equipment, host and upkeep calculations while removing automatic hereditary-vassal contributions. Appointed officers replace generated feudal council seats; appointed_council intentionally returns no feudal Council seat catalogue. territorial_diplomacy retains ordinary war/peace law. public_administration AI includes the common realm decision policy and reviewed office work. Delegated Consent uses the existing authority facade. Display words belong in the government lexicon layer.

An authority.appointments row remains the only appointment record. Optional publicTerms schema 1 stores kind (salary or revenue_service), source {kind:county_tax, provinceId}, share, salary, serviceShare, confirmation (continues or reaffirm), remittance, arrears, unpaid, lastPeriod and last. Existing holder, institutionId, appointerGeneration, scope, term and lifecycle fields remain authoritative. county_tax identifies the county's existing collectible public receipts, including its public tolls and national receipts; it is not an independently simulated harvest. Private delegated settlement property contributes only its existing dues, never its private gross income. publicOfficeRows returns detached projections, and publicAppointmentEffective checks live/free incumbency, current institution, source possession, exact scope, host survival, term and declared confirmation.

publicAppointmentQuote and publicOfficeQuote are pure reviews. publicAppoint and publicOfficeApply recompute the full review before writing. Quotes include qualifications, retained prestige for a resident petition, productive source, available active treasury, incumbent capacity, assigned shares, exact scope, ruler generation and current parties. One person holds at most two commissions in one court. Each office/scope has one current incumbent, and all unexpired active/contested assignments together reserve no more than 100 percent of one source. The court pays the appointment fee once. Rejected or stale reviews spend nothing and allocate no authority id. Trusted low-level authority APIs remain available for authored content.

Continuity and enforcement

Abbasid commissions continue across sovereign succession for their stated personal term; Byzantine commissions require the new sovereign's confirmation. Unconfirmed terms become ineffective immediately, then contested at seasonal cleanup. A county without an effective officer remains inhabited and administered by its sovereign. Conquest, death, captivity, expiry, vanished host and lost source remove effective public power. Dismissal ends jurisdiction and future pay while preserving private property and any recoverable arrears. Confirmation restores only a still-valid scope without displacing another incumbent; it cannot cancel debt or revive an expired term. Replacement prunes unreferenced ended commissions, but retains ended records with real arrears or legal evidence.

Source accounting and military service

The ordinary treasury season credits public receipts once, settles ordinary expenses and military accrual, then pays public remuneration from that same available purse. Player-court pay occurs after the existing player income credit and before household necessities. Each assignment settles at most once per 90-day period, starting in the period after appointment. Payment never exceeds its share of the actual source or the available treasury. salary also caps payment at its declared stipend; revenue_service pays its declared source share. Officers receive actual player cash, an existing ruler's treasury or ordinary character gold. A missing/inactive payer cannot mint pay. Household income/budget views project this existing transfer; they never perform it.

Local remuneration precedes a governor's remittance of the remaining receipts. Full remittance leaves the remainder in the same treasury; it is bookkeeping, not a second credit. A holder may remit 50 to 100 percent. Actual withheld cash moves from the treasury to the officer and becomes recoverable arrears. An audit restores full remittance and contests a commission with arrears. Repayment debits the officer's real purse and credits the court once; separate confirmation restores public powers. Unfunded remuneration records the latest bounded obligation rather than issuing cash or compounding an invented debt. The last-period assessment is a compact source/payment summary, not a growing transaction log. Multiple offices share one source remainder, so their explanatory remitted fields must not be added together.

The Abbasid salary profile leaves the existing paid-army baseline available. The Byzantine profile reserves half the county's levy capacity for an effective funded commission; the fictional revenue/service profile reserves 35 percent. Existing population, equipment, recruitment, supply and upkeep remain authoritative. Unpaid remuneration, outstanding arrears or ineffective commissions remove that commissioned share. Funding does not create a host, men, equipment or conquest; an actual military commission commands one already-existing host. These percentages, stipends, fees and terms are gameplay abstractions rather than historical measurements.

Recognition and accession

government_recognition.js owns the reusable reviewed recognition interface: members, candidates, nominationQuote/nominate, supportQuote/applySupport, accessionQuote/resolve. Membership is projected from the court and declared interests held by living effective public officers, with actual principal, scope, legal basis and weight. Saved authority.governments recognition stores only the bounded current principal/candidate/generation votes. The court's designation lives in the existing compact succession tree. A sovereign cannot vote for other principals or silently override divided recognition.

designation selects an eligible designated claimant, with a declared eligible fallback; dynastic selects the eligible frontier's first candidate. recognition requires more than the declared share of all current constituency weight. Ties and missing eligible candidates preserve a dated publicVacancy record and local administration. Public succession suppresses the default generated-collateral repair, so an empty slate does not invent a new heir. Minority is profile data: Abbasid designation permits it, Byzantine and fictional recognition require an adult. Adult public officers can enter a bounded eight-person recognition slate where officeCandidates permits; admission adds a compact candidate/member link without father, mother or invented ancestry. Departed public candidates lose their eligibility, and later nominations prune inactive slate entries.

For an AI court, the reviewed outcome passes through the original advanceRealmSuccession transaction exactly once. That transaction installs the selected person, updates generation, handles existing life/title and dynasty effects, and revalidates the canonical religious-head record. Political accession does not elect a separate caliph or award the head office to an ineligible person. A lawful political female ruler can leave a male-restricted religious office vacant. Stale support and accession reviews cannot replay a transition. The shared recognition interface is available to later forms; this change does not implement another monarchy form.

Player crown and household continuity

Absorbing a public crown rekeys its government profile, commissions and eligible religious-head binding to the player institution before the old realm is retired. The player uses the existing household heir review plus admitted living public officers, with character ids rather than a second compact royal tree. A recognized household heir inherits the crown through the existing household transition, advancing the ruler generation once. Another recognized candidate receives the public realm through the existing abdication transaction while private household property continues separately. Voluntary retirement requires a recognized successor; death without recognition leaves the public court vacant under its existing administration. Commissions follow their declared continuity or confirmation rule. The existing abdication cash convention remains: household cash stays with the household, and the independent court subsequently earns its own receipts.

Public references keep officeholders and supported candidates reachable during character compaction. Save restoration skips public court succession repair and materialization, leaving dated vacancies and recognition untouched until normal play resumes.

Religious institutions and public service

PublicAppointmentQuote/publicAppoint and authorityAppoint retain public and judicial authority in authority.appointments. A qualified religious-community member can receive a commission through the ordinary public contract, but it neither admits or promotes them spiritually nor rewrites their primary faith, career, affiliation, local membership or canonical spiritual head. Conversely, a religious role supplies no public commission, productive source, salary, county title or jurisdiction. Existing finance, occupation and public qualification checks remain in force. tests/e2e/specs/religious-institutions.spec.js covers both a paid public commission and a separate judicial mandate for a headless communal-school member.

Careers, AI and presentation

All eight saved station bands remain. Personal freedom, wealth, service and effective office support the first standing promotions. Public commissions then supply scoped civil or military powers and remuneration independently of station. Personal standing does not buy a hereditary county or supreme office; sovereign bands belong to actual accession and authored starting authority. Household/property succession keeps its existing owner. Dismissal immediately removes public powers from ongoing justice/command permission queries and stops later salary; it does not seize an enterprise or stop an unrelated private project.

The existing authority seasonal scheduler calls publicAdministrationAI. It audits breached remittance, repays from a real officer purse where affordable, removes unresolved commissions and confirms eligible terms. It appoints at most one existing local adult per court per season, within that court's productive jurisdiction, through the same review/apply path and the court's own treasury. Vacancies never generate a feudal count. Recognition AI selects eligible candidates using declared interests and existing skills while preserving valid principals' recorded choices. No new scheduler or AI money source is introduced.

Public administration is a scoped Deeds sheet. It shows the court/profile, treasury, current commitments, office catalogue, source receipts, powers, qualification, term, salary/share, effective status, private property distinction, remittance, arrears and recognition policy. Every mutation has a second concrete review and stale rejection. Shared review cards, person cards, details disclosure and modal navigation retain selectors, open disclosure, scroll and focus on return. Chrome uses FB.T and structured office/profile names use dataText. Durable news uses a message descriptor. Both standard and CrazyGames editions share the mechanics and use their existing distribution-specific title, storage and language behavior.

Save and technology decisions

Public candidate validation accepts only the canonical royal character/member links omitted by save compaction, before inflation or live-state adoption. Explicit wrong links, missing characters and duplicate candidate ids still fail validation. Restoring these links creates no people or ancestry and consumes no RNG or uid.

Optional schema-1 publicTerms, administrationProfileId, recognition, publicVacancy, lastAccession and compact publicCandidateIds are reviewed save-v4 additions inside existing canonical ledgers. Detached validation rejects unknown profiles, malformed amounts/sources, duplicate scope assignments, overlapping shares, invalid support principals/candidates and malformed vacancy/accession records before adoption. Restore never seeds a profile from revised bookmark content, appoints officers, funds pay, resolves a vacancy or reruns an opening reward. Resolution/projection caches and review quotes stay transient. Existing subsystem and envelope versions remain unchanged; schema 1 belongs to the optional assignment record.

Technology impact is none independently for public_service_appointments, public_fiscal_assignments, public_military_obligations and public_sovereign_recognition. Baseline personal service, assessment, source assignment, remittance, audit and recognition have no credible research prerequisite. Participants, jurisdiction, consent and actual resources constrain them. Existing equipment and advanced military technology remain in force; no accounting/logistics hard gate is invented.
