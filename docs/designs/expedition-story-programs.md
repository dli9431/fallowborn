Declaration

Persistence

The optional version-1 state.stories[id] record has exactly version, definitionKey, preparationId, realmId, commandId, programId, actorId, realmGeneration, openedTurn, endTurn, closedTurn, reason, revision, status, lastActionTurn and formation. definitionKey is the exact JSON representation of the declaration and effective mobile definition. openedTurn is zero, endTurn equals termDays, revision is an integer 0-1000000000 and lastActionTurn is -1 or an actual integer turn before endTurn, never future. Original actorId and realmGeneration match the required parent; ids and clocks cannot retarget. formation is initially null, then exactly {migrationId,hostId,leaderGeneration} matching the parent's original formed receipt. It is neither a copied host nor a second formation receipt.

status is preparing, active, paused or closed. Active has null reason; paused has manual or authority; closed has expired, preparation_closed, stopped, refused or complete. closedTurn is null until closure, then the actual nonfuture turn. Closed records have positive revision; formed closed records require terminal canonical intent and unformed closed records require a closed preparation. Missing required records, erased stories, unknown fields/versions, foreign original identities, forged formation references or active-state downgrades to preparing reject detached restore. A truthful stale current-authority/manual projection is accepted for next live reconciliation, without changing it on restore. Formation's detached atomic staging alone can validate the temporary preparing-to-formed transition before publication. The existing envelope and mobile schemas retain their meanings.
