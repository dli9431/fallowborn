Spring 451: Italy, Africa and Dalmatia

The hidden date's italy_africa_dalmatia regional source corrects the western
court and the African division without changing the shared geography. All 71
physical rows, settlement slots, development values and resident communities
remain present. Public ownership changes do not migrate residents, confiscate
private property, create estates or replay a treaty payment. Other dates and
their retained worlds remain independent.

Western government

The existing italy identity represents Valentinian III's western government,
with Ravenna as an institutional opening anchor. Its Roman/Nicene governing
identity and roman_civic form remain separate from its residents. The ruler's
birth year is 419. Licinia Eudoxia, Eudocia and Placidia form the declared living
imperial family; no living Galla Placidia or invented male heir is supplied.
Eudoxia's 422 and the daughters' 439/440 birth years are approximate gameplay
seeds, and Ravenna is a household placement convention, not a verified spring
itinerary. Aetius is a separate, unrelated court member, about sixty, with no
invented spouse or children. Membership grants neither an imperial throne nor
a military command. Dated office, mandate and player-start declarations belong
to the separate government and start sources.

The seven inherited Dalmatian-block rows (Kotor, Knin, Split, Zadar, Zagreb, Sisak and Bosna)
use the western public owner. The independent Nepos court is absent. Bosna's
anonymous civil appointment refers to that same western government. The later
Italian federate host, Odoacer, Orestes and his honorary appointment are absent.
The copied Basilius family and Simplicius identity are removed; ordinary Roman
civic and clerical roles remain anonymous. No 476 pay or land dispute is seeded.

African division and islands

The existing aghlabids identity denotes Gaiseric's Carthage-centered Vandal
kingdom, a settled regnum with Vandal/Homoian governing identity and distinct
Roman/Nicene residents. Gaiseric's birth seed is approximately 389, not the
scaffold's age-shifted 364. His authored household is explicitly empty: no
later captive Eudocia marriage, Hilderic or unsourced additional dynasty is
installed, and no generated starting spouse or children fill that omission.

Carthage and the Byzacena anchor at Kairouan remain Vandal-held. Tripoli and
Sirte represent retained Roman Tripolitania. Tlemcen, Tahert, Algiers and Oran
represent the retained Mauretanian provinces under the western owner; the later
independent Altava court is removed. Tangier is the limited western Tingitanian
coastal anchor. Sicily's Palermo, Messina and Syracuse, Sardinia's Cagliari and
Sassari, and Corsica's Ajaccio use the western owner rather than permanent
Vandal ownership of entire islands. These are coarse regional choices, not
claims that every coast was secure against raiding.
