Religious activities and patronage

Ownership and declarations

The optional religiousActivities and religiousPatronage catalogues declare accepted consumers. Campaign and mod authors can add their own. Shared strategies make no new historical destination, institution, legal rule or calendar claim. Travel remains player.travel, property remains abbeys.houses through the local institution's abbeyId, and outreach remains the existing population communityProject. Primary faith, offices, community membership, standing ranks and rite performance remain separate.

Activity profiles declare name, period, accepted openings, faithIds, affiliationIds, roleIds, institutionId, mode, purpose, siteId, payer, cost, reserve, duration, calendar, interruption, recipient, milestone, reward, legacy and service. Empty openings explicitly permits the declared year interval in any retained campaign; a nonempty list matches both start id and exact revision. Empty eligibility lists impose no additional requirement. Role requirements use qualified active membership and actual location. No missing field inherits a Catholic vocation or monastic rule. Calendar any_day is the only supported calendar; a seasonal Hajj shortcut is rejected. Calendar-dependent rites need a separately reviewed strategy before authoring.

Journey mode adapts an existing authored travel site and purpose, charges the existing route/supplies quote, and retains travel eligibility, encounters, return and cancellation. It requires zero additional cost, duration and reward. The ordinary capstone owns the existing reward. An attached journey declaration records journeyCompleted:true and performed:false; arrival never attests a specific rite. Existing pilgrim traits, religiousRanks and travelHistory suppress an equivalent new milestone without inventing performance. New activity capstones carry the journey start turn and reject stale resolutions before event effects. Existing unwrapped journeys retain their behavior.

Local mode uses a single explicit character commitment, with a frozen declaration, actual location, start/due turns and paid cost. The scheduler supports managed household members and explicitly declared public AI participation, with at most 64 active commitments worldwide. A transient state-keyed index is derived once after restore and visits only those commitments each day; it is not saved. An early completion does nothing. Death, captivity, exile, travel, movement, unavailable institution, lost role or insufficient restricted service funding cancels without a reward. retain_cost is the explicit simulation rule for the initial fee; an unperformed restricted service remains unspent. Costs and reserve use household gold or a proven NPC ruler's active public account, never an inferred institution balance. Household members use the same household purse. Independent or reigning kin cannot spend it. Active personal military obligations also prevent household participation. Public actors use only their own active treasury and receive no household piety or prestige.

Local completion awards bounded participant skills and household piety/prestige once per actor milestone. At most 64 permanent milestone receipts are accepted; they are never evicted to permit another reward. A service additionally names its restricted fund and unit cost. The house must have funded capacity at completion, and the same atomic operation consumes that cost and grants the lesson or other declared result. Ordinary household activity needs no land or institution. No rank, vocation, title, recurring income or appointment is awarded.

Pure review and apply

religiousActivityQuote and religiousPatronageQuote return eligibility, reason codes, payer and funds, actual cost, recipient, duration/interruption or property/service terms, and the dependent facts. They do not initialize travel, land, a house, a project or a receipt. Apply recomputes the proposal and compares the complete result before any payment. Changed funds, actor, date, catalogue revision, property, control, institution, project, route or recipient reject the old proposal. Unsupported declarations and terms fail closed. Review reason codes are protocol; presentation adapters localize them and use dataText for declaration names.

Property and service funds

Patronage profiles declare the same eligibility/date fields, payer, reserve, foundation, funds and permitted outreach policy ids. Foundation names startup consumption, permanent capital, unrestricted treasury and landRequired:true. A foundation needs an active declared local institution with an unused abbeyId, protected worship and an exact unpledged group of the protagonist's freehold plots in the institution's county. Existing manor groups are rejected rather than implicitly changing station. The group leaves household ownership once. Foundation money equals startup consumption plus capital plus treasury. The house is explicitly created only by apply, within the existing twelve-house/one-house-per-county limit. Foundation grants no institution membership, office or public rights.

The same abbeys.houses property ledger stores activityInstitutionId, activityProfileId, activityServices, restrictedFunds and activitySpent. Each declared fund has unitCost and capacity; donations cannot exceed its funded unit ceiling. There is no extra purse on the institution or donor. Gifts and land remain institutional property across sponsor death, succession, withdrawal and canceled activities. No donated plot remains in family rent or inheritance. The initial implementation preserves institutional plots and capital without inventing automatic rent, capital yield or upkeep; services consume explicitly funded units. Future recurring finance must use one property owner and reviewed service rules.

Legacy abbey queries and repair preserve these explicit property records but exclude them from Catholic appointments, residents, automatic succession, allowance and finance. Legacy houses are unchanged and cannot be relabeled into a new tradition by this adapter. Historical family profiles must declare their own service and property rules. This separation does not enable papacy or medieval abbeys in an ancient campaign.

Outreach

Outreach pays one declared service to support an already existing county or settlement faith project. The reviewed target, policy and sponsor must match; the actor must fund their own sponsored project, its current control must be valid, and the receiving institution's jurisdiction must include the county. The fee is consumed once and the project's bounded activitySupport record lasts 360 simulation days. A current support record prevents stacking. The population owner adds balance.religiousOutreachPressure, initially 0.10, to its pressure calculation; existing rate ceilings, resistance, war/occupation factors, holdouts and fractional carry remain authoritative. Loss of sponsor control makes the support ineffective. No progress resets, extra annual tick, direct community replacement, new mission position or ruler conversion occurs. Expired support can be replaced without a growing log.

Technology and integration

religious_activity_participation, religious_activity_patronage and religious_activity_outreach have technology impact none. Presence, admission, lawful property and actual funding govern these baseline activities; no discovery gate is credible. Optional route improvements keep their existing technology rules. Household participation and autonomous decisions separately declare technology impact none. The new optional state is created only by explicit starts; no restore path initializes an activity or endowment.

Household review and profession gates

Deeds, the travel purpose picker and career advancement open the same retained review. The household selector uses managed people rather than faith heads or a monastic profession test. Every proposal discloses participant, destination, payer, cost, reserve, time, expected benefit and interruption. Patronage also shows transferred land, consumed startup, retained capital and treasury, prepaid service capacity and continuing liability. Optional fund names are catalogue text; unnamed funds use stable numbered service labels. Details, scroll and semantic focus survive review, Back, Escape and Continue. Confirmation re-quotes the exact terms; stale proposals spend nothing. Local cancellation is separately reviewed and preserves unconsumed service funds and property.

Profession audit: religiousCareerReview already admits explicit local-role careerIds independently of religiousPaths. The family selectors use the character's actual path, including East Syriac paths. Headless traditions may perform declared lay learning without adopting priest or monk work. This integration grants no career, rank, canonical office, papacy gate or medieval abbey gate. Household and local-institution role restrictions remain authoritative. No broad root default supplies a later house.

Bounded autonomous decisions

Only ai:true public local activities and public patronage participate. Each quarterly invocation considers at most 16 sorted, rotating realm slots, at most 64 quotes and four successful transactions. The saved religiousActivityAI.period prevents repeats. The routine never materializes a ruler, account, foundation or project. Ineligible, warring or insolvent realms skip participation. NPC journeys remain unavailable because canonical travel belongs to the protagonist; AI cannot create a parallel route. Eligible patronage uses the same actual receiving house and existing project checks as household proposals. A separate public patronage declaration can fund the same institution only when each offered service matches the canonical stored capacity and unit cost. The foundation's identity and property remain unchanged.

Retained saves

The optional character commitment and milestone receipts, travel adapter, institution service accounts, project support and quarterly AI period serialize with their canonical owners. Validation uses the saved campaign's institutions and provinces, validates frozen definitions and bounded amounts, and performs no live date or access reevaluation during load. It neither starts an activity nor redirects travel. A restored commitment next completes or interrupts through the normal tick. Existing ranks, old journey history, unwrapped routes and legacy abbey records are untouched. Detached validation rejects malformed dates, destinations, receipts, capacities, restricted balances and future AI periods. The specification includes standard and CrazyGames restore after another bookmark, deterministic continuation, household interruption, public treasury funding and narrow-screen retained review.
