Fort material profiles

The optional fortProfiles root extends existing buildingProfiles without creating another fort building, siege record or material stock ledger. The base table starts empty; data/buildings_circuit_profiles.js adds the optional urban_circuit described below. Each key must identify a buildingProfile whose base has the single fortification role. That profile keeps an empty replace object and the ordinary localized name, desc, applicability, requiresTech and optional inheritance:retain. Source, operation and institution bindings are unsupported for strategic material profiles. Materials are identity and construction eligibility, not stock quantities or claims of real-world defensive strength.

Each fortProfiles entry has exactly material, levels and, when gated, techImpactReview. material is a safe lowercase identifier of at most 80 characters, with no executable meaning. levels is a nonempty map with at most four keys, each the string 1, 2, 3 or 4. Each value is an all-of list of at most sixteen unique known technology ids. The map declares supported canonical levels; it replaces that level's default construction requirement for this selected profile. The buildingProfile's own requirements remain additional. No other fields, level numbers, effects, costs, work amounts, population or siege parameters are accepted. A profile may support an inherited higher level without offering a sequential new-construction route to it.

Any technology named by either declaration requires the fort profile's exact hard technology-impact review, a rationale and a meaningful ordinary-fortification fallback. The ordinary base technology path remains selectable. The generic fort_material_identity review is none: material identity, retained property and project accounting have no discovery gate. The generic mechanism introduces no technology; optional content declares its own knowledge. The timber and masonry import requirements are simulation choices for fictional worlds, not historical level mappings.

Construction and repair

The existing fortProjectStatus projection is the sole sequential level, direct county authority, landed player rank, disputed-county, site, occupancy and invoice owner. New material construction also checks current profile applicability, a living free builder and its exact level requirements. The same profile is retained across upgrades. An unprofiled existing fort cannot acquire a material identity by upgrading or repairing, and an existing selected fort cannot switch identity. A selected fort or its ruins occupy the county's only strategic fort position, including when another settlement is selected. A keep remains an ordinary stronghold and cannot declare fortProfiles or supply another strategic defense bonus.

FB.fortProfiles.quote(state, request) accepts exactly countyId, settlement, profileId, targetLevel, realmId and repair. settlement is an integer from 0 through 7 (the canonical eight-slot bound), targetLevel is 1..4, realmId is the actual county ruler or player, and repair is a boolean. The quote reports the canonical gold invoice, work amount, completion turn, exact requirements, funding, blocker and immutable reviewed context. It reads current physical occupancy, authority, original actor, date, price, exact definition, active mod fingerprint and settlement identity. FB.fortProfiles.commit re-quotes and compares the entire review before starting work. Stale, repeated and failed reviews spend nothing. Leaving or cancelling a review starts no project. Once actually funded, the ordinary fort project cannot be cancelled for a refund; demolition retains its existing destructive behavior.

Players pay the ordinary market-adjusted canonical gold price once. The public AI pays the existing canonical level work amount from fortWorks, with its existing annual accrual, cap and one-project guard, without a new cash payment. Annual planning considers at most the bounded catalogue of material alternatives, chooses the first eligible lexical identity and retains existing capital/frontier/development county priorities. A selected ruin is repaired through the same quote and annual budget. Existing unprofiled forts retain the ordinary planner. There is no second fort treasury, special reserve, new production unit or altered work rate. Default-only worlds preserve the original annual plan and four level numbers.

A funded project retains the selected identity, canonical target, original start/completion turns, payer and actual cost/work record. Expiry of a construction window, loss of knowledge, succession or conquest does not cancel or charge the funded project again. The existing fortificationDay hook alone completes it. Defense, garrison burden, upkeep, minimum siege strength, attrition, siege duration and completion prestige remain the canonical level values. Upgrading retains the current level until completion. A repair uses the ordinary half-price gold invoice, restores the existing canonical ruined level, retains identity and grants no construction prestige. It does not recheck new-construction chronology or technology and creates no people, development, stock or institutional authority.

Inherited inventory and saves

Existing initialization records devGranted:0 and exact inherited provenance; it creates no project or paid receipt. The optional buildingProfile identity retains v:1, id, definition and optional inherited provenance. For a material profile only, definition fingerprints the complete buildingProfile, base building, fortProfiles entry and canonical level table. Old profile fingerprints are unchanged. An active material project additionally stores fortProfileProject with exactly v:1, profileId, fromLevel, targetLevel, startedTurn, completeTurn, repair, realmId, cost and work. All numeric fields are finite; turns and levels are bounded integers. The original payer remains a historical reference after conquest or death. The record must agree exactly with the canonical active project, supported level, duration, repair flag and work amount. Completion and demolition remove only that pending project provenance, retaining the physical profile identity.

Detached v3/v4 platform validation rejects unknown versions, fields, identities, altered definitions, impossible levels/times, missing or orphan project references and duplicate county forts. Matching restore preserves pending, ruined, standing or captured assets without assigning missing profiles, reconstructing removed projects, paying work, reseeding forts or replaying completion prestige. Overdue funded material projects retain the original completion turn rather than being rescheduled by legacy normalization. Envelope and subsystem versions remain unchanged.

Bundled masonry circuit

data/buildings_circuit_profiles.js adds urban_circuit on walls, material masonry, empty replacement values, inheritance retain and supported canonical levels 1 and 2. Both sequential steps require masonry_circuits. New construction requires the late_antique campaign era and an inclusive year from 476 through 649, plus the ordinary direct holder, living free builder, unoccupied site, funding and uncontested-county checks. The empty per-level requirement lists replace only the selected level gates; they do not change the ordinary Ringworks or Castle Towers paths. Level 3 or 4 is not supported by this profile. No direct jump from empty ground to level 2 is supported.

The level-2 project uses the current canonical cost 220 before market pricing, upkeep 4, three seasons, annual AI work 120, garrison 80, siege delay 2, defense 0.10 and completion prestige 25. The level-1 enabling project also pays its own canonical price, duration and award. These are game values, not an archaeological classification. One physical walls record persists across the two projects. Repairs cost the ordinary half invoice, preserve the supported ruined level and original profile, bypass new-construction dates and knowledge, and award no prestige. Funded projects complete through the existing boundary hook after expiry or conquest without a second charge. No keep bonus, second fort, population, materials or historical placement is installed.

masonry_circuit_construction is hard with masonry_circuits for these optional new works. Ordinary fort progression, timber defenses and inherited repairs remain available. Material identity and retained property keep the existing none decision. The reverse technology consumer is the actual construction profile, with no invented building unlock. The independent masonry-circuit-river.json imports the bundled masonry choice in a late-antique fictional estuary with inherited level-2 ruins. masonry-circuit-upland.json imports a later timber choice in another fictional geography, with a 750-850 window and distinct level-one Fortified Camps and level-two Lime Mortar requirements. Both use the same canonical transactions and strict profile/project saves. Historical circuit-to-level and condition assignments remain outside this catalogue.
