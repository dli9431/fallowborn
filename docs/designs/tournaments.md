Scheduled games

Specification and historical basis: docs/plans/tournaments-and-regional-games.md. data/tournaments.js owns bounded terms; js/tournaments.js owns transactions; data/events_tournament.js owns exact-context stories; js/ui_tournaments.js owns the calendar and review sheets. The existing instant invitations remain load-compatible but have never triggers.

Funding and dates

Funding is ceil(max(scale minimum, scale multiplier * reference net recurring seasonal income) / 25) * 25. Local, regional and grand minimums are 500, 2500 and 10000, with multipliers 2, 4 and 8. Reference income is max(0, current projection, average of up to four saved samples). playerCivilianBudget's excludeFocus option removes daily focus receipts without changing the ordinary income ledger; tournament funding also omits military costs. AI realm fiscal rows include tax, tolls, vassal dues and ordinary upkeep/government; delegated barons use their settlement accounts. Reads requiring legacy economic normalization use detached state for player quotes. Cash gifts, loans, asset sales and festival payouts do not become recurring income.

Each edition saves its complete funding split when charged. The four prize amounts are floor(prizeReserve / 2), floor(prizeReserve / 8), floor(prizeReserve / 8), floor(prizeReserve / 4). Services receive floor(total * 0.3); preparation receives the remaining amount. Services cover anonymous workforce contracts plus individually reserved jobs. Contracts use the represented seasonal work rate, seven days / 90, a 2x festival premium and a whole-gold contract bounded from 1 to 12. Specialist commissions add 2 gold at regional games and 4 at grand games within the same 12-gold contract bound. Completed daily portions may be fractional, like ordinary wages; the last portion closes the exact contract. Anonymous staff use at most 60% of the service fund over seven days; individually contracted money is excluded from their available funds. Unused service capacity and unawarded prizes return exactly once.

The original funding account is retained for cancellation refunds, including a player household whose protagonist has changed. The host's actual account is debited only after a fresh quote matches the review signature. Signature inputs include the host, venue, programme, date, annual ceiling, funding, income samples, available treasury and occupied slots. No quote consumes RNG or reserves capacity. Starts need 60-180 days' notice, spring or summer, one funded edition per host and at least 360 days between that host's starts. Closing is startTurn + 7, exclusive. Annual intentions save the next opening turn and spending ceiling; they renew at opening minus 60, skip failed years without spending and never reserve slots before payment.

Capacity and political eligibility

Capacity counts settled counties in the active world's de jure kingdom. Nested limits are max(1, floor(N/5)), max(1, floor(N/10)) and max(1, floor(N/15)); grand editions consume all three. Each county has one slot; the world has 64. Counties without a kingdom share two local slots. Geography is read afresh, so conquest cannot create capacity, and a changed world definition can leave existing commitments above the new ceiling without cancelling them.

Political checks walk the character's actual ruling chain and home county chain separately from de jure geography. Ordinary active campaigns, active great holy wars and armed rebellion groups block entry. Holy-war preparation alone, threats and truces do not. Host death, custody, lost venue, lost rank, military service or war also block visitors before the next tick can reconcile the whole edition. Host/venue failure cancels the edition; personal/home-realm failure withdraws only the visitor. All pending tournament context records are removed and their validator independently rejects retained modal copies.

War transitions call reconciliation after registering an ordinary campaign, launching an active holy war or creating an armed revolt. Restoration and daily travel call it again before any movement or tournament payout. The paused player succession chooser retains funded household commitments without advancing them, clearing the predecessor's participation. The completed succession immediately rechecks ownership, age and eligibility. Eligible successors retaining the settlement inherit a funded commitment; personal entries never pass to the heir. Participant escrow marked as belonging to the player household returns to that household on invalidation. Dead or replaced participants cannot resolve predecessor rounds. Annual settings can pass to an eligible venue successor, but their next payment still goes through renewal.

Travel

The tournament purpose is targeted, repeatable and open to free adult tiers 1-7. Tournament travel quotes reuse county routes, transport leg duration and market supply pricing, charging the outward journey and reserving a return. Onward travel begins at the current venue, preserves the household home and charges the newly quoted outward and return total less the old unspent return reserve. It targets only a scheduled edition. Local attendance changes no journey record. Ordinary departure cooldowns and destination stays are untouched; tournament journeys neither consume nor consult them.

Returning consumes the saved reserve once and follows a physical county route. Reconciliation starts wartime return before the ordinary travel engine can cancel the journey. Death, captivity and protagonist replacement retain their normal priority. Exile can hold a traveller before a prohibited next county until entry becomes lawful. Closure leaves three days for another booking or a manual funded return. A still-reachable registered onward edition preserves the return reserve while the visitor reviews the next journey; cancellation starts the return immediately. Already started competitions reject late entry, while spectators and remaining jobs remain open until closure.

Contests and livelihood

Each competition freezes eight named entrants and three rounds. Jousting and wrestling eliminate pairs; archery and performance accumulate scores; melee compares two teams of four over three phases. Abstract local entrants belong to the venue's peaceful field and do not create permanent characters. Circuit contact snapshots can recur and remain bounded to eight, alongside victories, earnings and reputation. Active real host and participant ids protect their characters from court compaction only until closure or withdrawal.

Individual power is relevant skill, plus Brave/Craven adjustment, plus elite worn-equipment bonus bounded to 3, plus (health - 7) * 0.4, plus preparation, minus fatigue * 0.65. Performance averages Diplomacy and Learning. Generated entrants use their saved ability, health adjustment and fatigue. Landholding and armies never appear in this formula. Paired round chance is clamp(0.5 + (own power - opponent power) / 24, 0.08, 0.92); an absent participant forfeits rather than receiving that floor. Missing a round refunds unused entry and forfeit reserves, marks the competition missed, and prevents injury, prize or skill earnings from the missed field. Later arrival still allows in-person social festivities. Scored rounds add a seeded integer from 0 to 12 and clamp the result to zero. Melee uses team-average power in the same probability formula; its winning team shares the purse four ways.

Conservative tactics add -1 power, 1 fatigue and 0.55x injury risk; balanced adds 0, 2 and 1x; aggressive adds 2, 4 and 1.8x. Assisting adds -1 personal power plus 2 team-help power, 2 fatigue and 0.7x injury risk, and is offered only for melee. Preparation is once per round: rest removes up to 3 fatigue, practice gives 1 preparation and 1 fatigue. Injury risk is min(0.6, contest baseline * tactic risk * (1 + fatigue / 10)). The saved severe subset uses the same tactic multiplier and applies six health damage through the ordinary health effects, potentially causing death; ordinary injury applies one. Preview and mutation read the same powers and probabilities. Unanswered player rounds use conservative tactics on the next day; AI-only fields resolve without a modal chain.

One primary activity per edition is retained even after withdrawal. General activities allow adults of either sex. Armed contests and wrestling require men. Elite entry needs Martial 5, Health 5 and worn battle equipment; any competition needs Health 3. A free commoner instead qualifies for sponsored elite equipment access with completed Soldiering training, Martial 8, and an open victory or 40 host Standing. Serfs can watch, work, or enter open games only at home, without modifying customary duties. Specialist jobs retain trained-career and sex restrictions. Performing uses its own scored field; local merchant trading uses the existing travel_trade chance and market stock/price operations with a disclosed 10-gold stake.

Elite fees and monetary forfeit reserves are bounded from 2 to 25 by the edition budget. Fees remain refundable until actual competition; losing a joust pays the disclosed forfeit, never creates imprisonment and never removes heirlooms. Reserved payments use edition-local unique keys. Completed competitions or all seven days of contracted work can award at most one skill increase at closure. Prizes, life-history facts, hosting prestige, local support and circuit earnings settle once. Personal social meetings and universally faith-appropriate gifts require attendance and are limited once per edition. Commission care raises circuit reputation by 2; patron and recruitment introductions grant 3 host Standing; the closing celebration grants 1. Up to three eligible local guests are retained for a hosting edition, with completion granting each 1-8 Standing toward the player host according to incident quality. Guest references are released at closure.

Interfaces and saves

FB.tournaments read methods: get, active, venueName, defaultProgramme, capacity, eligibility, attendance, projection, income, funding, date, dates, quote, participant, entry, roundPreview, travelQuote, list, contextValid and referencesCharacter. Mutations: ensure, book, stopAnnual, enter, prepare, resolveRound, withdraw, cancel, reconcile, samplePlayer, season, tick, depart, returnHome, social and incident. book, enter, depart and resolveRound accept the exact reviewed quote and refuse stale signatures. Payment and simulation mutations alone draw seeded randomness.

state.tournaments.version is an internal additive record version, currently 1; save format remains 3. Records contain nextId, events, annual, samples, starts, summaries, cursors and lastSeason. Events contain the funding-account identity, bounded local guests, frozen funding, remaining prize buckets, service commitments, paid transaction keys, participants, fields/results and incident serials. Completed summaries are bounded to 32; full closed editions are released after the return window, except while their traveller still needs the reference. Each income list is bounded to four samples; obsolete host samples and start records are pruned seasonally.

Queued choices carry tournamentId, tournamentCharId, tournamentTrack, tournamentRound and tournamentKind. scheduled_games_valid validates the exact record before any custom effect. All custom choices register impact preview/report adapters. UI uses the shared modal history, review cards, Details controls, native labeled selectors and disabled-action wrappers. Events is an independent map overlay with screen-space clusters at distant zoom and exact settlement anchors at close zoom; it does not replace political or market state. Child sheets return by rebuilding their parent from current state, so confirmed transactions never leave stale sheets in modal history; ui.md records the navigation and presentation rules.

Technology and distribution

scheduled_games, tournament_circuit_travel, regional_open_games, tournament_sponsorship and festival_livelihoods have technology impact none. tournament_jousting has hard cavalry_lances under the venue sovereign, with open games, melee and company as visible alternatives. Accepted jousts grandfather that permission. Existing careers and equipment production retain their own gates.

All three new scripts are classic local scripts registered in index.html. Offline asset discovery follows those references; existing directory allowlists include the data and engine/UI files. There are no new assets, external requests, build dependencies or private packaged files. Both standard and explicitly flagged CrazyGames builds use the same scheduling engine. New structured labels have extraction support and English fallback; generated catalogs remain integration-owned. Owner-controlled coverage lives in the scheduled-games, scheduled-games-lifecycle and scheduled-games-ui specifications, alongside legacy invitation regressions.
