# Spring 451 story declarations

The hidden spring 451 world attaches `western_objective_451` and
`gallic_relocation_451`. The first is an ordinary aggression campaign for
Aurelianum, offered during 451–452 with a 180-day commitment. It uses the elected
coalition's canonical war review and exact member assent. No route, supplies,
army, victory or land is granted by the declaration. Failure leaves ordinary
war and cohesion consequences with their existing owners. Leadership changes
can interrupt an unfinished campaign; no historical death is scheduled.

After an actual failed coalition campaign, the western court may authorize
relocation from Aurelianum to Parisii during 451–453. Both counties must still be
its possessions and the court must be at peace. Ten percent of the eligible
Gallo-Roman Nicene community is the ceiling, transferred in seasonal batches of
100 through the existing conserved population transaction. These quantities and
the route are simulation choices, not a claim of an attested evacuation. A
player court can decline; authorization is not completed movement.

Personal declarations remain separate from world attachment. The Attila agenda
provides demand, bounded raid, receipt distribution, seasonal arrival at Mursa
and lawful settlement reviews. Proposals require independent member responses;
an outside demand needs actual accepted payment. Defer, stay and departure do
not complete transaction stages. Settlement requires physical presence, land
rights, peace and member assent. Final settlement includes remaining fighters.
The optional opening goals observe actual source evidence and grant no reward.

Three local household graphs describe Orleans civic records, Arles field work
and Toulouse garrison work. They branch on actual local war and observed changes
of sovereign. Selecting work supplies no wages: only actual focus earnings close
the opening. A control incident closes once and routes to a separate work stage,
preventing replay of that incident. No review grants military enlistment, office,
conversion, refuge, free travel or release from estate duties. Protagonist death
or succession interrupts unfinished work. The matching opening profiles observe
paid-work terminal stages without further rewards. These personal graphs are
available for owning start declarations; the generic scaffold farmer remains
unchanged.

The Aurelianum civic household remains held in normal selection. In an
explicitly enabled review, its qualified curialis can choose Keep records
through the household card in Deeds. Closing the review defers the decision.
Selecting or resuming the focus spends no gold or action day. A worked day
that pays private gold at the Aurelianum home completes the story and the
opening goal. Council appointments and the court's relocation decision stay
with their respective owners.

The local war review lets the household resume work even while the war lasts.
An actual change of sovereign takes priority over that review and requires an
explicit decision to resume under the current rule. Closing the sheet leaves
the occurrence pending, and confirming it records the change without moving
any household member. The restored pending and completed stages retain the
ordinary focus and purse without replaying a payment. This uses the existing
`household_story_decisions` and `household_story_work` technology decisions
(none) and the existing curialis qualification. An ordinary JSON example
varies the home, continuity, and decision routes with a Ravenna ledger offer.

All declarations reuse existing capability decisions: `mobile_story_transactions`,
`mobile_campaign_principal_review`, `household_story_decisions` and conserved
civilian migration. Their political, resource and consent gates have no new
technology dependency. No independently gateable engine capability is added.
Literal display tables use existing household, agenda, opening-profile,
campaign and expedition presentation owners. English fallback remains valid.

The Aetius household graph uses the existing `senior_service_451` appointment to
review actual `court_paid_service` consent or accept an actual recorded mandate
loss. Consent is the holder's transaction, not a claim that bilateral service has
begun. Its opening goal observes only those canonical outcomes. Cancelling any review spends nothing.

## Capability holds and start-owned attachment

The following boundaries retain the existing engine contracts. They do not
substitute a calendar event or an acknowledgment for an unsupported outcome.

- **Coalition foundation after leadership loss:**
  `js/story_polity_foundation.js`, lines 25 to 28 and 98 to 119, requires a
  territorial-seat collapse and an authored new ruler. It has no declaration
  contract for a countyless following's member cohesion, leader death or a
  founder selected from its existing principals. That foundation remains on
  hold; these stories create no successor polity or scheduled breakup.
- **Court agendas:** `js/story_ruler_agenda.js`, line 41, requires
  `agenda_priority` in every stage without a mobile transaction completion.
  That acknowledgment advances the personal stage. Court agendas therefore
  remain out of this declaration set. The missing contract is a nonmobile
  transaction stage with costless deferral and completion only from canonical
  source evidence. Ordinary court and mandate controls remain available.
- **Opening federate contracts:** `js/federates.js`, lines 632 to 638, requires
  a compiled `court_settlement` and a host declared by that story.
  `js/story_courts.js`, lines 841 to 876, validates the broader court cast and
  context, while `aiSettlement` at line 259 owns its political settlement
  lifecycle. A neutral `court_settlement` surface is needed to declare existing
  parties, hosts and finite service terms without importing that unrelated
  lifecycle. Opening federate contracts remain on hold; no standing host,
  initial payment or bilateral consent is fabricated here.
- **Expedition automation:** `js/expedition_programs.js`, lines 405 to 413,
  requires `mobile.command` and its authored opening. The elected coalition has
  no such command. A program contract for an elected coalition would need to
  preserve its own leader, member consent, resources and physical movement
  rather than granting command authority. Command-bound expedition automation
  remains on hold; the campaign and seasonal reviews retain their own owners.
- **Personal and refuge attachment:** owning start declarations in
  `data/starts_451.js` supply personal track attachment and any named
  `household_party` and refuge composition. `js/story_household_refuge.js`,
  lines 147 to 169, requires declared parties of the supported type and a party
  reachable through an owning start. The missing composition is that actual
  start-to-party attachment, together with the refuge's existing seat,
  provider and finite terms. These personal graphs do not attach themselves,
  enroll relatives or promise refuge. Start-owned attachment is separate from
  the engine capability holds above.

The focused fixtures exercise malformed declaration rejection and atomicity,
including invalid mobile completion, campaign admission, migration bounds,
household graph routes and mandate references. Together with the shared world
checks, they validate the supported declaration set. They do not establish a
staged run covering victory, defeat, buyoff, settlement and breakup, nor do they
establish personal entry or refuge behavior before the owning starts attach it.
