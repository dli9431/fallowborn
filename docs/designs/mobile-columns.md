Finite column provisioning

An optional profile supplyPolicy now composes with columns. The existing mobile_provisioning technology decision remains none for each actual local purchase and camp transfer, and mobile_column_reorganization remains none for class-aware recovery. Ordinary unit, equipment, travel, access and resource gates remain binding. No remount model or new public account is introduced.

The pure columnSupplyPlan and shared supplySnapshot count camp civilians and resting fighters separately from all actual columns. Camp procurement includes only halted colocated column deficits; remote food never contributes to camp stock. Each host keeps its ration modifiers and configured carryDays capacity. Opening allocations must fit that capacity. Retained excess stock after losses or reorganization is conserved and blocks further replenishment until space exists.

columnSupplyReview and reviewColumns expose policy-tagged trade, load and unload actions. A halted remote column buys whole food units only at its actual market, bounded by unmet carrying target, finite stock, current lawful access and affordable public funds above cashReserve. Owning and holding the county removes payment but still withdraws real market stock. Hostile occupation alone grants no procurement right. Loading and unloading require actual camp reunion. Loading preserves one day of current camp rations and may transfer fractional food; unloading returns the actual carried stock. The overall commander or the exact living column commander may request these supply actions under the trade permission. Stale stock, ownership, access, position, command, policy or funds require a new review.

replacement.targetMen is a command-wide deployed target, never a target per column. The planner tries declared columns in order, then unitIds in order, selecting the first eligible local transaction. All actual deployments count against each living member pledge. A retired slot requires the ordinary minimum. Current camp rations, cashReserve, maxPerReview and ordinary unit unlocks bound recovery; no remote person or local inhabitant is recruited. Explicit manual recruitment keeps its existing ordinary checks.

At the ordinary daily boundary AI buys for the camp, processes one local transfer or local-market purchase per actual column, then attempts one recovery batch. Every step re-quotes the shared transaction against preceding stock and payment changes. lastDay bounds automatic dispatch, and existing daily army provisioning, consumption, casualties and the shared finite pasture ledger remain authoritative. An opted-in expeditionProgram withholds this automatic procurement/recovery and seasonal mobile AI, including after its terminal disposition; explicit supply steps and manual actions retain the same canonical policy reviews. Its dispatcher revalidates scoped commands and owned routes before movement and never creates a replacement column. Policies add no saved cursor or restore initializer. The independent mobile-column-supply-harbor.json and mobile-column-supply-dune.json imports vary ration coverage, carriage, cash reserves, recovery batch, class preference and command authority; mobile-column-supply.spec.js covers their real imports and supply lifecycle. Program execution is documented in docs/designs/expedition-programs.md.

Column operations

Technology impact mobile_column_orders, mobile_column_reorganization and mobile_column_settlement is none. These capabilities govern existing political authority, conserved people and lawful residence. Existing unit, equipment, route, transport, access, treasury and population gates remain binding; the ordinary single-host path remains available.

The mobile_columns selection adapter reviews exact column identities, current commanders, the overall mandate, hosts, member pools, camp, resources and political state. Both player and AI callers use mobile.reviewColumns and mobile.commit. A column commander may order only that column under the current march permission. The overall commander alone moves the camp, assigns an existing eligible member, reorganizes columns, recruits or starts an ordinary war. No automatic replacement follows death or custody. Assignment upgrades the optional saved column set to schema 2; schema 1 retains its original immutable commander meanings.

One selected host can escort the camp from their actual shared locality, through the ordinary army path and court-arrival owner. Other columns keep their own positions and orders. Field orders leave the camp behind. The escort stops carrying it when explicitly released or retired. Changing authority or peaceful passage stops every affected route before the next army march. Raw map orders and generic split/merge shortcuts remain unavailable; the shared command sheet exposes the reviewed operations.

Split moves an exact eligible unit count and a deterministic integer demographic partition into a retired declared column slot at the source locality, retaining its original host id. It transfers proportional carried food and muster capacity without recruiting. Merge requires stationary hosts at the same locality with the same war commitment and pools their exact units, demographic rosters and food before retiring the emptied source. Reused slots do not create additional columns. Demuster requires actual camp reunion and completed military/service commitments. A forced remote disband records surviving people as dispersed and loses their carried stock; it cannot teleport them or food to camp. Canonical army casualties remain the sole death allocator.

Recruitment explicitly selects an existing normal unit class and uses only currently pledged living member pools at camp, with the ordinary unit and treasury gates. A retired slot needs at least the normal minimum to reform. Recovery creates no replacement people and cannot replenish a remote host. Optional demand policies and automatic class selection use the column-aware supply transactions above. Partial civilian settlement uses the ordinary population transaction while the camp is stationary; final settlement requires every column to have returned and demustered first. Each independently active war and each remote host otherwise remains intact.

Final settlement resolves the living commander before closing the active mobile record and reuses that person for the ordinary territorial ruler binding. The prior mandate remains archived with its original generation and cannot issue new commands; a newer territorial ruler generation does not invalidate that inactive provenance. Matching restore creates no replacement person or command.

Saved schema 2 retains the same exact column entry fields and original host identities, allowing an explicitly assigned commander from the bound opening members. At most one active host carries the camp, at its actual locality. Matching restore validates active and retired identities without creating a host, selecting a person, moving the camp or restoring resources. mobile-column-operations.spec.js exercises both independently configured ordinary imported worlds and the shared transactions.

An optional column declaration divides an existing authored command opening into two to four field hosts. It reuses the opening's exact member and unit-class muster, people and food. The declaration supplies no additional population, treasury, equipment, local access or historical campaign. Omitting it preserves the existing single-host path and schemas.

The mobile row owns a bounded set of stable column ids, canonical host references, original commander references and active or retired dispositions. Ordinary armies own deployed headcounts, demographic rosters, unit counts, positions and carried food. The migration remains the sole owner of the civilian court position. Member civilian and resting pools, camp stock and the public account keep their existing owners. There is no copied total, second camp or column treasury.

Declaration and creation

mobile.columns has schema 1 and an entries list. Each entry supplies id, commanderMemberId, muster and food. Column ids are distinct lowercase slugs of at most 48 characters. Each commander names a different member with an explicit opening principal binding. Every opening member/class fighter is allocated exactly once across the column musters. Every column has at least the existing army minimum; food is finite, nonnegative, within its configured carryDays capacity (thirty days by default) and withdrawn from the declared opening stock. Normal opening class eligibility still applies. The four-column bound limits records and work; it is not a historical force-size claim or a change to ordinary army caps.

The first host keeps following_<realmId>; additional hosts use column_<realmId>_<columnId>. Generated ids must fit 160 characters and be unique across the complete opening. All hosts start at the existing camp origin. This schema does not declare remote field placement. It accepts either baseline provisioning or the optional column-aware supplyPolicy described above. Pure detached validation rejects the complete candidate on duplicate identities, unbound commanders, excessive entries, incompatible commands, unallocated or overallocated fighters and impossible food sums.

Fresh live preflight checks the opening at turn zero, actual actor eligibility, military technology and collisions with existing armies or migrations before initialization. The existing initializer withdraws the aggregate muster once. The column initializer repartitions those deployed people and their food into ordinary hosts without another recruitment, identity allocation, treasury credit or resource grant. Repeated initialization is inert. Restore never calls this initializer.

Authority and presentation

columnSnapshot projects the camp and each actual host without mutation. columnAuthority accepts a character actor with the exact column command or the effective overall command. A living, available and allegiant commander and current overall mandate are necessary; a deputy's scope does not extend to another column. Death, custody, withdrawal of allegiance or issuer expiry suspend the affected authority without changing its original identity or selecting a successor.

Opening ownership supplies read-only scope. The shared command sheet shows each numbered column's actual commander, position, carried food and active or suspended authority alongside the separate camp stock. Existing keyboard, mobile, Back, disclosure, scroll and focus behavior is retained. One-host recruitment, demuster, camp movement, field orders, departure, replacement and service shortcuts stay blocked for opted-in columns. Raw map orders likewise remain unavailable. The reviewed column transactions above own movement and reorganization; ordinary settlement observes the additional final-reunion restriction. Existing provisioning, hostile combat and demographic accounting retain their canonical owners.

Persistence and conservation

The optional mobile.columns saved object has schema 1 or 2 and entries containing exactly id, hostId, commanderId and status. The enclosing command row remains schema 3. Each active reference resolves exactly one ordinary mobile host with the matching realm and migration, a valid physical county, bounded food and a known class composition. At most one host carries the camp. Retired references have no surviving host. The set retains the original declared order and host identities after death or retirement; schema 1 also retains the original actor bindings. The primary migration reference remains the first canonical host id even after its retirement.

Existing validation reconciles each roster with its host men and units and the complete following with its initial, arrivals, dead, settled and dispersed account. Column validation additionally rejects duplicate hosts, missing active hosts, undeclared extra hosts, forged commanders, unknown classes, impossible carried stock and incompatible legacy markers before restore adopts state. It creates no missing host, replenishes no stock and repairs no record. Normal disband retires the corresponding identity; explicit court extinction retires the complete set. Exact imported-content fingerprints cover the complete declaration.

Technology impact mobile_column_ownership is none. These are allocations of already eligible forces under current political authority. Existing class, equipment, transport, resource and outside-consent constraints remain. Single-host openings remain the default fallback.
