Bounded mobile opening columns

An optional column declaration divides an existing authored command opening into two to four field hosts. It reuses the opening's exact member and unit-class muster, people and food. The declaration supplies no additional population, treasury, equipment, local access or historical campaign. Omitting it preserves the existing single-host path and schemas.

The mobile row owns a bounded set of stable column ids, canonical host references, original commander references and active or retired dispositions. Ordinary armies own deployed headcounts, demographic rosters, unit counts, positions and carried food. The migration remains the sole owner of the civilian court position. Member civilian and resting pools, camp stock and the public account keep their existing owners. There is no copied total, second camp or column treasury.

Declaration and creation

mobile.columns has schema 1 and an entries list. Each entry supplies id, commanderMemberId, muster and food. Column ids are distinct lowercase slugs of at most 48 characters. Each commander names a different member with an explicit opening principal binding. Every opening member/class fighter is allocated exactly once across the column musters. Every column has at least the existing army minimum; food is finite, nonnegative, within its existing thirty-day carrying capacity and withdrawn from the declared opening stock. Normal opening class eligibility still applies. The four-column bound limits records and work; it is not a historical force-size claim or a change to ordinary army caps.

The first host keeps following_<realmId>; additional hosts use column_<realmId>_<columnId>. Generated ids must fit 160 characters and be unique across the complete opening. All hosts start at the existing camp origin. This schema does not declare remote field placement. It requires baseline provisioning profiles; an optional supplyPolicy cannot be combined with columns until a column-aware supply owner supports it. Pure detached validation rejects the complete candidate on duplicate identities, unbound commanders, excessive entries, incompatible commands, unallocated or overallocated fighters and impossible food sums.

Fresh live preflight checks the opening at turn zero, actual actor eligibility, military technology and collisions with existing armies or migrations before initialization. The existing initializer withdraws the aggregate muster once. The column initializer repartitions those deployed people and their food into ordinary hosts without another recruitment, identity allocation, treasury credit or resource grant. Repeated initialization is inert. Restore never calls this initializer.

Authority and presentation

columnSnapshot projects the camp and each actual host without mutation. columnAuthority accepts a character actor with the exact column command or the effective overall command. A living, available and allegiant commander and current overall mandate are necessary; a deputy's scope does not extend to another column. Death, custody, withdrawal of allegiance or issuer expiry suspend the affected authority without changing its original identity or selecting a successor.

This contract supplies opening ownership and read-only scope. The shared command sheet shows each numbered column's actual commander, position, carried food and active or suspended authority alongside the separate camp stock. Existing keyboard, mobile, Back, disclosure, scroll and focus behavior is retained. One-host recruitment, demuster, camp movement, field orders, departure, replacement, settlement and service shortcuts are blocked for opted-in columns. Raw host orders and map control are likewise unavailable. Column-specific split, merge, movement, loss/recovery policy, reunion and final settlement require their own transactions. Existing provisioning, hostile combat and demographic accounting retain their canonical owners; this capability does not declare an expedition operational.

Persistence and conservation

The optional mobile.columns saved object has schema 1 and entries containing exactly id, hostId, commanderId and status. The enclosing command row remains schema 3. Each active reference resolves exactly one ordinary mobile host with the matching realm and migration, no civilian court carriage, a valid physical county, bounded food and a known class composition. Retired references have no surviving host. The set retains the original declared order and actor bindings after death or retirement. The primary migration reference remains the first canonical host id even after its retirement.

Existing validation reconciles each roster with its host men and units and the complete following with its initial, arrivals, dead, settled and dispersed account. Column validation additionally rejects duplicate hosts, missing active hosts, undeclared extra hosts, forged commanders, unknown classes, impossible carried stock and incompatible legacy markers before restore adopts state. It creates no missing host, replenishes no stock and repairs no record. Normal disband retires the corresponding identity; explicit court extinction retires the complete set. Exact imported-content fingerprints cover the complete declaration.

Technology impact mobile_column_ownership is none. These are allocations of already eligible forces under current political authority. Existing class, equipment, transport, resource and outside-consent constraints remain. Single-host openings remain the default fallback.

mobile-columns-harbor.json and mobile-columns-dune.json are independent ordinary Mods imports. They differ in retained versus externally commissioned authority, commander bindings, profile populations, class allocation and carried stock. mobile-columns.spec.js covers their real import and matching restore, detached and fresh rejection without mutation, scoped authority, old defaults, exact fingerprints, separate camp provisioning and incompatible one-host shortcuts. Device and operational acceptance are separate from this ownership contract.
