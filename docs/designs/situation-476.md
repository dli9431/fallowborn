# The 476 regional campaign

The 476 bookmark is a bounded, playable regional campaign. It uses the stable county seeds and terrain of europe_regional as spatial anchors, with independently authored rulers, direct holders, communities, faiths and settlement layouts. The active region now includes the western and eastern Roman courts, Gaul, Visigothic Iberia, the Suevic northwest, Armorican and Rhenish communities, Dalmatia, the Vandal coast, and Moorish communities. Distant Britain, Scandinavia, the eastern steppe, and other unauthored areas remain uninhabited in this bookmark; their 867 governments must never be relabeled as 476 rulers. Several county anchors stand for broader territories, not fifth-century county borders. Seeded generated rulers represent communities whose 476 ruler is not securely attested; their ruler declarations carry generated:true. The 867 and 1066 bookmarks retain their authored data and ordinary vassal generation. The 476 bookmark declares the late antique era, whose default `regnum` government form keeps each county in its realm's own hand because the feudal vassal generator does not describe the period's civic and military relationships. The bookmark data, not the story, gives the western, Dalmatian and eastern courts the `roman_civic` form and the federate body the `federate_polity` form ([realms](realms.md#eras-and-government-forms)).

The western court begins at Ravenna under Romulus Augustulus, with Orestes holding an exact Italian host command. Odoacer leads a separate, nonterritorial federate polity and host near Ticinum. Nepos holds Dalmatia; Basiliscus holds Constantinople while Zeno is displaced. Syagrius holds the Roman domain from Soissons (laon), Childeric sits at Tournai (ghent), and Chilperic I is senior Burgundian king at Lyon. Gundobad co-rules at Vienne and is the named heir, while Godegisel has a court at Geneva. The Amal Gothic host under Theoderic is nonterritorial in Macedonia, with a separate Thracian Gothic host under Strabo. A generated Gepid ruler holds the Pannonian cells from Sirmium; Rugian pressure north of the Danube is represented by Feletheus in the wien cell, while Noricum remains nominally Italian. The displaced Zeno is a living claimant at the Isaurian court site in ikonion, without county ownership; the site is a regional anchor rather than an identification of his siege fortress. His conditional return requires him and Basiliscus to live, Basiliscus to remain in office, Constantinople to remain eastern, and the realm to be at peace. A new ruler generation then invalidates Basiliscus's reaffirmation-dependent commissions. Backing Zeno supplies a new commission only on his actual return.

Ravenna and Classis, Salona and Spalatum, and Carthage and later Tunis are separate sites or names, not aliases of one physical point. The 476 layout omits Kairouan and uses Sufetula within the retained county polygon. Site coordinates are authoring anchors, with [Salona](https://www.levantineceramics.org/sites/2500-salona), [Carthage](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/37/maps/), and the [Classis port](https://comune.ravenna.it/vivere-il-comune/luoghi/parco-archeologico/antico-porto-di-classe/) checked against published site records. The county grid remains approximate; the names do not imply exact fifth-century boundaries.

The 476 bookmark disables great holy wars. Its bounded map leaves the later Catholic and Sunni sacred targets uninhabited, so their global campaign definitions do not apply to this start. The 867 and 1066 bookmarks keep their normal date and target rules. The 476 population and ruling courts use the authored Nicene, Homoian, Chalcedonian, Miaphysite and traditional faith identities; no Muslim population is placed in this bookmark.

The playable start is a 24-year-old free Gothic/Homoian soldier at Ravenna with 20 game gold and no land or hereditary command. The Deeds situation begins with a delegate choice and an exact-host commission claim. Political advancement uses authority records: an army acclamation supports a field appointment, a paid federate contract binds two sovereign counterparties, civic acceptance is scoped to Ravenna, and an imperial commission names its issuer. The Nicene civic majority and Homoian player household make episcopal mediation a possible condition of civic acceptance. None of these grants automatically transfers ownership, judicial Peace, or revenue.

The opening now also offers a local civic clerk career and a Homoian clerical route. The civic clerk can petition for a scoped judicial appointment from a living western court; the church route requires a living Homoian bishop before Nicene mediation, and uses late antique lay and clerical rank paths rather than the later Catholic Papacy. A living senator holds a separate Rome civic delegate mandate; his consent is required before the federate court can recognize Nepos for the western office. Each route can end without land or office, leaving the household playable.

The negotiated route offers 30 from the western treasury immediately and four seasonal payments of 10 to the federate body. Two of each seasonal payment go to the household; the share is included in the ten. The player may seek Classis for a named veteran, with the grant requoted and confirmed only at ratification. The court's escort follows an ordinary host route to Ticinum, and one separate payment of 5 can be made for maintenance. Ratification requires the live contract, civic consent and court arrival. Refusal can return to pay negotiation or proceed to a contested march. The contest uses the real map host and battle; taking Ravenna can displace the western office without rewriting its population, private holdings or other counties. Defeat and withdrawal leave the household playable.

A ratified Classis award moves the named veteran from Ticinum to Ravenna and records the exact new site in the federate track's saved record; the normal settlement grant transfers only that port holding. A ratified civic bargain establishes saved local Peace and consent rights for Ravenna’s delegate, separately from county ownership. A victorious federate host can affirm its own government or accept Roman civic rule after living civic and Nicene church actors agree. It can leave the western office vacant under an eastern commission or acknowledge a living Nepos; if the western court later recovers Ravenna, a living Romulus can return to that office even after a rival claim was recognized. These are separate decisions. Authority records, rather than result flags alone, hold the mandate and can lapse on succession or loss of scope. If negotiations collapse through death, extinction, loss of the court or a stale contract, the player can leave the dispute and continue as a household. If the player declines involvement, the courts attempt the same paid service and civic transactions without them; a failed bargain can become an ordinary objective war. The outside household takes no service share.

The later history is conditional. Clovis can challenge Syagrius around 486 only if he inherits the Frankish realm and the Roman domain still holds Soissons. Theoderic may receive Zeno’s commission and contest Ravenna in 489–493 only while both rule their courts and the target remains western or federate. An eastern court retaining Constantinople can open African and Italian reconquest campaigns from 533 and 535 against their then-current owners. These are ordinary objective wars; their outcome follows armies, occupation and peace, and a failed or diverted campaign does not transfer a county. Conquest can displace a court to surviving territory; a Gothic settlement in Ravenna grants civic rights only with surviving delegates and a Nicene majority. The 476 bookmark adds a Syracuse–Carthage sea crossing to make the long African route possible on its bounded map. These dates identify opportunities, not scripted replacement of divergent rulers.

The situation runs as two registered stories (stories.md). The bookmark attaches the western court world story, which authors the cast and standing hosts, moves displaced courts, runs Zeno's return, settles the dispute through the courts when the household declines, and runs the later campaigns. The Federate Soldier scenario attaches the federate track, which holds the household's choices and the contest it starts. Each bargain record belongs to whoever made it. An observed 476 world has the world story only, so the courts' settlement there waits on a household that is not present, as before. The Deeds card and decision sheet are the shared story presentation.

All situation facts and outcome flags are serializable under state.stories.western_court_476 and state.stories.federate_track_476; branch saves holding the former state.situation476 split into the two once on load. Quotes are pure and stamped from current actors, treasury, territory, host positions and authority records. Applying a reviewed decision rechecks those facts. News uses durable message descriptors. The display-only 476 vocabulary changes career, troop, holding and rank text without changing internal IDs or saved prose. New English strings fall back to English until the owner requests a catalog regeneration.

Technology impact: civic clerk service, clerical mediation, and conditional regional campaigns are each `none` because living actors, recognition, existing careers, ordinary travel and war resolve them. Court escort, claimant recognition, and city settlement are each `none` in the forward-review ledger. Their constraints are route, living issuer, treasury, community, consent and existing site grant rules. The ordinary historical technology graph seeds period knowledge; no new research gate is attached to baseline service or civic negotiation.

The historical frame and remaining editorial uncertainties are recorded in docs/research/476-bookmark.md. The map places Zeno and the two Gothic hosts according to that research and separates the senior Burgundian king from his co-rulers.

These questions are now answered in docs/research/476-bookmark.md, which also records period offices, ladder, legal traditions, religious institutions, names, dress, place names and corrections to the current 476 data. The regional map declarations now apply its ownership, seat and climate corrections. Office terminology and further regional institutional content remain separate system authoring.

## Phase 6 save integration

The branch now carries this campaign through the normal v4 save, slot, autosave
and portable import/export paths. V3 prototype saves migrate their existing
situation and authority records without restarting the opening or repeating a
payment, award, campaign declaration or ruler replacement. The same boundary
preserves 867/1066 saves without adding 476 state. The authority schema remains
separate from population communities and ordinary property. This persistence
integration changes no gameplay eligibility and needs no technology-impact entry.

See the [Phase 6 record](../plans/v2-476-phase-6.md) for authored coverage and the
remaining historical, device, performance and owner-run release gates. The branch
is an integration candidate, not evidence that those gates have passed.

Optional institutions in this campaign

The 476 bookmark explicitly disables papacy, scutage, manorial_tenure, excommunication, feudal_service, crown_bloc and tournaments. Its previous greatHolyWars:false rule is now the great_holy_wars disable in the same layer. These institutions stay disabled as campaign years pass, including for an inherited or converted Catholic faith. The shared availability contract is documented in docs/designs/systems.md.

Authored map and administrative hierarchy

The bookmark entry contains composition declarations only. data/bookmark_476_italy.js, data/bookmark_476_dalmatia_east.js, data/bookmark_476_gaul_iberia.js and data/bookmark_476_africa.js own the inhabited cells and realm profiles. Each cell explicitly names its realm, principal population, communities and development. With the late antique direct-holder governments, that realm is both its sovereign owner and its direct holder. There is no 867 owner remap, copied title table or generated feudal vassal layer. Stable province ids remain the saved geographic keys; pannonia remains the legacy id of the Amal body, which owns no Pannonian county at the opening. Britain, Scandinavia, the steppe, Cyprus, the Caucasian fringe and the Near East remain uninhabited pressure areas.

Italy, Dalmatia and the East use new d_476_, k_476_ and e_476_ groups. Roman civic vocabulary calls these province, diocese and prefecture. The retained county grid approximates administrative coverage; Dalmatia's intermediate group is a gameplay container for Nepos's separate administration, not an attested diocese. Other regional realms have no title hierarchy and use the existing customary-right claim path. Roman administration in Syagrius's domain is represented by its government form without reconstructing uncertain province groups. Vandal Sicily, Sardinia, Corsica and the Balearics have no Italian title groups; mainland southern Italy belongs to the western court. Arles and Marseille are Visigothic at this editorial snapshot.

Dated county names are geography data. The 476 settlement layout includes Ravenna and Classis, Salona and Spalatum, Carthage, Sufetula, Rome and Ostia, Soissons and Tournai. It includes Altinum instead of a medieval Venice site. Unresolved identifications use regional names rather than asserting an uncertain ancient town. No settlement list is constructed inside the bookmark entry.

The bookmark's climate layer assigns temperate conditions to the Po valley, northern Gaul, the Rhine and Danube; Mediterranean conditions to the southern and coastal core; continental winters to the Anatolian plateau; and arid conditions to the Saharan and Tripolitanian margins. Mediterranean mild winters have no winter hardship surcharge, while hot dry arid summers do. The existing army supply rule reads those flags. The physical geography and medieval bookmarks keep their original assignments. Climate and generated profiles are data authoring and extensions of existing geography readers, with no new technology-dependent player capability.
