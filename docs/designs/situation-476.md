# Spring 476 prototype

The 476 bookmark is a bounded, playable political slice. It borrows the 867 county polygons as spatial anchors, then authors its own rulers, communities, faiths and settlement layouts. Counties outside the Roman, Gothic, Frankish and Vandal test area are uninhabited in this bookmark. The 867 and 1066 bookmarks retain their authored data and ordinary vassal generation. The 476 start uses direct realm county holders because the feudal vassal generator does not describe the period's civic and military relationships.

The western court begins at Ravenna under Romulus Augustulus, with Orestes holding an exact Italian host command. Odoacer leads a separate, nonterritorial federate polity and host near Ticinum. Nepos holds Dalmatia; Basiliscus holds Constantinople while Zeno is displaced. The Roman domain in Gaul, Franks, Visigoths, Burgundians, Ostrogoths and Vandals are on the bounded map. The displaced Zeno is a living character and claimant, not a second owner of Constantinople. His conditional return requires him and Basiliscus to live, Basiliscus to remain in office, Constantinople to remain eastern, and the realm to be at peace. A new ruler generation then invalidates Basiliscus's reaffirmation-dependent commissions. Backing Zeno supplies a new commission only on his actual return.

Ravenna and Classis, Salona and Spalatum, and Carthage and later Tunis are separate sites or names, not aliases of one physical point. The 476 layout omits Kairouan and uses Sufetula within the retained county polygon. Site coordinates are authoring anchors, with [Salona](https://www.levantineceramics.org/sites/2500-salona), [Carthage](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/37/maps/), and the [Classis port](https://comune.ravenna.it/vivere-il-comune/luoghi/parco-archeologico/antico-porto-di-classe/) checked against published site records. The county grid remains approximate; the names do not imply exact fifth-century boundaries.

The playable start is a 24-year-old free Gothic/Homoian soldier at Ravenna with 20 game gold and no land or hereditary command. The Deeds situation begins with a delegate choice and an exact-host commission claim. Political advancement uses authority records: an army acclamation supports a field appointment, a paid federate contract binds two sovereign counterparties, civic acceptance is scoped to Ravenna, and an imperial commission names its issuer. The Nicene civic majority and Homoian player household make episcopal mediation a possible condition of civic acceptance. None of these grants automatically transfers ownership, judicial Peace, or revenue.

The negotiated route offers 30 from the western treasury immediately and four seasonal payments of 10 to the federate body. Two of each seasonal payment go to the household; the share is included in the ten. The player may seek Classis for a named veteran, with the grant requoted and confirmed only at ratification. The court's escort follows an ordinary host route to Ticinum, and one separate payment of 5 can be made for maintenance. Ratification requires the live contract, civic consent and court arrival. Refusal can return to pay negotiation or proceed to a contested march. The contest uses the real map host and battle; taking Ravenna can displace the western office without rewriting its population, private holdings or other counties. Defeat and withdrawal leave the household playable.

All situation facts and outcome flags are serializable under `state.situation476`. Quotes are pure and stamped from current actors, treasury, territory, host positions and authority records. Applying a reviewed decision rechecks those facts. News uses durable message descriptors. The display-only 476 vocabulary changes career, troop, holding and rank text without changing internal IDs or saved prose. New English strings fall back to English until the owner requests a catalog regeneration.

Technology impact: court escort, claimant recognition, and city settlement are each `none` in the forward-review ledger. Their constraints are route, living issuer, treasury, community, consent and existing site grant rules. The ordinary historical technology graph seeds period knowledge; no new research gate is attached to baseline service or civic negotiation.

This remains a prototype. The exact historical label for the detachment commission, the placement of Zeno's displaced court, Gundobad's partition and the two Theoderics need source review before a full 2.0 bookmark.
