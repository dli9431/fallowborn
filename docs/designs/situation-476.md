# The 476 regional campaign

The 476 bookmark is a bounded, playable regional campaign. It uses the stable county seeds and terrain of europe_regional as spatial anchors, with independently authored rulers, direct holders, communities, faiths and settlement layouts. The active region now includes the western and eastern Roman courts, Gaul, Visigothic Iberia, the Suevic northwest, Armorican and Rhenish communities, Dalmatia, the Vandal coast, and Moorish communities. Distant Britain, Scandinavia, the eastern steppe, and other unauthored areas remain uninhabited in this bookmark; their 867 governments must never be relabeled as 476 rulers. Several county anchors stand for broader territories, not fifth-century county borders. Seeded generated rulers represent communities whose 476 ruler is not securely attested; their ruler declarations carry generated:true. The 867 and 1066 bookmarks retain their authored data and ordinary vassal generation. The 476 bookmark declares the late antique era, whose default `regnum` government form keeps each county in its realm's own hand because the feudal vassal generator does not describe the period's civic and military relationships. The bookmark data, not the story, gives the western, Dalmatian and eastern courts the `roman_civic` form and the federate body the `federate_polity` form ([realms](realms.md#eras-and-government-forms)).

Civic seats and episcopal institutions

Church councils and ruler conversion use the optional APIs in js/religion.js. Provincial councils require two living bishops in directly held lands and protected worship; a ruler can sponsor another communion's gathering for scoped recognition and Popular support. Convert faith's realm scope changes court and elite allegiance while leaving resident communities intact. It withdraws old church recognition, rebases court regard and shifts support according to the actual new and old communities, with temporary backlash where the abandoned faith remains substantial. The existing governing-identity calculation then carries that tension into annual support and civic consent.

Annual AI evaluates local following, bishops, spouses, zeal, funds, peace and the once-per-ruler commitment through that same transaction. The API is available to the Frankish world story without forcing Clovis's baptism or date onto a divergent campaign. Councils and public profession each have a none technology-impact review. The 476 systems layer also disables abbey houses; period lay, clerical and monastic progression continues independently of medieval abbey-house content. Rules are in docs/designs/religions.md and docs/designs/conversion.md.

Rome and Constantinople have senates; Ravenna, Mediolanum, Carthage, Salona and Arles have city councils. Their sees act through the institutional ledger, alongside Ravenna's distinct Homoian congregation and the off-map offices at Alexandria, Antioch and Jerusalem. The researched cast supplies Simplicius, Exuperantius, Glycerius, Acacius, Timothy Aelurus, Peter the Fuller and Anastasius. Other representatives are generated officeholders. Carthage's Nicene see starts vacant rather than inventing a bishop before Eugenius's election. Research and the differing 480/481 dates are recorded in docs/research/476-bookmark.md and https://www.newadvent.org/cathen/05602c.htm.

The Ravenna service story calls the generic petition, bargain and weighted ratification transaction. Its effective service contract supplies the provision bench; protected courts and worship and live episcopal support supply the other conditions. Classis, the escort, western office and household share remain separate transactions. City and church Deeds expose other seats and mediation. Seasonal AI uses the same institution APIs. The owning contract is in docs/designs/council.md.

Restore binds missing institutions to the story's saved delegate, senator and bishops through a registered restore hook. Existing ledgers, vacancies, pending votes and payments are preserved. Legacy civic recognition remains usable until a generic petition replaces it. Institutional bargaining and episcopal mediation each have a none technology-impact review.

The western court begins at Ravenna under Romulus Augustulus, with Orestes holding an exact Italian host command. Odoacer leads a separate, nonterritorial federate polity and host near Ticinum. Nepos holds Dalmatia; Basiliscus holds Constantinople while Zeno is displaced. Syagrius holds the Roman domain from Soissons (laon), Childeric sits at Tournai (ghent), and Chilperic I is senior Burgundian king at Lyon. Gundobad co-rules at Vienne and is the named heir, while Godegisel has a court at Geneva. The Amal Gothic host under Theoderic is nonterritorial in Macedonia, with a separate Thracian Gothic host under Strabo. A generated Gepid ruler holds the Pannonian cells from Sirmium; Rugian pressure north of the Danube is represented by Feletheus in the wien cell, while Noricum remains nominally Italian. The displaced Zeno is a living claimant at the Isaurian court site in ikonion, without county ownership; the site is a regional anchor rather than an identification of his siege fortress. His conditional return requires him and Basiliscus to live, Basiliscus to remain in office, Constantinople to remain eastern, and the realm to be at peace. A new ruler generation then invalidates Basiliscus's reaffirmation-dependent commissions. Backing Zeno supplies a new commission only on his actual return.

Ravenna and Classis, Salona and Spalatum, and Carthage and later Tunis are separate sites or names, not aliases of one physical point. The 476 layout omits Kairouan and uses Sufetula within the retained county polygon. Site coordinates are authoring anchors, with [Salona](https://www.levantineceramics.org/sites/2500-salona), [Carthage](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/37/maps/), and the [Classis port](https://comune.ravenna.it/vivere-il-comune/luoghi/parco-archeologico/antico-porto-di-classe/) checked against published site records. The county grid remains approximate; the names do not imply exact fifth-century boundaries.

The 476 bookmark disables great holy wars. Its bounded map leaves the later Catholic and Sunni sacred targets uninhabited, so their global campaign definitions do not apply to this start. The 867 and 1066 bookmarks keep their normal date and target rules. The 476 population and ruling courts use the authored Nicene, Homoian, Chalcedonian, Miaphysite and traditional faith identities; no Muslim population is placed in this bookmark.

The primary start is a 24-year-old free Gothic/Homoian soldier at Ravenna with 20 game gold and no land or hereditary command. Its free-household family preset and federate player track retain the delegate choice and exact-host commission claim. The second start is a 30-year-old Roman/Nicene curialis at Ravenna, with 80 game gold, 30 prestige, five completed years of curial work and a choice of an established family or an unmarried household. Its separate civic track offers registration and recognition for a local judicial mandate, or ordinary livelihood outside the court dispute. Political advancement uses authority records: an army acclamation supports a field appointment, a paid federate contract binds two sovereign counterparties, civic acceptance is scoped to Ravenna, and an imperial commission names its issuer. The Nicene civic majority and Homoian soldier household make episcopal mediation a possible condition of civic acceptance. None of these grants automatically transfers ownership or revenue.

The opening now also offers a local civic clerk career and a Homoian clerical route. The civic clerk can petition for a scoped judicial appointment from a living western court; the church route requires a living Homoian bishop before Nicene mediation, and uses late antique lay and clerical rank paths rather than the later Catholic Papacy. A living senator holds a separate Rome civic delegate mandate; his consent is required before the federate court can recognize Nepos for the western office. Each route can end without land or office, leaving the household playable.

The curialis opening uses state.stories.curialis_track_476 and the same shared story cards, choice review, return navigation and save validation as the federate track. A mandate requires the household to be at Ravenna, a living western court, civic delegate and bishop, a Nicene household and local majority, and the quoted funds and prestige. Registration costs 10 in addition to the ladder's current recognition fee, initially 50 and 20 prestige. The appointment supplies local Peace; recognition advances the household to Decurion through the ordinary standing transaction. Neither step grants host command, land or fiscal authority. Continuing without a mandate is free. Completing either choice leaves the courts to pursue their own service bargain. Restoring a completed opening does not repeat an appointment or charge.

Late antique careers include civic clerk, curialis and decurion work with the civic role, colonus agricultural work with the labor role, the existing soldier career with the arms role and period display labels, and the existing clerical career with the clergy role. Merchant and craft work retain their current roles and behavior. The late antique clerical path reads Lector, Deacon, Presbyter and Bishop; stable path rank IDs and indexes are retained for existing saves. Tenant farming changes occupation only. A colonus starting scenario and land-bound colonate tenure are not part of these starts.

The negotiated route offers 30 from the western treasury immediately and four seasonal payments of 10 to the federate body. Two of each seasonal payment go to the household; the share is included in the ten. The player may seek Classis for a named veteran, with the grant requoted and confirmed only at ratification. The court's escort follows an ordinary host route to Ticinum, and one separate payment of 5 can be made for maintenance. Ratification requires the live contract, civic consent and court arrival. Refusal can return to pay negotiation or proceed to a contested march. The contest uses the real map host and battle; taking Ravenna can displace the western office without rewriting its population, private holdings or other counties. Defeat and withdrawal leave the household playable.

A ratified Classis award moves the named veteran from Ticinum to Ravenna and records the exact new site in the federate track's saved record; the normal settlement grant transfers only that port holding. A ratified civic bargain establishes saved local Peace and consent rights for Ravenna’s delegate, separately from county ownership. A victorious federate host can affirm its own government or accept Roman civic rule after living civic and Nicene church actors agree. It can leave the western office vacant under an eastern commission or acknowledge a living Nepos; if the western court later recovers Ravenna, a living Romulus can return to that office even after a rival claim was recognized. These are separate decisions. Authority records, rather than result flags alone, hold the mandate and can lapse on succession or loss of scope. If negotiations collapse through death, extinction, loss of the court or a stale contract, the player can leave the dispute and continue as a household. If the player declines involvement, the courts attempt the same paid service and civic transactions without them; a failed bargain can become an ordinary objective war. The outside household takes no service share.

The later history is conditional. Clovis can challenge Syagrius around 486 only if he inherits the Frankish realm and the Roman domain still holds Soissons. Theoderic may receive a commission from Zeno and open a migration toward Ravenna in 488-493 only while both rule their courts and the target remains western or federate. The data-defined commission transfers 40 for equipment once and binds the exact Gothic host, whose court marches with ordinary routes and provisions. War must award Ravenna before a claims session establishes a territorial holding and regnum form; surviving civic and church delegates may then support Roman civic rule. An eastern court retaining Constantinople can open African and Italian reconquest campaigns from 533 and 535 against their then-current owners. These are ordinary objective wars; their outcome follows armies, occupation and peace, and a failed or diverted campaign does not transfer a county. Conquest can displace a court to surviving territory. The 476 bookmark adds a Syracuse-Carthage sea crossing to make the long African route possible on its bounded map. These dates identify opportunities, not scripted replacement of divergent rulers.

The situation runs as two registered stories (stories.md). The bookmark attaches the western court world story, which authors the cast and standing hosts, moves displaced courts, runs Zeno's return, settles the dispute through the courts when the household declines, and runs the later campaigns. The Federate Soldier scenario attaches the federate track, which holds the household's choices and the contest it starts. Each bargain record belongs to whoever made it. An observed 476 world has the world story only, so the courts' settlement there waits on a household that is not present, as before. The Deeds card and decision sheet are the shared story presentation.

Situation facts and outcome flags are serializable under the world story and the selected player's track; branch saves holding the former state.situation476 split into the original two records once on load. Existing federate saves do not acquire a civic track. Quotes are pure and stamped from current actors, treasury, territory, host positions and authority records. Applying a reviewed decision rechecks those facts. News uses durable message descriptors. The era's ladder now supplies progression rules separately from the display lexicon, including civic means, completed service, offices and political recognition. Saved band identities and translated prose remain separate. New English strings fall back to English until the owner requests a catalog regeneration.

Technology impact: civic clerk service, clerical mediation, and conditional regional campaigns are each `none` because living actors, recognition, existing careers, ordinary travel and war resolve them. Court escort, claimant recognition, and city settlement are each `none` in the forward-review ledger. Their constraints are route, living issuer, treasury, community, consent and existing site grant rules. The ordinary historical technology graph seeds period knowledge; no new research gate is attached to baseline service or civic negotiation.

Optional institutions in this campaign

The 476 bookmark explicitly disables papacy, scutage, manorial_tenure, excommunication, feudal_service, crown_bloc and tournaments. Its previous greatHolyWars:false rule is now the great_holy_wars disable in the same layer. These institutions stay disabled as campaign years pass, including for an inherited or converted Catholic faith. The shared availability contract is documented in docs/designs/systems.md.

Authored map and administrative hierarchy

The bookmark entry contains composition declarations only. data/bookmark_476_italy.js, data/bookmark_476_dalmatia_east.js, data/bookmark_476_gaul_iberia.js and data/bookmark_476_africa.js own the inhabited cells and realm profiles. Each cell explicitly names its realm, principal population, communities and development. With the late antique direct-holder governments, that realm is both its sovereign owner and its direct holder. There is no 867 owner remap, copied title table or generated feudal vassal layer. Stable province ids remain the saved geographic keys; pannonia remains the legacy id of the Amal body, which owns no Pannonian county at the opening. Britain, Scandinavia, the steppe, Cyprus, the Caucasian fringe and the Near East remain uninhabited pressure areas.

Italy, Dalmatia and the East use new d_476_, k_476_ and e_476_ groups. Roman civic vocabulary calls these province, diocese and prefecture. The retained county grid approximates administrative coverage; Dalmatia's intermediate group is a gameplay container for Nepos's separate administration, not an attested diocese. Other regional realms have no title hierarchy and use the existing customary-right claim path. Roman administration in Syagrius's domain is represented by its government form without reconstructing uncertain province groups. Vandal Sicily, Sardinia, Corsica and the Balearics have no Italian title groups; mainland southern Italy belongs to the western court. Arles and Marseille are Visigothic at this editorial snapshot.

Dated county names are geography data. The 476 settlement layout includes Ravenna and Classis, Salona and Spalatum, Carthage, Sufetula, Rome and Ostia, Soissons and Tournai. It includes Altinum instead of a medieval Venice site. Unresolved identifications use regional names rather than asserting an uncertain ancient town. No settlement list is constructed inside the bookmark entry.

The bookmark's climate layer assigns temperate conditions to the Po valley, northern Gaul, the Rhine and Danube; Mediterranean conditions to the southern and coastal core; continental winters to the Anatolian plateau; and arid conditions to the Saharan and Tripolitanian margins. Mediterranean mild winters have no winter hardship surcharge, while hot dry arid summers do. The existing army supply rule reads those flags. The physical geography and medieval bookmarks keep their original assignments. Climate and generated profiles are data authoring and extensions of existing geography readers, with no new technology-dependent player capability.

Customary rights and institutional identity across the region

The 476 bookmark now authors each court or polity's governing culture, faith and legal tradition separately from its county and settlement populations. Roman institutions use late Roman secular law; Gothic elites use Gothic custom with personal Roman law and mixed-court rules. Other communities retain customary law. The initial treatment defaults are confessional preference, equal taxation and personal law. These defaults are explicit gameplay abstractions, not assertions of identical treatment across all historical courts.

The regional declarations seed provincial court tenure for Peace and Revenue at Ravenna, Rome, Salona, Soissons (laon), Toulouse, Carthage and Constantinople. Every regional realm, including generated rulers and nonterritorial Gothic hosts, declares its institutional identity independently of the local communities. Rights elsewhere emerge through annual possession, lawful judgments and scoped legal recognition. Both player and AI use the same transitions and existing rights-based war goals. No new de jure hierarchy is synthesized for a rights claim.

A Homoian elite over a predominantly Nicene population therefore remains distinct from that population, and its treatment has concrete Popular support and civic-consent consequences. Players ruling their own realm can review identity and treatment under Governance, Position. Realm interaction sheets expose declared elite culture, faith and law. Conquest changes owner and holder through the existing transfer boundary and preserves the live county and settlement community partitions. Old saves receive no new identities or historical rights during restore.

Technology impact for identity, mixed courts, community worship, tax treatment and justice policy is none, recorded separately in the forward-review ledger. The implementation uses the existing authority script, legal tables, government rows, support and modal history. It adds no runtime dependencies, asset, script-order change or distribution-specific exemption.

The Amal migration

Theoderic the Amal starts as a landless federate ruler at the Thessalonica county anchor in Macedonia, rather than a hereditary owner in Pannonia. The stable pannonia realm id remains an institutional identity, not a location. The authored Pannonian cells remain under the generated Gepid court at Sirmium, independently of the landless Amal host; these county anchors do not claim exact fifth-century borders. The 867 and 1066 bookmark definitions are unchanged.

The authored theoderic_italy opportunity lives in data/political_institutions.js and is consumed by the western court world story. It names the patron, polity, existing host_476_amal, origin, Ravenna objective, 488-493 opportunity window, contract template and regnum settlement form. The migration reuses that opening banner with its current position, troops and casualties; a missing or destroyed host blocks the opportunity rather than creating another army. Generic state.authority.migrations carries the actual court, contract and claims session. The world story records its ids and result; it cannot replace lost troops, teleport the court, guarantee victory or award land by date. A commissioned Gothic settlement is territorial first; Roman civic government still needs the existing living civic and Nicene mediation conditions.

Research basis: Jordanes, Getica 285-288 places the Amal group in Macedonia after departure from Pannonia, while 291-295 describes the eastern commission, a movement of the people into Italy and the ensuing siege. The source gives a retrospective dynastic account, so the game treats the commission and war as conditional opportunities rather than guaranteed history. The departure window follows the 488-493 chronology; county anchors and the game-scaled equipment payment are abstractions, not historical boundaries, troop counts or coin amounts. The later move to Moesia is not separately authored in this bounded migration chain.
