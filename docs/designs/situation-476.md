# The 476 regional campaign

The 476 bookmark is a bounded, playable regional campaign. It borrows the 867 county polygons as spatial anchors, then authors its own rulers, communities, faiths and settlement layouts. The active region now includes the western and eastern Roman courts, Gaul, Visigothic Iberia, the Suevic northwest, Armorican and Rhenish communities, Dalmatia, the Vandal coast, and Moorish communities. Distant Britain, Scandinavia, the eastern steppe, and other unauthored areas remain uninhabited in this bookmark; their 867 governments must never be relabeled as 476 rulers. Several county anchors stand for broader territories, not fifth-century county borders. Composite delegates represent communities whose 476 ruler is not securely attested. The 867 and 1066 bookmarks retain their authored data and ordinary vassal generation. The 476 bookmark declares the late antique era, whose default `regnum` government form keeps each county in its realm's own hand because the feudal vassal generator does not describe the period's civic and military relationships. The bookmark data, not the story, gives the western, Dalmatian and eastern courts the `roman_civic` form and the federate body the `federate_polity` form ([realms](realms.md#eras-and-government-forms)).

The western court begins at Ravenna under Romulus Augustulus, with Orestes holding an exact Italian host command. Odoacer leads a separate, nonterritorial federate polity and host near Ticinum. Nepos holds Dalmatia; Basiliscus holds Constantinople while Zeno is displaced. The Roman domain in Gaul, Franks, Visigoths, Burgundians, Ostrogoths and Vandals are on the bounded map. The displaced Zeno is a living character and claimant, not a second owner of Constantinople. His conditional return requires him and Basiliscus to live, Basiliscus to remain in office, Constantinople to remain eastern, and the realm to be at peace. A new ruler generation then invalidates Basiliscus's reaffirmation-dependent commissions. Backing Zeno supplies a new commission only on his actual return.

Ravenna and Classis, Salona and Spalatum, and Carthage and later Tunis are separate sites or names, not aliases of one physical point. The 476 layout omits Kairouan and uses Sufetula within the retained county polygon. Site coordinates are authoring anchors, with [Salona](https://www.levantineceramics.org/sites/2500-salona), [Carthage](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/37/maps/), and the [Classis port](https://comune.ravenna.it/vivere-il-comune/luoghi/parco-archeologico/antico-porto-di-classe/) checked against published site records. The county grid remains approximate; the names do not imply exact fifth-century boundaries.

The 476 bookmark disables great holy wars. Its bounded map leaves the later Catholic and Sunni sacred targets uninhabited, so their global campaign definitions do not apply to this start. The 867 and 1066 bookmarks keep their normal date and target rules. The 476 population and ruling courts use the authored Nicene, Homoian, Chalcedonian, Miaphysite and traditional faith identities; no Muslim population is placed in this bookmark.

The primary start is a 24-year-old free Gothic/Homoian soldier at Ravenna with 20 game gold and no land or hereditary command. Its free-household family preset and federate player track retain the delegate choice and exact-host commission claim. The second start is a 30-year-old Roman/Nicene curialis at Ravenna, with 80 game gold, 30 prestige, five completed years of curial work and a choice of an established family or an unmarried household. Its separate civic track offers registration and recognition for a local judicial mandate, or ordinary livelihood outside the court dispute. Political advancement uses authority records: an army acclamation supports a field appointment, a paid federate contract binds two sovereign counterparties, civic acceptance is scoped to Ravenna, and an imperial commission names its issuer. The Nicene civic majority and Homoian soldier household make episcopal mediation a possible condition of civic acceptance. None of these grants automatically transfers ownership or revenue.

The opening now also offers a local civic clerk career and a Homoian clerical route. The civic clerk can petition for a scoped judicial appointment from a living western court; the church route requires a living Homoian bishop before Nicene mediation, and uses late antique lay and clerical rank paths rather than the later Catholic Papacy. A living senator holds a separate Rome civic delegate mandate; his consent is required before the federate court can recognize Nepos for the western office. Each route can end without land or office, leaving the household playable.

The curialis opening uses state.stories.curialis_track_476 and the same shared story cards, choice review, return navigation and save validation as the federate track. A mandate requires the household to be at Ravenna, a living western court, civic delegate and bishop, a Nicene household and local majority, and the quoted funds and prestige. Registration costs 10 in addition to the ladder's current recognition fee, initially 50 and 20 prestige. The appointment supplies local Peace; recognition advances the household to Decurion through the ordinary standing transaction. Neither step grants host command, land or fiscal authority. Continuing without a mandate is free. Completing either choice leaves the courts to pursue their own service bargain. Restoring a completed opening does not repeat an appointment or charge.

Late antique careers include civic clerk, curialis and decurion work with the civic role, colonus agricultural work with the labor role, the existing soldier career with the arms role and period display labels, and the existing clerical career with the clergy role. Merchant and craft work retain their current roles and behavior. The late antique clerical path reads Lector, Deacon, Presbyter and Bishop; stable path rank IDs and indexes are retained for existing saves. Tenant farming changes occupation only. A colonus starting scenario and land-bound colonate tenure are not part of these starts.

The negotiated route offers 30 from the western treasury immediately and four seasonal payments of 10 to the federate body. Two of each seasonal payment go to the household; the share is included in the ten. The player may seek Classis for a named veteran, with the grant requoted and confirmed only at ratification. The court's escort follows an ordinary host route to Ticinum, and one separate payment of 5 can be made for maintenance. Ratification requires the live contract, civic consent and court arrival. Refusal can return to pay negotiation or proceed to a contested march. The contest uses the real map host and battle; taking Ravenna can displace the western office without rewriting its population, private holdings or other counties. Defeat and withdrawal leave the household playable.

A ratified Classis award moves the named veteran from Ticinum to Ravenna and records the exact new site in the federate track's saved record; the normal settlement grant transfers only that port holding. A ratified civic bargain establishes saved local Peace and consent rights for Ravenna’s delegate, separately from county ownership. A victorious federate host can affirm its own government or accept Roman civic rule after living civic and Nicene church actors agree. It can leave the western office vacant under an eastern commission or acknowledge a living Nepos; if the western court later recovers Ravenna, a living Romulus can return to that office even after a rival claim was recognized. These are separate decisions. Authority records, rather than result flags alone, hold the mandate and can lapse on succession or loss of scope. If negotiations collapse through death, extinction, loss of the court or a stale contract, the player can leave the dispute and continue as a household. If the player declines involvement, the courts attempt the same paid service and civic transactions without them; a failed bargain can become an ordinary objective war. The outside household takes no service share.

The later history is conditional. Clovis can challenge Syagrius around 486 only if he inherits the Frankish realm and the Roman domain still holds Paris. Theoderic may receive Zeno’s commission and contest Ravenna in 489–493 only while both rule their courts and the target remains western or federate. An eastern court retaining Constantinople can open African and Italian reconquest campaigns from 533 and 535 against their then-current owners. These are ordinary objective wars; their outcome follows armies, occupation and peace, and a failed or diverted campaign does not transfer a county. Conquest can displace a court to surviving territory; a Gothic settlement in Ravenna grants civic rights only with surviving delegates and a Nicene majority. The 476 bookmark adds a Syracuse–Carthage sea crossing to make the long African route possible on its bounded map. These dates identify opportunities, not scripted replacement of divergent rulers.

The situation runs as two registered stories (stories.md). The bookmark attaches the western court world story, which authors the cast and standing hosts, moves displaced courts, runs Zeno's return, settles the dispute through the courts when the household declines, and runs the later campaigns. The Federate Soldier scenario attaches the federate track, which holds the household's choices and the contest it starts. Each bargain record belongs to whoever made it. An observed 476 world has the world story only, so the courts' settlement there waits on a household that is not present, as before. The Deeds card and decision sheet are the shared story presentation.

Situation facts and outcome flags are serializable under the world story and the selected player's track; branch saves holding the former state.situation476 split into the original two records once on load. Existing federate saves do not acquire a civic track. Quotes are pure and stamped from current actors, treasury, territory, host positions and authority records. Applying a reviewed decision rechecks those facts. News uses durable message descriptors. The era's ladder now supplies progression rules separately from the display lexicon, including civic means, completed service, offices and political recognition. Saved band identities and translated prose remain separate. New English strings fall back to English until the owner requests a catalog regeneration.

Technology impact: civic clerk service, clerical mediation, and conditional regional campaigns are each `none` because living actors, recognition, existing careers, ordinary travel and war resolve them. Court escort, claimant recognition, and city settlement are each `none` in the forward-review ledger. Their constraints are route, living issuer, treasury, community, consent and existing site grant rules. The ordinary historical technology graph seeds period knowledge; no new research gate is attached to baseline service or civic negotiation.

The exact historical label for the detachment commission, the placement of Zeno's displaced court, Gundobad's partition and the two Theoderics remain source-review tasks for the release gate. See [the scenario fixture](../plans/v2-476-phase-0/scenario.md) and [the phased plan](../plans/v2-476-phased-implementation.md).

These questions are now answered in docs/research/476-bookmark.md, which also records period offices, ladder, legal traditions, religious institutions, names, dress, place names and corrections to the current 476 data. The data changes it recommends are applied when the 476 map and cast are re-authored, not by the note itself.

## Phase 6 save integration

The branch now carries this campaign through the normal v4 save, slot, autosave
and portable import/export paths. V3 prototype saves migrate their existing
situation and authority records without restarting the opening or repeating a
payment, award, campaign declaration or ruler replacement. The same boundary
preserves 867/1066 saves without adding 476 state. The authority schema remains
separate from population communities and ordinary property. This persistence
integration changes no gameplay eligibility and needs no technology-impact entry.

See the [Phase 6 record](../plans/v2-476-phase-6.md) for authored coverage and the
remaining historical, device, performance and owner-run release gates. The branch
is an integration candidate, not evidence that those gates have passed.

Optional institutions in this campaign

The 476 bookmark explicitly disables papacy, scutage, manorial_tenure, excommunication, feudal_service, crown_bloc and tournaments. Its previous greatHolyWars:false rule is now the great_holy_wars disable in the same layer. These institutions stay disabled as campaign years pass, including for an inherited or converted Catholic faith. The shared availability contract is documented in docs/designs/systems.md.
