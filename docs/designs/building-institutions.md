Buildings at existing institutions

Building knowledge, a physical asset, endowed property and institutional powers remain separate. An optional institutionProfile on a nonfort building selects a buildingInstitutionProfiles policy. A bookmark or whole-field revision supplies buildingInstitutionAssignments, each naming that policy and one existing local institution. Nothing initializes an institution, member, office, successor, plot or treasury. The ordinary Mods file flow compiles the same effective definitions before publication and fingerprints them with the whole imported world.

Policy and authority

Each policy declares compatible canonical kinds, seat or abbey property, holder or property_patron permission, required or none incumbent, and exclusiveRoles. Seat property requires ordinary direct settlement lordship. Abbey property additionally requires an actually founded canonical abbey house, a real endowed plot at this exact settlement and its recorded patron dynasty. Property patron permission never replaces settlement lordship. A spiritual title grants neither construction authority nor access to a public account.

Assignments resolve the institution's exact inhabited physical seat in the effective geography and layout. A policy has at most one assignment per physical seat, and all references resolve in the detached candidate. Missing property remains missing and blocks its building; a declared future receiving property is not an endowment. Profiles do not allow arbitrary predicates, imported callbacks or state paths.

Occupancy and transactions

FB.buildingInstitutionStatus projects permission and physical occupancy. The same institution or referenced property cannot receive another bound construction while its foundation is occupied, including ruins. At its exact site, existing works in the declared exclusiveRoles also occupy that foundation, even without a saved binding. This prevents an ordinary sanctuary and its institutional alternative from generating two construction awards on one footprint. Other roles and separately declared sites in the same county remain available. Existing county and construction-group limits still apply.

Player construction, automation, private barons and annual realm construction recheck the same projection before payment. BuildingInstitutionQuote and buildingInstitutionApply expose exact construct and reaffirm proposals, including the actor, payer, live price, funds, incumbent, property facts, generation and prior binding. Confirmation rejects stale or repeated proposals. Construction still pays the ordinary price from the builder's existing account: player household gold, the actual barony account or the actual realm treasury with ordinary reserves. The builder and maintenance payer are the same explicit actor. There is no house-account debit, public subsidy, public/private substitution or cross-account transfer. A player realm without an independent public account is not a second payer.

Normal demolition of bound works removes its physical record without refund, including a ruin. Existing development reversal still applies. Ordinary unbound demolition retains its previous behavior. Repairs use the existing asset and price; a bound repair also needs current permission and does not acquire a new technology or foundation.

Operation and maintenance

The ordinary building and settlement fiscal owners remain the only benefit and upkeep owners. A valid bound asset contributes its normal ongoing numeric fields and its upkeep once to its actual recorded holder/payer. A changed county controller, political generation, office incumbent, custody, property, patron or settlement holder immediately suspends these institution-conditioned projections. Standing assets, physical occupancy, past construction awards and existing property are retained. There is no replacement incumbent, transfer, refund or new economic effect.

An authorized current builder must explicitly reaffirm permission for the same asset before operation resumes under changed identity or generation terms. Temporary unavailability of the same original authority blocks operation for its duration; return of that unchanged permission does not manufacture a replacement or require a new construction. Reaffirmation costs zero, creates no construction award and records a new bounded revision. It cannot enroll a legacy unbound building. NPC and player callers use the same reviewed transaction; there is no automatic heir or replacement-payer enrollment. The county building cache keys the current operation status of its bound records so death or changed permission cannot leave a stale benefit or maintenance charge. Unbound counties keep the previous cache path.

Persistence and presentation

Each newly bound ordinary building has a strict optional institutionBinding version 1. It retains profile, institution, property and stable site identities, the exact builder and equal payer, the original incumbent and authority/property generation, boundTurn and revision. The parent save validator checks the detached campaign definition, original character/realm references, actual site slot, finite chronology, generation shape, assignment and unique foundation before adopting state, RNG or uid. Dead original people and lost current permission can remain truthful inactive references. Missing unbound legacy records stay unbound; restore never founds, enrolls, charges, reaffirms or replaces anything.

Building Works names the foundation requirement, the household construction/maintenance payer, occupied foundations and current operation status. Its permission review shows the current asset, payer, zero review cost and recurring maintenance. Back, Escape and confirmation retain county/site, expanded disclosures, scroll and semantic focus. UI chrome uses FB.T; imported building names and descriptions use the ordinary building display owner and English fallback. No new catalog display namespace is required.

Technology impact

building_institution_binding is none. Identity, property permission and foundation occupancy are legal and physical conditions rather than discoveries. Optional building technology requirements remain separate, and ordinary unbound construction remains available.

Examples and limits

The complete building-institutions-see.json and building-institutions-house.json ordinary imports use different geographies, foundations, incumbent requirements, costs and finance rules. The see uses its existing canonical bishop and a seat footprint. The headless house needs an explicit paid property endowment and real donated plots before patron-funded physical works can be raised. Neither initializes a historical facility. These examples are development assets outside shipped allowlists. Basic institutional binding does not create an endowment, authorize independent property construction, appoint a replacement, convert anyone, create a mint, distribute relief or add a new passive benefit channel.

Selected construction definitions

The occupied-role check also applies when the proposed alternative has no institutionProfile. An ordinary temple or cathedral cannot be built over a bound worship foundation, including a ruin or an asset whose original permission has lapsed. The gate reads the existing selected definition and preserves occupancy across holder changes; unrelated roles and separate sites remain available. Existing unbound assets are not enrolled or suspended.

A building profile may select an existing institutionProfile while retaining the base physical asset and role. Construction and saved-binding validation use that selected definition; original incumbent, property, builder and payer identities stay with this ordinary owner. The same foundation occupancy excludes base and profiled alternatives, including ruins. Profile selection grants no office, endowment or account transfer. The typed construction contract is in docs/designs/building-profiles.md.

Required binding policy

An unbound required facility exposes the existing Review permission control and dormant explanation in Building Works. It uses the same exact current foundation/payer quote and preserves the existing phone, keyboard, Back, Escape and originating-focus behavior.

A policy may explicitly opt into requiresBinding:true. Its existing unbound assets then remain physical but supply no ongoing benefit or upkeep until an authorized current builder explicitly reaffirms their actual compatible foundation. The same dormant footprint excludes alternative worship construction. This is a costless permission transaction on the original asset, not construction, endowment or enrollment on restore. Newly constructed assets still bind before payment and operation. Omission preserves all legacy unbound semantics, including ordinary temple and cathedral assets. The inherited ordinary see import exercises dormant matching restore, occupancy and once-only explicit review through the same canonical transaction.
