Household associations

Camp carriage and personal survivor recovery

Technology impact household_party_camp_carriage is none: baseline named civilian carriage requires actual separate personal admission, exact consenting companions, finite host capacity, current commander acceptance and the real migration owner. Technology impact household_party_survivor_recovery is none: an already present stranded named survivor may explicitly consent to a new priced road after succession. Ordinary road, transport, custody, dependent care, affordability and settlement gates remain authoritative. Neither capability needs a discovery.

The household_party track records a finite plan involving named existing people. A fresh record has an empty roster. The protagonist invites a particular present household member, and the invitation reserves that person against competing household plans. Inviting oneself also requires a separate consent decision. Invitations expire within the declaration's bounded reply period. Accepted adult consent persists until withdrawal, ineligibility or closure. Refusal, expiry and withdrawal release reservations immediately; a lifetime roster row is never reused to erase an earlier decision.

Every adult answers personally. The declared volunteers or refuse policy supplies nonplayer adult replies through the same pure review and revalidating transaction that a direct participant uses. The protagonist's reply always remains a player decision. Policy cannot answer for a dependent. A dependent invitation identifies one actual recorded parent who is already an available accepted adult. That parent must separately consent to care. Removing a caregiver explicitly includes all their still-reserved dependents in the review and one atomic removal. No replacement parent is chosen automatically. Reaching adulthood ends the earlier dependent arrangement rather than silently turning parent permission into adult consent.

Eligibility reads current household membership, county residence, explicit remote residence, custody, exile, health, individual travel, protagonist obligations, mobile-principal commitments and expedition participation. It grants no permission to move a serf, leave custody, abandon a command or join a camp. The snapshot API divides roster ids into presently accepted, waiting and excluded people. These are availability facts for later consumers, not a location or admission witness.

Association alone does not own motion or change residence, property, money, population or religious vows. An optional ordinary journey adapter can separately review exact accepted people and start the existing personal travel owner. The separate optional camp adapter supplies independently reviewed civilian places and canonical migration carriage. Ordinary solo travel keeps its existing behavior.

Death or incapacity of the original protagonist closes the plan. Succession closes the original plan without enrolling an heir. Other members becoming unavailable withdraw at the next ordinary boundary; their dependents release in that same reconciliation. A closed record preserves its original decisions and releases every reservation. Missing live eligibility can remain in a structurally valid save until that boundary. Restore performs no AI response, closure, replacement, move or reservation repair.

The version-1 record stores the declaration id, original household and protagonist ids, bounded roster, revision, opening turn and optional closure. Each row preserves invitation time, finite reply deadline, adult or dependent category, exact caregiver where needed, decision time and consenting person. Parent shape, consent identity, original declaration limits, chronological ordering, duplicates and unknown fields are checked detached. The parent save validator also rejects missing opted-in tracks and a person reserved in two open plans. Unopted campaigns keep their ordinary save shape and envelope.

Technology impact household_party_consent is none. Making a household plan, deciding whether to participate and agreeing to care for a child require no discovery. All underlying custody, authority and travel constraints retain their own gates. The ordinary harbor and upland JSON imports vary capacity, adult replies, dependent eligibility and deadlines. The harbor example admits an adult couple to a plan; the upland example permits an explicitly consenting parent and child while another adult can decline. Neither fixture adds a runtime historical declaration.

The shared story sheet lists each named invitation and its consent state, with ordinary person links and reviewed costless choices. A removal review names the affected dependents before confirmation. Back, Escape and Close retain the existing story navigation, scroll and focus behavior. All chrome uses FB.T; imported title and intro retain effective English fallback. No rendered name or prose is stored in the association record.

Ordinary named journeys

An optional journey declaration selects one existing sites-mode study, pilgrimage or expedition destination. It grants no route, rank, transport or affordability exception. Departure requires the protagonist and every selected relative to have separate valid consent, actual home availability and no incompatible commitment. A previously accepted person who becomes unavailable must be explicitly removed before departure. The private purse pays the ordinary quoted fare for each actual traveller. Household members who declined or were never invited stay at their own locations. No named person is added to demographic population or an army.

Departure closes the invitation record and upgrades it to version 2 with a bounded journey child. Its members refer back to accepted version-1 consent rows. The actual player journey remains the sole route and movement clock. FB.characterLocation projects each accompanying person's current county; FB.characterResidence keeps the permanent home. Checkpoints preserve only counties actually reached and never imply presence at a particular physical settlement. Home employment, enterprise labor, institutional schooling and home medical care cannot use absent companions.

An adult may withdraw personally at the current checkpoint. A dependent remains with the exact consenting parent when that care group separates. Voluntary withdrawal remains distinct from interruption: a household return decision cannot renew another adult's revoked consent. That adult's explicit rejoin_person review, or a parent's explicit care review for a dependent, is required before renewed carriage. Death creates no population loss or relocation. Custody, incompatible independent commitments, adulthood ending parental permission or loss of a carrier separate the affected people at the checkpoint. Losing the protagonist interrupts the remaining carrier. Succession enrolls nobody and preserves the original people's recorded locations.

Cancellation holds the reached county and disables destination work and settlement. The shared story offers a separately reviewed recovery route, priced from the checkpoint for the exact eligible survivors using current transport and provisions. Its confirmation spends once; cancellation preserves the next checkpoint. An ordinary uninterrupted destination stay instead retains its minimum-stay gate, already funded return fare and original saved travel speed. Return completion releases only the people actually accompanying that return. A voluntarily detached person remains at the actual recorded county.

The original protagonist owns the original return review. A successor does not inherit that carrier or enroll new people. A separate explicit survivor recovery below can price a new personal road for an already present named survivor; it never takes over the former protagonist's return authority. An unresolved named location blocks a new ordinary departure from an invented household-home origin.

Permanent settlement retains ordinary rank, inhabited destination, residence, local work and lifetime-move gates. Only people still accompanying the eligible protagonist move permanently. Excluded managed relatives receive explicit householdResidenceId values retaining their prior residences; participating relatives receive the destination. This optional residence fact outranks the managed household's implicit home, while property and demographic ledgers remain unchanged. Named people left temporarily elsewhere keep their separate actual locations until a supported later operation changes them.

Detached restore validates exact record versions, original consent and parents, bounded unique people, dates, residence counties, journey identity, route adjacency and exclusive location claims. It neither starts travel, moves someone, renews consent nor repairs missing carriers. Existing association-only version-1 records retain their exact shape. Technology impact household_party_journeys is none: named accompaniment and recovery need consent and real resources, with ordinary travel and settlement gates preserved. The independent household-journey-harbor.json and household-journey-upland.json ordinary imports contrast a consenting adult couple with a parent and dependent while another adult declines, using different destinations and fares.

Named civilian camp places

A mobile_camp declaration references an existing civilian personal-admission track and names a separate finite companion capacity, family term and host policy. Actual local named consent precedes a request; a request reserves companion places without granting movement. Living current-commander acceptance revalidates exact original command generation, migration, people and remaining term. The effective minimum capacity is shared across requested and active bindings for the same host, separately from the protagonist's personal place. Family acceptance never creates a principal, demographic person, soldier, vote, resource or right of settlement.

Only the existing civilian migration owns camp motion. The field host may move, split or be destroyed while a surviving camp and its named civilians retain their own real position. Command change, expiry, lost migration, death, custody and incompatible commitments preserve last actually reached checkpoints. Dependent separation includes the exact caregiver and dependent group; no replacement care is guessed. Renewed personal adult or parent-care consent at a currently eligible camp is a separate rejoin_person review with a fresh place check. Reaching adulthood ends the prior parent permission.

Family refusal preserves existing solo admission. If an independently admitted adult continues after family carriage ends, their original roster row is released to that one personal location owner, and family places release. The named adapter and personal adapter share checkpoint projection before personal closure, so one person cannot move with both an obsolete family binding and a continuing individual carrier. Explicit whole-party withdrawal and the protagonist's continuing dependent-care obligation end their personal carriage at the actual checkpoint through the existing transaction. Other named survivors stay where they actually stopped. The next separately funded camp_return requires personal admission to have ended and names every included and excluded person.

Personal survivor recovery

After succession, only a current adult protagonist already present in the original stranded roster can explicitly review survivor_return. A formerly dependent survivor must reach adulthood and explicitly consent personally; the former parent permission does not become adult consent. That new road names self and the existing dependent-care group, renews only their own consent, prices current ordinary road resources, and retains the original carrier identity. An absent heir, another adult or a newly generated household member cannot be enrolled. The ordinary player travel record is the sole route and clock. Interrupted recovery preserves its next real checkpoint and pays anew. Original ordinary journey settlement remains selective under its existing work and residence gates; family camp terms themselves grant no work, settlement or refuge entitlement.

Version-3 camp records retain association fields and add a schema-2 journey with exact original personal-admission, host, migration, leader, generation, acceptance and deadline provenance. The optional recovery child names the new personal road without replacing that original carrier. Ordinary version-2 records upgrade to version 3 only for survivor recovery, preserving their schema-1 journey and original carrier. Matching restore validates finite places, actual people and parents, exclusive location claims and exact travel references without accepting, moving, charging or enrolling anyone. Association-only version 1 remains unchanged. Both new capabilities use the none technology decisions above and retain all ordinary gates.

Temporary shelter composition

A household_refuge declaration can name an existing party without granting carriage or reusing association consent as shelter consent. Each adult or actual dependent caregiver decides again for the exact request. The existing named-party county carrier supplies actual arrival, separation, interruption and priced return; refuge entry is a separate local site transaction after arrival. It changes no permanent residence, roster, demographic population, custody or property. Ended protective terms leave admitted people physically present until an actual ordinary movement.
