Carolingian inheritance and diplomacy

The selectable 800 bookmark attaches divisio_800, ordinatio_800, gifts_800, recognition_800 and verdun_800 before its four ordinary campaigns. data/bookmark_800_history.js owns the opening royal courts, opportunity windows and compressed region definitions. js/story_settlements.js supplies five content-free types; data/story_instances.js composes their declarations into the shared registry. js/royal_settlements.js supplies bookmark-neutral branch linking, heir designation, complete regional grants and release. Shared world code calls these helpers at ordinary succession, birth and death boundaries without naming the bookmark or story.

Opening family

Only people already born in 800 are declared. Charlemagne's sons govern their existing courts as single characters. The imperial genealogy borrows exact member references from those courts; it does not manufacture another Louis or Pippin. Their birth courts own mortality and compact-member materialization until absorption. Derived reverse links keep borrowed records current after births, deaths and compaction and rebuild through ordinary dynasty repair on restore. Absorption moves the heir's character and surviving household into the senior genealogy, keeps historical source tombstones, reparents regional subjects and conserves treasury and technology. See realms.md and state-and-saves.md.

Inheritance settlements

The 806-813 window can create a plan while Charlemagne, at least two sons and a subordinate regional crown survive. A plan stamps the current ruler, designated member and regional beneficiaries. It changes no owner or holder. A dead designated son can be replaced by a surviving direct son while the father still rules. After natural accession, surviving original regional rulers can receive independence; an active senior war delays this. A beneficiary who died, lost the crown or inherited the senior realm receives no separate award. This compresses the 806 geography into the already governed regional crowns.

The 817-823 window requires the actual Louis to have inherited. His oldest surviving son is designated senior successor. Younger surviving sons receive Aquitaine and, if another son exists, Bavaria. The grants move whole direct-subject hierarchies and retain imperial sovereignty. They do not depose an unrelated Italian king or schedule a future birth. A changed capital or unrelated imperial successor closes the opportunity.

After the senior succession, regional sibling pressure builds over four season reviews and can produce one independence war through the ordinary war registry. Existing wars and truces block that opening. Hosts, battles, defeat and ordinary peace remain authoritative. The story records the commitment rather than creating substitute armies or granting war objectives.

The 843-846 window can negotiate western and eastern shares while the surviving designated senior and two original regional beneficiaries rule. The middle share retains the senior court. Only wars among those three parties can be concluded by the agreement; foreign wars and holy wars block it. The grant quote requires complete current subject territories, distinct shares and current imperial ownership. An independent sibling keeps already held territory. The agreement closes participating wars through ordinary white peace, moves whole local subject hierarchies, releases subordinate crowns and creates two-year truces. Foreign-held counties are excluded, and the senior capital remains in the middle share. Missing or dead parties cause a different campaign, never a fabricated three-way treaty.

Embassies and recognition

Frankish-Abbasid gifts require the original living rulers, sovereign courts, their capitals, peace and sufficient treasury funds. Each side transfers its own declared amount exactly once. The two-year peace uses the existing realm-pair truce ledger, not a defensive alliance. Eastern recognition requires the original western ruler, an independent living eastern court, both capitals, the existing imperial title and participating control of the Adriatic cells. An agreement records imperial_commission recognition in the existing authority ledger and returns participating western-held coastal cells through the territory transaction. Recognition follows the authority ledger's existing issuer-succession validity. The story's concluded record is historical and never creates a replacement mandate on reload.

Negotiation and presentation

Each eligible opportunity receives one seeded 65 percent autonomous negotiation attempt per season, in the original settlement order. Player-controlled parties must first authorize the reviewed terms; changed parties or shares invalidate their authorization. Date windows open eligibility and close expired opportunities; they guarantee no settlement. Quotes read ids, generations, current ownership, holders, money and any participating war ids. apply rechecks the complete stamp before a payment or award. The shared Deeds card and story sheet expose localized opportunity labels and outcomes. Durable news uses literal message descriptors; saves contain no rendered prose. No new screen or control pattern is introduced.

Persistence and limits

Five independently typed records store phase, result, turn, attemptSeason, plan and approvals. divisio_800 retains the opening cast; verdun_800 retains pressure and its war id. Resolved agreements never reopen. Restore consumes saved state without calling init, reauthoring courts, paying gifts or reinstating recognition. Older 800 saves without this story remain unchanged. Existing bundled branch saves migrate on the detached copy by splitting opportunities into these five records and retaining cast, plans and pressure. Mixed old/new records or malformed source records fail before adoption. Settlement helpers remain trusted engine operations; the instance catalogue is authored through the storyInstances JSON root with registered settlement types. Exact historical clauses, object gifts, Gregorian dates and every 843 boundary remain editorial abstractions. See ../research/800-bookmark.md for sources.
