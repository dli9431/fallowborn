Adopted dynastic public treasuries

An ordinary nonmobile dynastic realm may declare publicTreasury with exactly schema:1. This opts its existing-ruler starting adoption into separate public cash without changing its government, legal property, liege, incumbent, private station or ordinary office eligibility. Omission retains the unified dynastic purse. Elective and delegated institutions retain their existing contracts and cannot declare this option. The effective bookmark revision's complete realm declaration is the owner; ordinary JSON imports use the same pure validator.

The public opening balance is the original realm's existing treasury object, moved once to realms.player.treasury. Explicit-inventory worlds adopt first and initialize that same account once from the final fiscal inventory using the ordinary opening reserve formula. The private opening balance is scenario.gold. No sum is credited twice, no opening cash amount is added to the schema, and an empty public account never falls back to private cash.

All existing dynastic landed fiscal receipts (including the ruler's direct hereditary settlements), vassal dues, county remittances, building and modifier upkeep, administration and court costs retain the existing player fiscal calculation and settle once into this public account. This is fiscal separation of the existing landed domain, not a change to legal settlement ownership. Household holdings, enterprises, work, personal rewards and private gifts remain personal. Household necessities and death dues charge private cash. Existing office salaries paid to the protagonist use an explicit private endpoint; public office commitments charge their actual institution. There is no automatic public-to-private distribution or ruler allowance.

The ordinary treasury snapshot continues to exclude the player from NPC seasonal settlement. treasuryDynasticSeason owns the public part of the existing player season, including any pre-adoption accrued military bill, and stamps it once. Current player hosts spend public cash daily through the existing military owner; their daily costs never accrue a second seasonal bill. Funds reserved for old unpaid military costs remain unavailable. Revaluation, grants, provision receipts and manual public distributions use the same canonical account. Autonomous NPC surplus distribution does not take over the player's decisions.

treasurySeparated identifies a genuine separate player public account independently of the elective-government predicate. treasuryPublicAccountSnapshot exposes only a live account, never player.gold. treasuryFundingTransfer distinguishes an explicit public source from the protagonist's explicit private source. treasuryPayPrivate transfers an actual payer's available cash into the household purse, including a salary from the player's public institution. These low-level transactions supply no consent; existing reviewed owners retain that responsibility. Staged expedition formation publishes realms, which owns both the public debit and provision-provider credit; it never changes the private purse.

Optional start.publicTreasury retains schema:1, originalRealmId, realmId and retirement. Its original owner must match the selected effective realm declaration. The current alias is player, or the same original realm after an ordinary court release. retirement is null while active, otherwise exactly turn and recipientId. The canonical treasury carries publicOwnerId equal to the original declaration id. Succession retains this account independently of the protagonist. Court release rebinds its alias without copying cash or old military costs. Retirement transfers through the existing canonical retirement transaction, zeros the old balance and accrual, and records terminal provenance. A terminal account may subsequently disappear with its former realm; it is never reconstructed or restocked.

Detached save validation runs before treasury repair. Active provenance requires exactly one matching canonical account and finite signed cash, nonnegative accrued military costs and valid accounting clocks. Missing, erased, duplicated, forged or mismatched active accounts reject. Retired provenance cannot expose spendable cash. Unopted starts forbid the optional record and retain their previous save meanings. Restore pays nothing, changes no alias, and grants no consent, host, wages or opening reserve.

This identity and accounting capability has technology impact none. Existing unit, market, building, travel, home-authority and government gates still apply. The shared realm sheet displays public available cash; the household forecast excludes the public landed cash flow. Imported declarations contain no executable fiscal strategy. Runtime conversion into a new preserved account, authorable opening money and automatic ruler allowances are unsupported.
