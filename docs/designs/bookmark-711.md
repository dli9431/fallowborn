Across the Strait: spring 711 bookmark

Status

The 476 branch registers this data-only definition in FBDATA.bookmarks during ordinary boot. New Game, start codes and save loading can select 711 without a console setup. The authored definition remains in bookmarkCandidates for the existing static localization extractor, and the live registry references that same definition. Its supplemental culture, faith and site tables install before engine indexing. Selectability does not resolve the historical and capability gaps listed below.

Ownership and presentation

All 498 europe_regional cells have independent literal rows, including the existing impassable cells. Geography supplies only seeds and terrain. The candidate declares its own 69 courts, owners, communities, d_711_, k_711_ and e_711_ title groups, straits and local climates. Territory belongs to each county's declared realm; sovereign colors follow explicit liege chains and the territorial holder strategy assigns the usual generated dukes and counts. Shared geography, accepted bookmark objects and their communities are never remapped at runtime.

The opening precedes the Iberian conquest. Roderic holds Toledo and most of Hispania, Achila's rival court holds the northeast and Septimania, and neither Asturias nor an Andalusi emirate exists at creation. The Umayyad sovereign rules at Damascus above the Ifriqiyan, Tangier, Egyptian, Iraqi and Khurasanian provincial courts. The Roman pontificate is a landless ecclesiastical court subordinate to the eastern sovereign, with Rome remaining eastern territory. The county-based tax and liege model cannot express every historical provincial relationship; these approximations require review.

Every inhabited county has one explicit head presentation and a distinct 711 site id. Other slots remain ordinary procedural settlements. Later urban foundations use contemporary district or predecessor names, including the Asturian uplands, Iria Flavia, al-Mada'in, Brahmanabad and Volubilis. Many outer-region coordinates anchor a district to the shared county seed and do not claim an excavated settlement site. Community shares and development values are gameplay estimates, not a reconstructed census. docs/research/711-bookmark.md records the historical evidence and uncertain boundaries.

Tarsos and Adana keep their authored Armenian and Eastern Christian principal identity in both the county fields and the first community. The community shares remain 7000, 2500 and 500. Matching those declarations satisfies the ordinary bookmark validation gate and makes the principal community usable for household starts and saved campaigns. The regional ownership and minority identities retain their existing definitions.

Institutions and starts

The early_medieval era supplies territorial government, historical technology seeding, existing peasant, farmer and craft scenarios and their ordinary progression locks. No named-player opening is added without a separate owner choice. The candidate disables great holy wars and tournaments. Papacy remains faith-scoped and its institutional dates govern the later college and investiture machinery.

The umayyad_sunni_711 faith inherits Sunni doctrine but names the independent umayyad_caliph_711 head office. Its normal caliphal_claim strategy recognizes the Umayyad realm and uses Damascus or the combined Mecca/Medina possession for claims. This is an allegiance definition, not a proposed eighth-century theological school. Hindu and Buddhist frontier communities are minimal separate roots with no central head, using existing ordinary marriage and legal strategies. The Sindhi culture uses its own name pools with the Persian parent for shared regional game affinities. These supplements install at data-load time without changing earlier bookmark records.

Conditional campaigns

data/story_campaigns_711.js supplies four instances of the registered campaign type. The existing data-registration boundary registers them before story sealing; only 711 lists them, so they attach nothing and consume no campaign RNG in other bookmarks. The crossing contests Malaga in 711-714 while the Umayyads hold Tangier. Malaga is the grid's proxy for the southern landing region, not a claim that Gibraltar lies at Malaga. The Toledo opportunity opens in 714-718 only if the coastal foothold survives. Narbonne opens in 719-725 after actual possession of Toledo; the Aquitanian opportunity opens in 725-732 after actual possession of Narbonne.

The windows are bounded gameplay opportunities rather than exact historical invasion dates. The original Toulouse attack was in 721; this later review opportunity leaves time for the ordinary Narbonne campaign and does not recreate that battle or guarantee a march to Tours. An unmet ownership prerequisite resolves divergence when the opportunity is checked; it does not wait for that prerequisite to become true later. Busy courts wait within their window. The owner must review this coarse sequencing before acceptance.

Each opportunity starts an ordinary war. No date transfers a county, manufactures a host, kills Roderic, destroys a surviving Gothic court or converts its communities. Victory, defeat, third-party control, succession and timeout use the existing campaign lifecycle. Saved records hold semantic ids and turn stamps; restore does not replay campaigns or pay rewards. The shared story card and reviewed choice sheet keep the existing keyboard, mobile and return-state behavior.

Conditional Asturian foundation

The same data file declares asturias_foundation_711 using the reusable polity_foundation type in js/story_polity_foundation.js. Asturias and Pelayo are absent at creation. Between 718 and 730, actual Umayyad possession of Toledo can open a northern foundation while at least half the Asturian uplands' population remains Catholic. If the uplands are still Gothic-owned, a peaceful remnant can establish Pelayo's independent court in that county. Busy Gothic courts, occupied uplands and armed local revolts wait. Surviving Gothic possessions outside the county retain their existing owners and ruler; neither Roderic's death nor the extinction of his surviving court is required or scripted. Loss of Toledo is the coarse collapse predicate, not proof that every Gothic institution has vanished.

If the Umayyads own the uplands, the opportunity observes an ordinary armed county revolt. It does not lower support, bypass warnings, muster extra troops, guarantee siege success or create an independent realm while the revolt is unresolved. The existing revolt settlement calls a generic registered-story hook only after every joined county is occupied by rebels with a surviving host. Pelayo then heads the actually won connected component containing the uplands. Defeat, local concessions, changed northern control, recovery of Toledo or a 1080-day commitment timeout closes the opportunity; the underlying ordinary revolt retains its own settlement and military outcomes. A healthy conquered county can remain Umayyad and never form Asturias.

The 718-730 opening and three-year revolt commitment are bounded gameplay windows, not asserted historical event dates. The oviedo county remains the Asturian uplands; it does not claim an eighth-century city of Oviedo. Pelayo's birth year 685, skills and generated family are game-scale estimates. Foundation uses the shared territorial transfer, treasury, technology inheritance and royal-court transactions, preserving communities and excluding player ownership or a player-controlled holder chain. Existing-ruler starts resolve their authored political ids through the ordinary player mapping. Terminal records never recreate a lost Asturias or restore Pelayo to a successor's throne. Older 711 saves missing this story gain only an additive pending record, without foundation effects during restore.

Technology impact

period_711_crossing, period_711_toledo, period_711_septimania and period_711_gaul each declare none. These are independently authored political opportunities with no credible discovery prerequisite. Existing military transport, supply and equipment rules remain in force. The data pass adds no new army, religious-head or government capability.

conditional_polity_foundation declares none. Local political independence after a collapse or victorious uprising is a baseline capability governed by possession, community continuity and ordinary military outcomes. No credible discovery should block it. The foundation adds no troop, equipment or government strategy and grants no technology beyond the ordinary inheritance from its former sovereign.

Acceptance gaps

Fresh Gothic courts use the separately registered crown_election strategy and shared selection state. Existing saved governments remain unchanged. Bookmark data must not implement elections with date-based transfers.

Asturias now has a conditional foundation rather than an opening kingdom or unconditional historical conquest. The loss-of-Toledo predicate, peaceful remnant route, revolt route and game-scale Pelayo court remain explicit gameplay approximations requiring historical and natural-play review.

The outer-region tribal aggregates, Cyprus condominium proxy, northeastern Gothic boundary, provincial relationships, game-scaled rulers and ordinary papal election behavior need historical and gameplay acceptance. The Hindu and Buddhist roots deliberately omit specialized institutional progression. Those differences must remain visible in review. Separate authored regressions cover one season of observer simulation and ordinary household play with natural events, any death or succession interruptions and midpoint/final saves. Natural long-term succession and annual mortality require separate review. Owner execution, mobile appearance and distribution-package acceptance remain review steps.
