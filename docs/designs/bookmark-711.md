Across the Strait: spring 711 review candidate

Status

This is a hidden, data-only candidate. data/bookmark_711.js stores the atomic definition in FBDATA.bookmarkCandidates['711'], not FBDATA.bookmarks. The title picker, ordinary start codes and ordinary save loading cannot select it. Its supplemental culture, faith and site tables remain inert during accepted campaigns. No js/ source is changed. There is no general hidden-bookmark flag on this platform, so adding hidden:true to an accepted registry entry would not actually hide it.

Ownership and presentation

All 498 europe_regional cells have independent literal rows, including the existing impassable cells. Geography supplies only seeds and terrain. The candidate declares its own 69 courts, owners, communities, d_711_, k_711_ and e_711_ title groups, straits and local climates. Territory belongs to each county's declared realm; sovereign colors follow explicit liege chains and the territorial holder strategy assigns the usual generated dukes and counts. Shared geography, accepted bookmark objects and their communities are never remapped at runtime.

The opening precedes the Iberian conquest. Roderic holds Toledo and most of Hispania, Achila's rival court holds the northeast and Septimania, and neither Asturias nor an Andalusi emirate exists at creation. The Umayyad sovereign rules at Damascus above the Ifriqiyan, Tangier, Egyptian, Iraqi and Khurasanian provincial courts. The Roman pontificate is a landless ecclesiastical court subordinate to the eastern sovereign, with Rome remaining eastern territory. The county-based tax and liege model cannot express every historical provincial relationship; these approximations require review.

Every inhabited county has one explicit head presentation and a distinct 711 site id. Other slots remain ordinary procedural settlements. Later urban foundations use contemporary district or predecessor names, including the Asturian uplands, Iria Flavia, al-Mada'in, Brahmanabad and Volubilis. Many outer-region coordinates anchor a district to the shared county seed and do not claim an excavated settlement site. Community shares and development values are gameplay estimates, not a reconstructed census. docs/research/711-bookmark.md records the historical evidence and uncertain boundaries.

Institutions and starts

The early_medieval era supplies territorial government, historical technology seeding, existing peasant, farmer and craft scenarios and their ordinary progression locks. No named-player opening is added without a separate owner choice. The candidate disables great holy wars and tournaments. Papacy remains faith-scoped and its institutional dates govern the later college and investiture machinery.

The inert umayyad_sunni_711 faith inherits Sunni doctrine but names the independent umayyad_caliph_711 head office. Its normal caliphal_claim strategy recognizes the Umayyad realm and uses Damascus or the combined Mecca/Medina possession for claims. This is an allegiance definition, not a proposed eighth-century theological school. Hindu and Buddhist frontier communities are minimal separate roots with no central head, using existing ordinary marriage and legal strategies. The Sindhi culture uses its own name pools with the Persian parent for shared regional game affinities. These supplements install only for candidate review.

Conditional campaigns

data/story_campaigns_711.js supplies four instances of the registered campaign type. The existing data-registration boundary registers them before story sealing; accepted bookmarks list none of them, so they attach nothing and consume no campaign RNG there. The crossing contests Malaga in 711-714 while the Umayyads hold Tangier. Malaga is the grid's proxy for the southern landing region, not a claim that Gibraltar lies at Malaga. The Toledo opportunity opens in 714-718 only if the coastal foothold survives. Narbonne opens in 719-725 after actual possession of Toledo; the Aquitanian opportunity opens in 725-732 after actual possession of Narbonne.

The windows are bounded gameplay opportunities rather than exact historical invasion dates. The original Toulouse attack was in 721; this later review opportunity leaves time for the ordinary Narbonne campaign and does not recreate that battle or guarantee a march to Tours. An unmet ownership prerequisite resolves divergence when the opportunity is checked; it does not wait for that prerequisite to become true later. Busy courts wait within their window. The owner must review this coarse sequencing before acceptance.

Each opportunity starts an ordinary war. No date transfers a county, manufactures a host, kills Roderic, destroys a surviving Gothic court or converts its communities. Victory, defeat, third-party control, succession and timeout use the existing campaign lifecycle. Saved records hold semantic ids and turn stamps; restore does not replay campaigns or pay rewards. The shared story card and reviewed choice sheet keep the existing keyboard, mobile and return-state behavior.

Technology impact

period_711_crossing, period_711_toledo, period_711_septimania and period_711_gaul each declare none. These are independently authored political opportunities with no credible discovery prerequisite. Existing military transport, supply and equipment rules remain in force. The data pass adds no new army, religious-head or government capability.

Acceptance gaps

Visigothic kingship still uses territorial/dynastic succession. The elective and contested monarchy requires a separately registered succession strategy if the owner wants that distinction modeled. Bookmark data must not implement elections with date-based transfers.

The existing campaign factory cannot conditionally create a new Asturian polity or attach a named Pelayo court after the Gothic collapse. Asturias is therefore absent from the opening and no misleading pre-created kingdom or unconditional scripted conquest substitutes for its emergence. A generic conditional polity-foundation story type is a platform dependency before that requirement can be fulfilled without js/ changes.

The outer-region tribal aggregates, Cyprus condominium proxy, northeastern Gothic boundary, provincial relationships, game-scaled rulers and ordinary papal election behavior need historical and gameplay acceptance. The Hindu and Buddhist roots deliberately omit specialized institutional progression. Those differences must remain visible in review. Ten years of observer simulation is covered by an authored regression; full natural household play, mobile appearance and distribution-package acceptance remain owner review steps.

Review setup and promotion

The tests install the candidate in a fresh browser context after ordinary boot. For an owner-controlled local review, the equivalent console setup is Object.assign(FBDATA.cultures, FBDATA.bookmarkCultures711); Object.assign(FBDATA.religions, FBDATA.bookmarkReligions711); Object.assign(FBDATA.settlementSites, FBDATA.bookmarkSites711); FBDATA.bookmarks['711'] = FBDATA.bookmarkCandidates['711']; FB.invalidateCultureData(); FB.invalidateReligionData(). Then use Choose a Starting Date or a 711 start code. This setup is transient and must be repeated before loading a candidate save after a fresh page load.

Publication requires resolving or explicitly accepting the gaps, the requirement check, and owner review. Only then should integration install the supplemental catalogues and put the definition in FBDATA.bookmarks. Until then, do not merge the review setup into ordinary boot. Feature work leaves FB.VERSION, FB.CHANGELOG and generated language catalogues untouched.

Localization and regression coverage

The static extractor reads literal candidate labels, the supplemental culture and faiths, head title, settlement names and all campaign outcome messages. Its stable namespaces are bookmark.711, realm.711, province.711, settlement.711, the canonical title ids, culture.sindhi_711, religion.umayyad_sunni_711 and storyCampaign instance ids. Historical personal names are not translation keys. No catalogue regeneration is part of this branch.

tests/e2e/specs/711-bookmark.spec.js covers hidden status, data isolation, review activation, the full political grid, communities, head claims, dated papal institutions, commoner starts in both distribution flag modes, save round trips, campaign divergence and ten years of natural world simulation. tests/e2e/specs/stories.spec.js includes the expanded registration order. tools/test_i18n_catalog.py covers the new extraction owners. Follow the repository's owner-controlled testing workflow.
