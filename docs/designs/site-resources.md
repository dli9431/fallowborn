Site resources

The physical resource owner supplies opt-in typed capacity and conserved reservations. Flow, storage and access are distinct: flow is an allocation available per declared campaign period, storage contains only water actually transferred into it, and access counts places for grazing or launching. Profiles and dated site assignments are declarations, never deductions from terrain, development, a national technology or a settlement name. The bundled profile table is empty and no bundled world is assigned a source by this capability.

Authority and conservation

A source binds one stable county and physical site to one profile, an explicit suitability declaration and a single rights policy. The holder policy uses ordinary settlement property authority, including the existing institutional-property boundary. A permission policy instead requires the exact declared existing civil office in the actual holding institution and an effective current appointment with that county or realm scope. The resource declaration explicitly permits that office only at this source; it grants no appointment, land or wider right. A military-only Ban does not qualify. Adoption resolves institution aliases through the existing start owner. Death, custody, changed incumbent, revoked appointment, expiry, capture, occupation and siege prevent new consumption immediately.

The same quote and commit API serves player reviews and trusted AI consumers. A reservation binds a concrete living person's identity and, for a realm actor, its incumbent generation. A successor cannot use it. Its original eligible person or the current authorized holder may release it without producing anything. Consumption requires the original actor and current resource authority. Competing and stale reviews change nothing, including RNG, uid and every unrelated owner. Resource actions have no money charge and award no building, goods, income, population, military supply or operating benefit. AI has no speculative automatic demand or source allocation; a consuming system must request an actual reviewed allocation through this API.

Flow and access capacity use floor(campaign turn / periodDays), anchored at campaign turn zero. At a boundary, their spent capacity and outstanding reservations expire rather than accumulate. Projection of a new period is pure; only a successful transaction records it. Storage has no renewal period and starts empty, including a declared catchment tank. Its stock and reservations persist. A transfer consumes one actual reservation and credits only a storage destination whose connectedSourceId names that source, with the same unit, sufficient space and current authority at both ends. The connected edge declares an existing physical connection, not an instruction to construct a conduit or transport goods remotely. There is no arbitrary deposit, stock initialization, cash conversion, weather simulation or off-map supplier. Facility operation, its route/work/input costs and downstream benefits belong to their own transactions.

Definitions and bounded compilation

Public siteResourceProfiles are keyed by stable ids. Effective bookmark and whole-revision siteResourceAssignments arrays declare actual sites; a missing field inherits and an explicit empty array opts out. Detached validation resolves the same-import profiles, geography, settlement presentations, courts and office definitions before publication. It rejects unknown fields, wrong units/periods, negative or fractional amounts, scenery or foreign sites, repeated site ownership, duplicate coordinates for the same resource unit, conflicting rights, unsupported suitability and connected-source cycles. It uses no active map, people, RNG or uid. Runtime indexes derive from the accepted campaign revision and exact mod signature; no compiled registry enters saves. There are at most 256 profiles, 256 assigned sources, 64 outstanding reservations per source, 1,000,000 units per capacity and 360 days per renewable period. Device and memory acceptance still requires owner measurements.

Technology decision

site_resource_claims is none. Baseline lawful allocation, empty storage and conserved receipt of water need no credible research dependency. Ordinary facility, route, transport and advanced-operation technology gates remain with their owning systems. Reserving launching or grazing capacity does not supply a ship, pasture agreement, herd or mobile provisioning permission.

Groundwater suitability

The optional boolean groundwater flag is allowed only on an actual source:true water flow. It distinguishes an explicitly authored aquifer intake from an ordinary river or catchment without creating capacity, storage or rights. An operation may require this flag and gradient through sourceRequirements. No bundled source receives it automatically.
