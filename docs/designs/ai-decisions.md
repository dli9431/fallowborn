AI decision authoring

Scope

Live religious choices read holy-war strength/rank/trait weights, forecast participation probabilities, failure memory and target weights, prospective vow/desire chances, papal election/investiture/sanction/legation preferences and conversion scoring. Existing vows are normalized without reseeding; deterministic compatibility vow repair retains its historical hash mapping. Existing faith/head/date/authority/ground rules, saved promises, campaign caps, election round rules and canonical conversion/appointment transactions remain fixed or under their existing typed profile owners. Ruler conversion chance/share/threshold reuse realm-scoped balance. Optional religious target ordering applies only to currently eligible later-call targets and conversion options; inaugural target ordering remains authored campaign content.

Government variants read bounded agreement, service, settlement, office, negotiation and recruit willingness. Deterministic zero/one willingness consumes no extra RNG; intermediate authored probabilities draw only at that prospective AI choice. Mobile legacy trade/recruit sizes and food horizons, candidate diplomacy/martial weights, aristocratic concession size and civic reserve/aid/peace preferences are declared. Existing mobile supply policies retain their own finite logistics owner. Optional government mode ordering affects mobile leaders, public-selection candidates, eligible administrative/local religious appointments and provincial recognition. Facts without a meaning in a particular candidate class are zero. Constitutional eligibility, consent, mandatory incumbent/vacancy synchronization, unpaid-obligation maintenance and direct player choices remain canonical; disabled ordinary-war strategies stay disabled. Civic elections, institutional succession sources, parliamentary postures, historical foundation eligibility and profile-owned budgets retain their existing declared strategies/data rather than a second generic algorithm.

Live intrigue uses declared baseWeight, minimumWeight, chanceRate and maximumChance, optional hostile schemeOrder, and optional actor/target ordering. Default trait scheme order, weight/id actor sorting and canonical target order remain. New selection never bypasses target eligibility, duplicate-target, cooldown, motive or player-facing/global workload limits. The latter retain the existing typed intrigue data and engine ceilings. Diplomacy uses declared alliance opening, optional partner/cultivation/rebel/player-approach ordering, cultivation multipliers and a bounded rebel-support cap. Optional aimOrder references registered agencyRules aims and breaks trait-weight ties after the existing security/independence gates. Existing agencyRules remains owner of cadence, promises, marriage terms, aim values and rebel payments/duration. Court stance slopes/postures remain their existing data, with declared martial/age/economic clamps, relationship scaling and aim contribution multipliers. All-neutral values retain prior calculations and draws.

Live sovereign research scoring reads all declared exposure, historical timing, horizon, warfare/rank/coastal, transport and unlock multipliers. Optional research-only domainWeights maps effective technology domains to [0,4], independently of existing trait researchWeights. Slot filling consumes the selected mode and candidate facts after canonical prerequisite checks. Neutral weighted selection retains score/id order and the former subtractive RNG sampler, including boundary behavior; zero weighted candidates stop selection. Player research automation keeps its own choice path and slots, progress and diffusion remain canonical.

Live investment consumes policy parameters for county/fort priorities, development preference, aiBuildOrder weighting, measurable benefit/cost/upkeep preference, profile score weight, private-baron preference and whether to fund new operating terms. Optional investment-only buildingRoleWeights maps registered building roles to finite [-100,100] preferences; unknown roles reject. Annual building and profile ordering retain their stable legacy ties by default. Private barons retain their original uniform paid-build choice unless baronPreference is one. New seasonal/water operations may be withheld by operationSpending zero without changing an existing paid receipt. aiMaxBuildingsPerYear and distributionSurplusRate use existing realm-scoped balance rather than duplicate policy fields; explicit zero construction is respected. Reserves, affordability, applicability, materials, technology, authority and real invoices remain binding.

Live army consumers share aiTacticsPolicy engagementMargin for non-player voluntary pursuit and coordinated advances. objectiveFirst (integer zero/one) optionally advances feasible ordinary objectives before primary-host pursuit; default zero keeps the previous order. First mode retains existing distance/objective candidate order. Ordered/weighted policies may reorder candidate counties by projected distance, objective and actual attack/defense power, while canonical route, fort and siege-feasibility checks still run before use. Same order-phase projections are discarded before movement. Player commands/automation margins, forced routs, supply/fiscal recovery and expedition ownership keep their original boundaries. No retreat or supply threshold is made optional by a risk preference. The bounded disposition contract is below.

Live campaign consumers use aiWarPolicy for consideration, strength margin, maximum offensive commitments and aggression appetite, with explicit government warChance overriding the policy. The legacy bellicose field retains its exact effect. New ordinary targets use aiExpansionPolicy with legal candidates and one batched regional fact projection. The default strategic_expansion ranks geographic consolidation within a binding lawful-first tier; ordinary_expansion retains the former aggression ascending/id baseline for explicit neutral configurations. Ordered selection adds no RNG draw. Custom weighted choices draw once. Raids keep one uniform target draw by default; aiRaidTargetDevelopmentWeight in [0,100] optionally weights developed counties. aiRaidChance and breakawayChance now use the ordinary realm-scoped balance reader. Explicit zero raid chance is respected instead of falling through the former logical-or default. Ownership, legal checks and financial transactions remain canonical.

AI choices have separate candidate enumeration, canonical eligibility, pure facts, preference evaluation, selection and apply steps. Authorable preferences never supply legal permission, resources, ownership, technology, physical reach or combat power. Trusted engine modules retain those algorithms and transactions. The live consumers described above now use the shared policy API. No additional scheduler, autonomous action or strategic planner is installed by loading it.

Public policy contract

FBDATA.aiDecisionPolicies is a table keyed by stable lowercase snake_case id. Each complete record has family, mode, parameters, order, weights, bias, tieBreak and adjustments. Families are war, expansion, tactics, investment, research, intrigue, diplomacy, religion and government. The trusted engine defines each family's balance selection key, supported numeric facts and bounded parameter names. Definitions select first, ordered or weighted algorithms; JSON never registers algorithms, functions, expressions, state paths or recursive conditions. Adding a new fact or parameter requires an engine protocol extension and its documented real consumer.

Policy ids are selected through aiWarPolicy, aiExpansionPolicy, aiTacticsPolicy, aiInvestmentPolicy, aiResearchPolicy, aiIntriguePolicy, aiDiplomacyPolicy, aiReligionPolicy and aiGovernmentPolicy in balance. Selection uses the existing global, era, government-form, bookmark/revision, runtime-mod precedence through FB.balanceFor. Scalar parameters already owned by balance, agencyRules, traitMechanics or government strategyParameters keep those owners; do not add a duplicate setting here. A layer may omit a selection key. A declared unknown policy, wrong family or malformed policy is an error, never a fallback to another policy.

Parameters are complete bounded per-family records, listed by the trusted protocol and consumed at the documented live choice sites. Order is at most sixteen unique supported fact/direction records, where direction is asc or desc. Ordered mode compares these lexicographically. Weights are supported-fact numeric coefficients between -100 and 100; weighted score is max(0, bias + sum(fact * coefficient)), with finite fact/score limits. First mode keeps the owning consumer's retained input ordering. tieBreak is input or id; weighted mode retains input order for cumulative sampling. First requires empty order/weights and zero bias; ordered requires nonempty order and empty weights/zero bias; weighted requires empty order and at least one weight or positive bias. Unsupported fields reject.

Adjustments are at most thirty-two declared trait/aim selectors. Each has kind, id and multiply, naming supported parameter multipliers from zero through four. Validate ids against effective traits and agencyRules.aims, including same-import additions. Apply each matching selector once in declaration order, regardless of duplicate runtime trait ids; clamp and, for integer parameters, round once after combination. Do not infer a character or aim inside the generic evaluator. The owning caller supplies current trusted ruler inputs; character identity, succession and trait synchronization belong to their existing owners. Ordinary war and tactics declare the built-in dispositions below; ruler_investment declares the role and benefit preferences below. The other bundled policies have no trait/aim adjustments. A complete replacement policy can retain neutral behavior with an empty adjustments list.

Ruler inputs

FB.aiDecisions.inputs reads the current NPC character through realmRulerCharacterSnapshot, or the current player.charId for the player realm. A character's complete traits list, including an empty list, takes precedence over the compact ruler summary. Without a materialized NPC ruler, only the summary's declared trait is available; no person is created or random trait drawn. Missing or inactive realms and global collectors have empty inputs. The player has no NPC ruler aim. FB.traitIds supplies detached, unique trait ids in saved order to this projection and the traitMechanic, traitAimWeight and traitSchemeOrder readers. Duplicate saved ids therefore contribute once to summed channels as well as policy adjustments.

The agency owner retains one valid saved aim for the current generation, even when traits change. Missing, unknown or predecessor-generation aims are projected without writing state. Hostile-liege independence and family security precede traitMechanics.rulerAims; aim weights take the maximum across traits, and diplomacy aimOrder breaks equal positive weights before the retained aim order. Compact aim projection uses the existing summary's birth/age, trait and canonical consort/family snapshots. It no longer treats absence of a full character alone as evidence that the dynasty needs securing. Materialization preserves that single-trait projection; later full-character trait changes apply on the next input read. rulerAimSnapshot returns a detached record, including existing approach stamps. Ordinary ensureAgency and restore retain their existing initialization and repair ownership.

Legacy ruler.personality === bellicose is an explicit compatibility switch for the original ordinary-war consumers only. It is not a trait id, a ruler-generation default or an alias for ambitious, wrathful or brave. It still selects bellicoseChance 0.08 and bellicoseAppetite 1.5; built-in trait adjustments leave those two fields unchanged. Other personality values select ordinary chance/aggressionAppetite and do not imply a trait. Existing explicit government warChance, including zero, still wins. Authors wanting an adjustment to affect legacy rulers must also name the corresponding bellicose parameter. Succession replaces the ruler summary, so the old switch does not transfer; matching current and supported v3 saves retain it without converting people or drawing RNG. The shared strength margin honors the current aim on both paths. Restoring never changes existing campaigns or rerolls declarations; future decisions use the effective policy and current ruler. No new personality field, migration or save format is introduced.

Strategic expansion

Ordinary sovereign and vassal campaigns share one live target selector in realmCampaignAI. It inspects at most 2048 distinct border counties in stable id order after one adjacency pass over the deciding territory. A vassal's territory includes its own subtree, not its sovereign's sibling holdings. Defender strength is projected once per distinct opponent during enumeration. Legal claims form the preferred tier before policy evaluation, so custom first, ordered or weighted preferences cannot put aggression ahead of an available lawful candidate. Zero total weight in that tier declines the campaign without falling back to aggression. The stable county id is the final ordered tie-break and the stable input order for weighted sampling.

strategic_expansion orders consolidationBand ascending, core ascending, remaining ascending, contacts descending, defensiveStrength ascending and development descending. The first three facts reuse the existing regional projection: band 0 finishes a held duchy (or an effective customary claim outside the title map), 1 extends a held kingdom, 2 a held empire, 3 starts another duchy and 4 has no title region. Core favors the capital's kingdom/empire within that band; remaining counts the still-unheld counties in the relevant title. Contacts count actual adjacency to the deciding subtree. Strength is the current canonical territorial defense projection, not a promise of affordable troops. No extra full-map projection is performed per target.

Optional expansion-only aimOrders maps registered agencyRules aim ids to complete ordered metric lists. It is supported only in ordered mode, with the same one-to-sixteen unique metric/direction validation as order. The current aim replaces the base list when present; omitted aims retain order. Built-in strengthen_crown puts core first; amass_wealth puts development then defense first; keep_peace puts defense then contacts first. expand_realm and other aims retain the base consolidation order. These preferences use the canonical generation-aware inputs projection; they do not reapply trait aim weights or the war strength-margin adjustment. No second aim, character cache or saved policy record is created. Complete replacements without aimOrders remain supported, including ordinary_expansion's lawful/id baseline and existing weighted imports.

aiExpansionFacts reuses the older aiExpansionPriorityWithProfile fact calculation with one shared profile. aiExpansionPriority and aiExpansionTarget are retained compatibility queries, not competing live campaign selectors. aiExpansionProvince retains missing-objective reconstruction and legacy peace settlement ownership; changing those outcomes is outside prospective target ranking. The live selector does not call these single-target helpers repeatedly.

After choosing, discard enumeration rights/frontier/strength snapshots. Recheck the selected cause and current strength, rebuild lawful claims against that defender, and follow adjacency with a queue to freeze one connected package of at most 2048 claims. Detached rights cannot join it. A final uncached declaration preview rechecks ownership, exact rights, frontier connectivity, consent, government objectives, truces, alliances and current war law. Permission requests receive another fresh preview before registration. Existing hostile-vassal defiance rules remain canonical. Changed rights or defenders fail closed rather than silently converting the chosen cause into aggression or selecting a new opponent.

The independent ai-expansion-consolidation.json and ai-expansion-wealth.json fixtures use the ordinary Mods file importer. One orders consolidation and declares a wealth aim override; the other weights developed prizes and penalizes border contacts. ai-expansion-ranking.spec.js covers pure fact ordering, sovereign/vassal outcomes, both imports, detached weight variation and malformed nonpublication, stale causes/packages/authority, the candidate ceiling, matching restore and actual succession. This changes prospective preferences and deterministic outcomes, without adding eligibility or a technology requirement; no technology-impact ledger entry is needed.

Bounded tactical dispositions

ordinary_tactics keeps engagementMargin 1.1, objectiveFirst zero and first-mode ordering. Its declared brave multiplier is 0.9 (margin 0.99); craven is 1.2 (margin 1.32). These existing opposing dispositions express willingness to engage. Wrathful, patient, ambitious and other traits have no implicit bravery meaning. No current aim adjustment is declared, and trait aim-selection weights are not reapplied. Missing preferences are neutral; explicit zero multipliers remain supported. Matching selectors multiply once in declaration order, including both if a malformed or imported character carries both opposing traits, then clamp once to the existing [0.5,4] protocol bounds. Duplicate trait ids contribute once.

The pure policy report adds tactics.neutralMargin (the selected policy's unadjusted margin) and tactics.reason: risk_accepting, risk_guarded or risk_neutral compares the final margin with that base. These are diagnostic codes beside the existing matched-adjustment report, not saved prose or daily news. The daily order phase shares one resolved report per realm against fixed positions, then discards it before movement. Primary pursuit and ordinary objective advances use the same adjusted margin against actual attacking power and the sum of hostile defending power. Coordinated hosts use it on combined power only when co-located, including the next route leg. Separated hosts gather only along uncontested paths; distant strength cannot justify a lone engagement. Existing in-flight coordinated legs retain their commitment.

Recovery uses the selected policy's base margin: armyCanPursue's optional recovery argument, autoResupply, supplyRetreat, recent routs and treasury retrenchment bypass trait/aim margin adjustments. armyRegroupGoal explicitly requests that base safety check. Cached pursuit results distinguish recovery from voluntary checks. Supply search sets autoResupply before checking refill counties. Supply thresholds, real power, troop counts, market invoices, muster/reinforcement approvals and financial recovery clocks do not change. Forced retreat still seeks a legal home route or reachable friendly fallback and stands when trapped. Fort blocking, objective occupation, siege force and route feasibility remain binding. Player automation retains its stance margins; manual holds/routes, delegated player command and expedition order ownership retain their existing guards.

The default rulerTraits pool in data/map_data.js excludes brave and craven. Ordinary initial generated rulers therefore keep the neutral tactical margin; historical profiles and actual characters carrying either disposition use it, and later successors use their own current traits. Compact rulers fall back to their explicit trait without materialization. The ordinary JSON ai-tactics-bold.json fixture opts both dispositions into the existing complete rulerTraits replacement; ai-tactics-guarded.json leaves generation unchanged and supplies different margins and objective ordering. Neither imports a second personality system. Matching saves retain exact mod fingerprints, ruler generations, existing routes and resources; projections are recomputed without a decision replay or saved cache. This is preference tuning of existing actions, with no eligibility expansion or technology-impact ledger entry. Runtime balance and device acceptance require owner evidence.

Ordered/weighted policies receive those current facts and score through the same policy selector; zero-total weighted choices decline without spending or drawing. Evaluation and choice

FB.aiDecisions.resolve(state, realmId, family) returns a detached compiled policy selected for the current campaign and government. FB.aiDecisions.report(policy, candidates, inputs) accepts at most 2048 unique-id candidates with explicit eligible boolean and a plain facts record of supported finite numeric facts. It returns detached parameter values, matched adjustments, per-candidate facts and score components, eligible ordering and total weight. Missing required facts reject, not zero-fill. Ineligible candidates never receive a selectable weight. Unknown input keys, malformed metrics and duplicate ids reject before choice. Reporting never reads campaign resources, RNG or uid and cannot authorize apply.

FB.aiDecisions.pick(report, roll) is pure. First/ordered return the leading eligible id. Weighted selection requires an explicit roll in [0,1); zero total or no eligible candidates returns null. Simulation-only choose/orderCandidates adapters supply a seeded draw for authored weighted choices; willing samples prospective probabilities strictly between zero and one. These adapters are not preview APIs. They never persist reports or issue orders. Keeping legacy draw cadence, candidate order and rechecking current state is the consumer's responsibility. Stale reports are advisory and cannot replace canonical transaction validation.

Lifecycle and compatibility

World revisions can replace the whole balance field through the canonical revision resolver. An omitted revision balance inherits the root; an explicit empty table clears the root bookmark overrides for that revision. Its individual values still obey the ordinary global/era/government/bookmark/mod balance precedence. Retained-save readers select the stamped revision, so fresh revision tuning does not change the retained revision's policy selection. Validate every declared revision through the same balance and AI-reference rules.

Boot validates shared policy definitions and shared selection layers with catalogFaults(data,null,{worlds:false}); it does not resolve or validate unrelated world revisions. Normal import preparation uses the exhaustive default and inspects every declared bookmark/revision balance reference. Runtime world validation checks its own resolved balance through balanceLayers.problems. A broken reference in an unused world does not become a global mod-readiness error. Canonical revision shape and start failure isolation retain their existing owners.

Compile policies only when the effective definition table changes or an explicit invalidation occurs, never by scanning every policy for each candidate. Resolution reads current layered selection on every call; it must not cache a realm's trait, aim, campaign or form. Detached import preparation validates definitions and every declared selection in global balance, eras, government forms and all bookmark revisions without publishing or compiling against live state. Whole policy records replace by id; nested parameter maps and arrays do not merge. Null/deletion is unsupported; choose a valid disabled decision policy through supported zero parameters where the consumer permits it. Mod removal/replacement follows the existing ordered-stack/reload path and exact content fingerprints.

No new saved fields or save version are needed. Matching restore recomposes from the stamped campaign revision and matching mod stack; existing wars, routes, offers and resources remain unchanged. Explicit neutral choices retain prior ordering, probability and RNG cadence; ordinary war dispositions and strategic target ranking intentionally change prospective decisions and may therefore change later outcomes and RNG consumption. No extra draw is added by projection or ordered ranking. Explicit zero raid/construction values now disable those prospective choices, correcting the old logical-or fallback. Research projection scores saturate at the existing policy fact bound of one billion only for extreme authored combinations. Technology impact: none for this preference tuning; it creates no independently gateable action or gameplay eligibility. New consumer capability would require its own review.

Integrated authoring contract

The shipped declarations support contrasting combinations without a saved personality catalogue.
