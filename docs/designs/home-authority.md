Home decisions and expedition funding

Opted-in government forms declare homeAuthority schema 1 with capital or territory presence and an explicit delegation choice. New levies, appointments, grants, wars and discretionary public spending require the living incumbent locally or an existing effective civil appointment with that exact homePowers operation. A host commission cannot delegate these decisions. The canonical projection is pure, returns the actual acting person and appointment, and never creates a regent. Ordinary upkeep, debts and already committed tasks retain their original owners.

Technology impact is none independently for home_decision_presence, home_decision_delegation, expedition_public_funding and expedition_private_contributions. Presence, political consent, current appointments and actual accounts govern these baseline capabilities. No credible discovery belongs between a household and a voluntary contribution or between a court and ordinary administration.

Canonical boundaries

The common government consent adapter applies home presence to new levy, war and grant decisions while retaining the underlying form's constituency and ordinary eligibility. Both office appointment review paths and authorityAppoint share the appointment guard. Optional treasury purchases, public distributions, realm-owned construction and new fort projects use spend; private settlement works keep their ordinary owner. Existing treasury transfers, debt and military upkeep, ongoing wars, construction completion and accepted political settlement execution remain outside new-decision gates. An exact effective service contract can deliver only its already accepted site/recipient award during absence; its quote includes the current commitment and cannot grant another holding.

A local ruler is resolved through the current actual officeholder without materialization. A protagonist's raw journey or existing temporary carrier precedes permanent home. Mobile membership uses the actual camp and rejects ambiguous separated field command as proof of home presence. Other known people use their canonical residence. Unknown, moving, captive, dead, underage, unfree or recalled deputies cannot supply local civil power. Deputies always need the current issuer generation even if their ordinary office otherwise continues through succession. Return changes eligibility through actual location; no reinstatement record is created.

Funding is an explicit policy on an existing command declaration. The shared funding quote identifies the actual consenting person, optional exact deputy, source kind and account, destination, amount, current balances and accrued commitments, live mandate and global fundingRevision. Public accounts remain canonical realm treasury objects; only the protagonist has a supported private household source. The apply boundary compares the entire current quote before the low-level conserved transfer and advances the optional revision once. No transaction log, purse mirror, new account, income, resource gift or replacement political mandate is created.
