Religious institutions and affiliation

Optional declarations

data/religious_institutions.js declares optional religiousAffiliations and religiousInstitutionProfiles catalogues. Complete bookmark localInstitutions tables place each institution. Profiles declare their own faith ids, date bounds, qualifications, household restrictions, allowed actions, fees, selection and recognition. None of these declarations changes a person's primary faith, community composition, doctrinal relations or government form.

Roles carry a minimum age, explicit permitted sexes (an empty list permits either), an unmarried requirement, permitted career professions (an empty list imposes none), skill floors and an optional affiliation prerequisite. A deceased spouse does not block an unmarried role. Eligible specialists need not be priests or monastics. On-map membership requires residence in the seat county. Off-map institutions declare offMap:true and explicit county jurisdiction. Opening records are vacant and create no fictional representative; actual admissions and selection are explicit transactions.

Membership and consent

The existing localInstitutions record owns status (active, suspended or closed), at most 64 member references and at most 16 current assent decisions. Each member retains roleId, active or withdrawn status, joinedTurn and changedTurn. Self-admission and qualified role changes require the profile's admit action. An affiliation is acquired by an active member under the profile's affiliate action. Each character retains at most eight unique religiousAffiliations references, with an id and institutionId (or null for an independently authored affiliation). Mutual exclusions are checked in either direction and produce a scoped blocker. No new institution, member or affiliation is added to an existing ledger by restoration or seasonal repair.

Selection declares the registered appointment strategy, patron or members policy, leader role, constituent roles and quorum. On-map patron selection belongs to the current county holder under ordinary possession. Off-map patron selection requires an explicitly declared patronRealmId. Members selection requires the acting person's current constituent role. Each constituent records their own assent using the same reviewed action contract. Submitted lists of alleged consenting people confer no consent. Votes are bounded and cease to count after loss of eligibility, county control, political generation or the vacancy they concerned.

Canonical offices and recognition

Each declared officeId shares religiousHeads and religiousHeadVacancies. The record's representativeId is a compatibility projection, never an independent incumbent. religiousOfficeSnapshot checks actual membership and profile eligibility. Death, withdrawal, lost qualification and occupation remove effective eligibility; explicit vacancy administration clears the canonical assignment without moving property. Selection does not allocate a replacement person, start a papal election or invent hereditary succession. A headless faith may have a local school office, while a leaderless profile declares selection:null and no officeId.

institution_support is an ordinary legitimacySources entry with institutional:true and succession:reaffirm. Typed religious-institution issuers live in the existing authority.recognitions ledger. Profiles declare permitted institutional sources, qualified constituent roles, quorum, whether a current leader is required and whether worship must be protected. Their generation includes current control, qualified constituency and canonical leader. A changed generation suspends support until a new explicit consenting action; disputed support revokes its existing record. Recognition grants no courts, receipts, county control or universal religious authority. Mediation is limited to declared county jurisdiction with protected worship. Neither recognition nor mediation converts the subject or the county.

Review and atomic application

FB.religiousInstitutionQuote(state, institutionId, actor, action, terms) reads eligibility, consent, vacancy, payer, cost, consequences and all dependent facts without repair, RNG, uid allocation or payment. Actors are typed character or realm references. Unsupported actions and fields fail closed. FB.religiousInstitutionApply(state, reviewed) recomputes the entire proposal before proceeding. Fallible canonical authority operations are staged on a detached state; only affected local records, affiliations, office references and authority records are adopted after payment succeeds. Costs use existing treasury counterparties and cannot spend a ruler's private household assets as an institutional purse. NPC characters without an authorized treasury counterparty cannot pay a fee.

The profile's existing abbeyId, when present on its local declaration, references abbeys.houses as the property authority. An unused property reference does not require a foundation before free membership or consent. Membership, selection, recognition, withdrawal and vacancy neither create, copy nor alter that property's capital, treasury or plots. Patronage and funded service independently require their actual property owner and balances. Withdrawal can remove one bound affiliation or the whole membership, preserving the character, followers, household and institution. No new purse or loyalty score is stored.

Public entry points

FB.religiousInstitutionSnapshot exposes the definition, profile, effective availability, bounded membership, canonical holder, vacancy, existing recognition records and referenced property. FB.religiousInstitutionRoleEligibility resolves declared qualifications. Existing episcopal appointment and civic bargain APIs reject profile institutions, ensuring that their legacy defaults cannot bypass declared qualification or consent. Legacy sees, patriarchates, congregations, papal elections and caliphal claims keep their original paths and ids.

Technology decisions

religious_institution_membership, religious_institution_selection and religious_institution_recognition have explicit none entries in FBDATA.techImpactReviews. Each depends on ordinary qualification, consent and existing authority or finance rather than discovery. They introduce no researched asset, military capability or production multiplier.

Household work and advancement

An optional role careerIds list grants access to declared existing careers after qualification, membership and current access resolve. It can override the career's generic faith-group or sex selector for this member; age, station, era and existing technology gates still apply. professions remains an admission prerequisite, not a grant. Admission alone never changes the current career. An optional pathId selects an existing religiousPaths route whose faith and profession filters must match the profile. A lay member can advance while keeping ordinary work; a specialist uses the declared vocation and role title. Existing rank ids and numeric rank records remain authoritative. No membership, spiritual office or head is inferred from a career or a generated character.

FB.religiousCareerReview, FB.religiousInstitutionCharacterPaths, FB.religiousHouseholdRule and FB.religiousCelibate are pure projections. A faith that explicitly declares religiousPaths:null blocks fresh generic clergy/cloister work unless a qualified role grants it. Existing saved careers and NPC role records remain usable. Active unmarried membership prevents marriage even during occupation or lost qualification, until explicit withdrawal ends the promise. FB.religiousMembershipUnmarried closes FB.canWed and FB.canWedSnapshot for that promise, and courtship names the vow rather than a full household. Released clergy and cloister vows keep their courtship gate and leave spouse capacity unchanged. Other roles can allow marriage without borrowing a Christian vow. Existing papal office and abbey vows retain their canonical rules. Fresh character creation applies the same career admission gate without adding membership. The East Syriac filters for the three existing late antique paths include east_syriac in place.

Bounded institutional AI

AI is absent unless the profile explicitly declares ai with roles, affiliations, reserve, maxActions and recognize. Each quarter it visits at most 16 available institutions in deterministic rotation, considers at most 64 qualified or existing non-household members per institution, attempts at most 64 decisions and commits at most eight or the declared lower maxActions. The optional saved aiPeriod prevents repeated work in the same quarter. Every proposal uses religiousInstitutionQuote/Apply, including admission, assent, selection, scoped support and vacancy. Paid decisions require the actual authorized counterparty to retain reserve after payment. AI never uses the player's funds, creates a purse, converts a faith or county, grants a career automatically, or invokes household advancement with player money. Occupation suspends access while preserving membership, vows and property.

Constitutional adapters and review

An ecclesiasticalProfile may declare source:institution_profile with chapter:false and appointmentCost:0. Its binding names the existing profile institution and character-held canonical office. The generic institution transaction owns qualification, patron/member selection, consent, actual payer and its single fee. The temporal adapter reads that same incumbent, retains its explicitly declared county scope, and projects public rule only after a lawful canonical change. Player review acts as the actual player character or their proven realm; it cannot impersonate an NPC patron. Spiritual membership alone supplies no public powers. Existing occupation, temporal recognition, treasury reserve and explicit conversion gates remain in force. Administrative commissions and judicial mandates remain authority appointments, with public qualification, productive source and salary separate from membership, affiliation, work and primary faith.

The Religious communities deed lists eligible managed household participants and local or explicitly off-map institutions. Its shared review cards disclose faith, membership, spiritual leadership, local scope, access, recorded support, age, sex, skills, existing work, affiliation and household restrictions. Exact confirmation names payer, cost and current assent; application rechecks the quote. Back, Escape and Continue retain participant, expanded details, list/body scroll and semantic action focus. Chrome uses FB.T and profile, affiliation and role names use FB.dataText. Saved title snapshots retain source ids and raw fallback role words for later localization.

religious_institution_household_paths, religious_institution_ai and religious_institution_constitutional_binding each have technology impact none in FBDATA.techImpactReviews. 493 Christian local service

The world_v2 opening adds independent service groups at Ctesiphon and Dvin through literal profiles. Both accept qualified resident households without ordaining them, assigning a bishop or granting temporal powers. Optional East Syriac Nicene affiliation records local participation without changing primary faith or universal allegiance. Dvin's helper role grants no clergy career or doctrinal identity. These service groups and their support rules are explicit gameplay reconstructions. No new abbey, endowed property, religious government, papal law or global relationship is inferred from these groups.

Dated independent offices

Legacy officeSystem defaults to episcopal_sees, accepts a declared registered system, or explicitly null for an independent office. This affects only the local institution and its canonical office; no central faith or era is enabled. Empty off-map jurisdiction denies county mediation. Explicit fresh communionWith references use the existing church_support ledger to recognize named counterpart incumbents without territorial scope. Ordinary issuer generations and living subjects govern effectiveness; repair and restore never repeat the opening correspondence.

The dated Christian declarations retain released faith ids and add no church principality, land, endowed purse or new election law. Only accepted revisions receive their fresh rows. Household, helper and existing vocation roles remain separate; unmarried membership is a local promise, not an automatic change to primary doctrine or an annulment. Sources and the simulation boundaries are recorded in docs/research/christian-institutions.md. Dated service extends the none technology review christian_local_church_service. The separate opening_office_communion review is none because ordinary personal recognition and correspondence need no discovery.

Dated communal placements

The Jewish and Samaritan opt-in opening revisions provide fourteen independently placed vacant lay groups across six dates. The 867 review adds no institution because local academy, Karaite and Samaritan bindings remain held. The later jewish_local_congregation_later and karaite_lay_congregation profiles provide only voluntary congregant admission and withdrawal, with adult eligibility for either sex and no profession, wealth or marriage restriction. These are simulation participation rules, not certification for rabbis, judges or priests.

Each on-map group binds an existing town or the separately authored Nablus town and only its seat county. No group seeds members, an incumbent, a canonical office or a treasury. Optional Sardis property references remain unfunded until an explicit voluntary transaction. Ordinary membership does not establish a property charter, protected sanctuary route, priestly descent or judicial consent. Exact chronology, reconstruction and numerical limits are documented in docs/designs/religions.md. Technology impact for these dated lay communities is none.
