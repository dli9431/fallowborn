Elective monarchy

elective_monarchy is a public crown selected through the existing governmentSelection contract. It declares direct holders, fixed territory, crown_election succession, local_reserved consent, elective_service recruitment, elective_receipts revenue, local_interests council, territorial diplomacy, elective_selection AI and local_standing careers. It creates neither a second election ledger nor a property or family store.

The fresh 711 Gothic courts use this form. Their opening rulers and county assignments remain distinct. Recognition within one court does not declare the other claimant illegitimate, annex the other court or transfer its possessions. German royal and imperial profiles remain unchanged until their dated procedures are independently accepted.

Eligibility and nomination

selectionProfile.candidatePool extends the common eligibility contract with gentle standing, allowed cultures, allowed faiths and excluded personal roles. An empty culture or faith list admits any corresponding identity. Age, optional sex, freedom, captivity, exile, ancestry and optional office conditions remain explicit. The crown profile disables the ancestry restriction. Existing living members of its canonical court can stand; a living political principal can nominate a qualified local person from another house during a vacancy. A person already reigning or linked to another court's royal tree retains that role and is unavailable to this nomination path.

Nomination quotes are pure. They inspect local people without materializing candidates or advancing RNG or uid. A nomination apply revalidates the full review, then adds one electoral_candidate reference to the existing court tree and binds the person's royalLine identity. Their parents, spouse, children, private gold, dynasty, holdings and settlement property remain canonical. The selection record's current candidates remain bounded to 64; its interests remain bounded to 24. No separate dynasty frontier is fabricated for election. No payment admits an ineligible person.

procedure declares realmRecognition, designation, interimRevenue and defensiveLevy. Designation is unavailable in the 711 profile. The independent river regression profile permits a preference, adult female and clerical candidates from any culture or faith, different age, terms, weights and seniority ordering. A permitted designation requires a reviewed current candidate, cannot be set during an unresolved vacancy and does not supply political assent. AI only uses a preference to break equally supported proposals.

Selection and disputes

Death, abdication and judicial displacement use the common vacancy transaction. A candidate cannot abdicate a living incumbent. Support, refusal, withdrawal, negotiation and accession share the common pure quote and atomic apply. Stamps bind generation, candidature, designation, living principals, eligibility, support, territory, obligations, cost, funds and turn. Changed facts invalidate a review without spending or accession. A resolved generation cannot accede twice.

The 711 procedure requires assent from both represented interests and a complete living quorum. These two equal weights and unanimity are explicit gameplay abstractions of elite assent, not a reconstructed ballot count. The profile's identity ordering and the river profile's seniority ordering make candidate presentation and equally supported AI proposals deterministic. Split assent remains contested. No calendar instruction chooses a winner and no random tie draw is made.

government_contest stories display the current canonical vacancy and supported claims. They allocate no rival ruler or county ownership. The Gothic opening's actual rival territory remains in its existing court. Recognition can be renegotiated or withheld and ordinary war can change possession, scope and eligibility. A dead principal, dead candidate, captivity, external subordination or insufficient assent prevents accession. The shared scheduler retains minor or empty pools without inventing collateral people.

Public and private continuity

advanceRealmSuccession remains the owner of ruler transition, generation, standing, alliances and faith revalidation. crown_election suppresses generated heir repair and generated consort replacement. The successful person's existing dynasty becomes the court's displayed dynasty. Secular accession performs no religious anointing or imperial coronation. Additional religious office eligibility continues through its existing owner.

The public account is the existing realm.treasury. An NPC accession leaves that exact account in place. A protagonist receiving the crown moves that account into realms.player without transferring its money to player.gold. This account move is scoped to public accession; ordinary escheat uses the existing retirement path. A starting-ruler adoption retains it separately from the scenario's personal purse. Public treasury availability, transfers, distribution and daily military expense use that account. The normal seasonal calculation routes its public tax and government-cost portion to it, while household wages, private holdings, land yield and items retain the personal purse. Private settlement actors remain separate from the public realm actor, including when their owner is the incumbent: ordinary NPC estate accounts and the protagonist's private purse receive their own net receipts. Personal bishopric and abbey receipts remain personal. Military expense accrued before player accession settles once; already-paid player daily expense is not accrued a second time on release. Relinquishing public leadership moves the same realm and account back to its institutional identity; household succession retains private inheritance.

Recognized realm-wide assent covers counties currently controlled by that court; it awards no private land. During a declared vacancy the 711 profile retains ordinary local receipts and courts, and permits defense against an actual defensive war. Reserved offensive decisions still require accession and consent. The independent river profile deliberately disables both interim options. Existing productive private settlement holdings retain their ordinary receipt rules throughout.

AI and presentation

The existing seasonal government office scheduler runs selection at most once per affected court per season. AI admits a qualified local nomination through the same principal transaction, scores actual refusals and obligations, pays negotiation from its own public treasury and resolves only a currently recognized winner. A poor court can receive free direct support from its principals, with the same refusal checks. Paid negotiation is never silently free. A refusal leaves the vacancy and local possession intact.

Both Deeds layouts expose Elective crown for an affected household. The shared kingship sheet shows candidate qualifications, living political principals, scopes, weights, quorum, required assent, designation policy, current claims, obligations, public treasury, costs and blockers. Nomination, designation, support, accession and displacement open separate reviews. Selectors, expanded Details, scroll and semantic focus survive Back, Escape and outcome Continue. UI chrome uses FB.T, constituency labels use governmentSelection dataText and durable accession news uses the existing message descriptor.

Saves, conversion and mods

The additive schema-1 selection record retains its existing envelope. Election progress references ordinary canonical court members and typed institution principals. Electoral candidate roles are validated against their royalLine binding and court origin. Invalid support, references, duplicate interests, excessive candidates and inconsistent results fail before adopting a detached save. Restore initializes no person, constituency, election, public account or historical outcome. Existing 711, 1066 and 1204 saved government forms remain as stored.

The four 711 capital institutions declare freshOnly:true. Only explicit new campaign selection initialization creates these delegates. A saved campaign missing that roster receives none during ordinary repair or restoration; an existing roster retains its ordinary representative succession. A living principal whose institution is outside the court's current sovereign territory cannot supply assent.

electiveConversionQuote and electiveConvert expose a reviewed conversion of an existing independent court. The current ruler must qualify; the target's capital institutions must already have available representatives; local support and an active canonical public treasury are required. Review binds the current ruler and family, public resources, principals, counties, rights, appointments, contracts and household property. Conversion creates only the optional selection record. It never creates delegates or divides a combined personal purse into invented public resources. A campaign without those facts receives a blocker and retains its form. A court already carrying selection state cannot replace that contract through this conversion path.

JSON mods compose registered strategy ids and the typed selection profile. They provide no executable election algorithm. Invalid pool, procedure, institutional selector or numeric bounds fail government validation. Fresh consumers using capital_elite and capital_clergy must declare a council and congregation at their capital. Engine code names no historical consumer.
