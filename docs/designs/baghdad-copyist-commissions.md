# Baghdad copying commissions

The Baghdad Copyist Household can review competing terms from the original Abbasid court in Deeds. Either offer reserves its entire fee before the first action day. That day and each later reviewed shift replace Daily Focus. The offer is available in Baghdad in 800–802. The review shows the acceptance date, the last day to act and the payment split.

| Offer | Working days | Reserved fee | Paid at delivery | Disputed | Time limit |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Shorter order | 3 | 6 gold | 6 gold | 0 gold | 10 days |
| Longer commission | 4 | 12 gold | 8 gold | 4 gold | 14 days |

The last working or dispute day is the day before expiry or the last day of 802, whichever comes first. The longer commission offers an immediate settlement of 2 gold and a 2-gold refund, a one-action-day local hearing for all 4 gold, or a waiver that returns all 4 gold to the payer. Before delivery, interruption pays only for the completed share of the guaranteed fee and returns the rest. After delivery, interruption or expiry pays the full remaining entitlement from escrow. The court can fund the shorter order alone when it has 6–11 gold available. The household can decline the commission without spending a day or paying a fee and can still take the unpaid practice day before deciding. After either commission is settled by choice, the original optional one-day order still reserves and pays its own 2 gold.

The longer job shares `first_copy`’s original payer binding and has its own escrow and stage IDs. The shorter and later orders retain their original IDs and terms, including in existing unmodded saves. Restore checks the recorded patron and account without booking new work or payment. The ordinary JSON fixture varies the fee, days and settlement using the same operations and exact matching content validation.

Technology impact is `none` under `household_finite_jobs`. Commission terms and recovery of the buyer’s reserved money depend on the original court, local presence and completed work. Ordinary scholarly career and qualification rules still apply independently.

Early Abbasid patronage and the circulation of Arabic texts provide the setting. The household, copied text, prices, deadlines and hearing are fictional game-scale terms. The choice concerns copying an Arabic exemplar and grants no appointment, translation qualification or access to an institution. [Ahmed Ragab, “In a Clear Arabic Tongue”](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/full/10.1086/694316).
