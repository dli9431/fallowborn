Temporary refuge

The household_refuge world type owns finite temporary protective places at one existing physical see seat. It does not own county movement, residence, population, private property, food, guards, custody or combat. Historical institutions and capacities require their own accepted evidence; the two shipped authoring fixtures are fictional. Capacity is a person-place simulation budget, not a demographic count or a claim about historical rooms.

A request names an exact subset of an existing household_party. Association consent alone is insufficient: every adult separately consents to this shelter, and an actual already-consenting parent decides for each dependent. Requests reserve identities, not protective places. The current county holder separately authorizes the specific request under ordinary home-presence and public-spending gates. The existing institutional incumbent then offers it. Both must be alive, capable, currently in the county and unchanged from the original request. Spiritual office alone supplies no civil authority. Collective, civic, delegated and chapter-based permission strategies remain unavailable to this adapter until their exact consent terms are supported. Current territorial consent, actual settlement property authority and the ordinary blocked-county check are mandatory. The supported permission is an institutional see seat under its actual direct county holder and lawful sovereign. Private foundations and abbey property are deliberately unsupported; this capability supplies no confiscation or implied property consent.

Offers reserve their exact finite places across every declaration using the same institution. Effective capacity is the minimum declared for that institution. Definitions cannot give it different offices, jurisdictions or sites. The offer prepays its explicit per-person service cost for the finite term through treasurySpend, using the separately authorized real public account. A free declaration charges zero. No household fee, compulsory contribution, refund, food inventory, guard force or unpaid upkeep is implied. Paid service remains prepaid if the offer expires or protection is interrupted. Authorization and offer confirmations recheck the actual account, authority, people, budget and committed incidents atomically.

Entry is a separate local transaction for an exact consenting subset, including every selected dependent's actual caregiver. Requests and entry keep each accepted care group together; they cannot select the caregiver and silently leave their accepted dependent behind. The shared sheet offers whole-party and individual care-group reviews without an exponential subset list. The real named-party or personal location owner must already place each entrant halted in the shelter county. A road still moving, a camp obligation, remote relative, custody or lost access cannot establish arrival. The confirmation enters the named physical site locally and starts its finite protective term, releasing unused offered places. There is no time-driven arrival and no new refuge route or residence. The new exact-site witness feeds household locality checks, overriding the protagonist's home settlement while physically present. The ordinary party carrier remains the only county-location owner.

Only a configured local_threat's actual committed attempted occurrence at the same site supplies pressure. Intimidation, a date or another settlement does not challenge protection. A current attempt blocks new requests, offers, entries and additional terms. It interrupts pending entry, drops pending extension reviews and marks an existing promise challenged. A resolved or withdrawn episode allows new admission reviews. An agreement that recorded a challenge is ineligible for extension. No occurrence grants a battle result, casualty, conversion, imprisonment, property loss or safety percentage.

Provider death, office replacement, civil recall, loss of territory, lost local presence, care failure, exhausted declared capacity and deadlines interrupt or expire protection without moving or harming occupants. Ending the agreement releases its protective budget exactly once. Its original named people remain physically at the admitted site until an actual departure, custody change or death invalidates that witness. Any named adult or caregiver, the original requester or either provider authority can end the whole agreement. The review identifies that scope. Separating requests by care group allows independent terms. After an agreement ends, new consent and new authorization can offer a new admission at the same seat. Confirming that entry supersedes the old site's witness and preserves physical location.

Additional funded term

A currently protected player adult can request one additional full term during an active, unchallenged agreement. The request includes the adult's own consent for their care group. Every other protected adult gives fresh consent for their group, the original civil authority separately approves the public expense under current spending gates, and the original institutional incumbent separately consents to provide the term. Both providers must still hold the same offices and civil generation with lawful access and authority at the original site. Declared NPC review, volunteer and refusal policies use the same transaction as the player. The player always makes their own decisions. Browsing, backing out and acknowledging a recorded decision have no authority or cost.

Only the host's final confirmation prepays the new term through the original real public account, after rechecking authority, threats, and the exact people and places. The existing declaration supplies a full `termDays` at its `funding.perPersonDay` rate for each person still protected. Released and unused places are excluded. A zero-rate declaration pays zero and still requires the original public account and fresh approvals. No alternate purse or partial payment is used. The additional term starts at the original expiry, without a gap, new entry, extra places, movement or residence changes. One funded extension consumes the agreement's opportunity. Withdrawal, refusal, deferred confirmation or changed occupancy preserves the original term and prepayment. A pending request lapses at the original expiry. Normal provider and threat interruption still applies after funding, with the new cost prepaid in full.

The shared review shows the old and proposed expiry dates, exact protected people, original host and spender, public payer, full amount and available funds. The shelter sheet distinguishes a pending request from the new effective deadline. The original `endTurn` remains the original deadline. The optional `extension` on an existing request records the requesting adult, request turn, exact protected person IDs, fresh adult consents, civil authorization and host payment. The reviewed debit determines the effective deadline and preserves both receipts. A pending record has no payment, and detached validation checks chronology, membership, the original payer and the exact cost. Older rows retain their existing shape and deadline. No declaration or catalogue identity changes are required. Native Mainz demonstrates the thirty-day zero-rate volunteer path. The Hill JSON review configuration demonstrates a four-day paid term and explicit reviews using the same typed fields.

The shelter review adapter exposes `extension_request`, `extension_consent`, `extension_authorize`, `extension_fund` and `extension_decline` with `{requestId}` terms. It derives occupants and decision makers from the existing agreement and rejects stale confirmations atomically. The canonical event-review adapter uses those same shelter choices and their bound parties.

An admitted adult may release their own protective place and the places of all their admitted dependents together. The review derives the exact group from the acting adult and original admission. It rechecks the current provider, term, individual consent, care, protective places and actual local presence before releasing the whole group once. A committed attempt pauses release from an active term until the ordinary boundary records the challenge. A fresh review may then release the adult's group while keeping the original challenge on the remaining agreement. The remaining people keep their exact places, provider and deadline. Releasing the last protected group ends the agreement normally. The original public prepayment stays paid in full.

Voluntary release preserves every occupant's exact-site witness, county position and residence until the ordinary location owners establish departure. A new confirmed same-site admission may supersede a released person's witness while the old agreement continues for others. Admission still requires fresh consent, authority and available places; the new term has its own payment and deadline.

Departure, interruption checkpoints, separately priced recovery, ordinary return and eligible permanent settlement stay with the merged household party and travel owners. Entry grants none of their eligibility. Those ordinary movements invalidate refuge presence immediately; the next normal boundary archives the departure. A refusal or missed deadline never invents an arrival or safe return. No heir is enrolled and an original provider is never replaced during restore.

The existing `leftTurn` continues to archive the physical witness. Legacy member rows lack `releasedTurn` and keep their original protection and witness semantics on restore. The ledger version, save envelope and mod definitions retain their existing forms.
