Ordinary JSON, player choices and NPC choices use the same detached validation and quote/commit. A declaration never calls opening initialization later or credits a population, purse or provisions constant at formation.

Authoring

A target bookmark realm declares mobile.preparationId naming exactly one attached expedition_preparation. It requires mobile.command, the existing opening principals and muster and a valid mobile.funding policy. Every profile member binds an existing opening person; at most 24 members are supported. The story contains id, type, title, intro, realmId, termDays, source, provisions, provider, acceptance and ai. Authority and transactions

FB.expeditionPreparation.review(state,id,action,actorId) is pure; commit(state,reviewed) re-quotes every fact and rejects stale or repeated changes. Actions are form, wait, refuse, stop, authorize, revoke, contribute and grant. Only the original commander can form or wait. The original commander or provider can close preparation. Only the original current provider can authorize or revoke local formation consent. The provider must still be living, present at the origin and its direct holder and sovereign controller. Local withdrawal also requires the ordinary homeAuthority levy permission. Actual original command, original realm/issuer generations, age, custody, current presence, other mobile membership, personal participation and named-party reservations remain binding. A missing or replaced person never retargets a preparation.

Funding uses the existing homeAuthority.fundingQuote/fund owner, extended to recognize a currently valid unformed preparation and include its full identity in the review signature. Public grants still require the actual source ruler or exact eligible civil deputy, ordinary home authority, distinct public accounts, amount maximum and available money. Private contributions require the current protagonist's explicit consent and private purse. No contribution is automated. The shared preparation sheet offers the original provider a grant and the original protagonist commander a contribution for exactly the currently missing training/provision cost. A contribution neither authorizes formation nor promises a refund. Stop, refusal and expiry leave already contributed money in its actual public account.

Formation stages the ordinary population, public payment, market and shared mobile-constructor transactions on a detached campaign. Only a successful complete result publishes the changed owners together. It creates no character, RNG draw or global UID. A stale or refused review changes none of these owners. The authority owner allocates the migration id only inside the staged successful transition. The shared constructor also serves normal opening creation, preserving existing generated defaults, sequencing, RNG and opening stock behavior for declarations without preparationId. An optional existing expeditionProgram checkpoint initializes once after successful formation; its subsequent movement remains with the program owner.

The daily hook checks one finite preparation. It closes expired or interrupted intent quietly. A volunteers provider who is not the player may authorize through the same review on one boundary; no formation occurs in that same dispatch. A nonplayer original commander may subsequently form or refuse only under the configured ai policy and the same transaction. A player provider or commander is never impersonated by policy. Failed formation may continue waiting until its deadline. Explicit wait, refusal and stop remain available; terminal preparation never reforms. External closure releases reserved people into its terminal off-map receipt, never into a county or another recruitable pool. Local closure withdraws nobody. Already formed hosts, vows and separate obligations survive the preparation's historical receipt.

Save contract

The optional state.stories[id] record has exactly version 1, definitionKey, status, reason, revision, openedTurn, endTurn, endedTurn, bindings, realmGeneration, issuer, provider, authorizedTurn, reserves, consumed, released and formed. definitionKey fingerprints the effective declaration, mobile definition and profile. status is preparing, formed or closed. reason is null except a closed record has refused, stopped, expired or interrupted. openedTurn is zero; endTurn equals termDays; endedTurn is null while preparing and the actual formation/closure turn thereafter. Bindings reuse original memberId/characterId/person provenance; realmGeneration and issuer retain the original authority. provider records original realmId/characterId/generation; authorizedTurn is null until consent or the actual consent turn. Revision and all clocks are bounded safe integers, never future occurrences.

Only preparing external records contain reserves, exactly matching their declared source. A formed record has empty reserves/released and the exact consumed member/culture/religion/count cohorts. Closed external records have empty reserves/consumed and the exact released source. Local unformed/closed records have no cohort rows. A formed receipt has exactly realmId, migrationId, hostId, turn, leaderId, generation, trainingCost, food and foodCost. It reconciles the immutable original bindings, real mobile accounting, migration identity and original start turn. Historical records survive subsequent loss of host, command, control, office or life; they never recreate them. Missing required preparations, extra fields, unsupported versions, inconsistent source totals, forged clocks, foreign host identities or impossible unformed mobile rows reject detached restore. A pending now-ineligible record can await the ordinary next boundary; restore never interrupts it, grants consent, pays, withdraws, initializes a program or forms anything.

The existing v4 envelope and mobile schema 3 remain unchanged. Campaigns without this optional capability keep their prior initialization and save meanings.
