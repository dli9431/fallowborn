Mobile command and personal continuity

Mobility is selected by the territory strategy's mobileFollowing marker. Coalition elections are selected separately by coalition_leadership succession. The hosted_following and expedition_command forms retain the same population, migration, camp, host and finite stock owners while selecting retained_principal or superior_commission succession, command_consent and command_receipts. No date, people, culture or historical identity selects these policies.

The retained policy keeps its declared opening leader without a replacement constitution. Death leaves a vacancy. There is no automatic child, majority election, generated replacement or commission by a host-country ruler. This narrow policy does not claim to reconstruct a historical following's political customs.

The commissioned policy names an actual superior through a direct opening actor, a different nonmobile realm's current materialized ruler, or the finite external warrant described below. A direct person retains the ordinary opening actor requirement for a real home. Neither route grants a local capital or territorial rights. A realm reference means the living current officeholder. The policies share no implied dynasty or compulsory calendar outcome.

Declaration and owner

Each opted-in mobile opening requires the existing opening.principals, leaderMemberId and conserved muster. Its additional command object declares a stable id, superior and a nonempty bounded list of operations. The command is scoped to this exact mobile realm and its existing single host, following and public account. A command id alone is never sufficient to appoint or spend.

Supported operations are recruit, demuster, move, march, offer, accept, work, trade, war, peace, service, land, contract, settle, pledge and leave. Omitting an operation withholds it from both player and AI. The existing exact transaction still checks resources, local rights, lawful war goals, recruitment pledges, population conservation and outside consent. The commission adds neither a conquest exception nor permission to tax a county. These forms use zero county, settlement and general realm revenue projections; explicit conserved payments still belong to the public treasury. Field command confers no local Consent or territorial Ban, Peace or Revenue appointment.

FB.mobile owns the record and transactions. commandSnapshot and commandValid are pure projections; commandPermits applies operation scope. The common governmentSelection adapter mobile_command exposes reviewCommand and the normal mobile.commit path for appoint and recall. Requests carry realmId, commandId, action, the actual character actor, and candidateId for appoint. Recall has no candidate. Only the current living superior may commit; an institution reference or a commander cannot impersonate that person. Replacement selects a living adult, allegiant member principal, without creating people or taking an independent landed ruler. Appointing oneself as one's own subordinate is refused.

Appointment installs the existing character through the mobile leader owner, increments the existing ruler generation, and records the current issuer and command revision. It clears orders for review but preserves the one host, units, member pools, camp, supplies, treasury, private property and household station. Recall suspends the mandate and stops orders. Neither operation charges money or grants private rank. Player and AI use the same operation quotes. Ordinary seasonal AI may supply the following within its permissions; it cannot appoint itself or invent a superior's decision.

Continuity and restore

Liveness, custody, member eligibility, leader generation, actual issuer identity and issuer generation are checked on every command read and operation. A realm issuer's succession suspends its predecessor's commission immediately. Only its new living ruler may recommission an eligible member. A direct actor's death leaves no automatic superior successor. The ordinary daily boundary halts retained host paths before army movement if the mandate or movement permission is missing. Recall and replacement also stop orders immediately. The following's ongoing consumption and ordinary hostile combat remain real while command is suspended.

Public command and private household station remain independent. An adopted commander keeps the public realm's stable id, account, people and institutions while beginning with the scenario's private purse and station. Character presentation uses the person's own social class rather than converting command rank into landed station. Later loss of command removes host control without confiscating private property or transferring the public treasury to an heir.

Only these declarations initialize mobile schema 3. The command record contains id, status (active, vacant or recalled), revision, issuer (null or characterId/generation) and leaderGeneration. OpeningBindings retain schema 2's identity provenance. Historical inactive issuers and dead people remain references rather than being regenerated. Unknown fields, wrong schemas, forged direct issuers, future generations, election state and malformed scope fail validation. Matching restore validates the record without calling initialization, choosing a leader, restoring orders or replaying payments. Schemas 1 and 2 retain their existing meaning. Exact mod fingerprints cover the command declaration and its operation policy.

The independent mobile-command-harbor.json example uses a retained leader, an outside hosting counterpart, explicit existing principals, mixed mounted/archery muster and access/work/trade permissions. mobile-command-dune.json uses a different profile, incumbent binding, deputy, three-class muster and a separate direct superior with military permissions that exclude hosted work and access. They are ordinary complete JSON mods. Both retain their mandatory on-map migration patron and inhabited origin/target. Additional independent sponsorship and external-warrant examples are described below. Historical biographies, multiple columns and expedition programs remain separate capabilities. No historical start or named historical person is installed by these generic examples.

Independent origin and sponsorship

A command-declared following can opt into mobile.sponsorship:{schema:1,kind:'independent'} with patronRealmId:null. Missing sponsorship retains the existing required-patron path. Independence requires the authored opening and command contracts. Origin and target remain actual inhabited physical counties. Their owners are neither sponsors nor implicit access grantors. The constructor accepts this waiver only for the effective bookmark's exact following, declared origin/target, canonical host id and profile settlement form, while its fresh command record has no migration yet and its mandate is effective. A missing patron on an ordinary service migration still fails.

The migration stores the same optional schema-1 sponsorship marker and null patron. It stores no sponsor account, grant, new person or copied command. Both mobile and authority restore validators reconcile it with the effective declaration and reject missing, forged, mixed or extra fields. Exact content fingerprints cover the policy. Retained migration records remain unchanged. Matching restore never calls a constructor or renews an entitlement.

Patron-based migration land/service/force requests and the generic federate settlement route are unavailable on an independent record. Actual owned-land settlement uses the conserved mobile review/apply transaction. Ordinary reviewed wars, outside access, separately negotiated real service contracts and settlement on actually owned land remain with their canonical owners and command permissions. A new service agreement does not retroactively change the expedition's origin or supply a migration patron. An independent settlement session includes only its real realm seat, after the ordinary ownership gates, never a null or invented outside seat. Independent status survives losses and command suspension; it supplies no food, money, replacement army, consent, local fiscal authority or safe passage.

Finite external commissions

superior_commission may declare superior:{kind:'external_authority',id,name,generation,endTurn}. The stable id uses the ordinary lowercase identifier bound, name is a nonempty proper label of at most 160 characters, generation is an integer from 1 to 1000000, and endTurn is an absolute campaign turn from 1 to 1440. This is an explicitly accepted finite warrant from an external authority. It creates no resident person, local court, sovereignty, household, purse or generic off-map imperial simulation. The declaration does not assert the unknown physical location or continued life of a historical individual.

The existing schema-3 command owner retains issuer:{externalAuthorityId,generation}, exactly matching this declaration. No parallel authority ledger is added. At or after endTurn the effective issuer is absent, command reads and operations suspend immediately, stale reviews fail, and the daily command boundary stops orders. People, ongoing expenses, actual host/camp and finite stock remain in their existing owners. Death, custody, loss of allegiance or loss of the commander still suspends command under the ordinary rules. Restore retains the original issuer after expiry without renewing it.

External issuers cannot be impersonated by a local character, realm or a fabricated actor kind. This bounded contract offers no external appointment, recall, renewal or automatic successor transaction. Those remote political decisions need an independently implemented owner before a consumer can promise them; a finite warrant does not guess their outcome. An unchanged valid warrant authorizes the existing commander only. A commander vacancy remains vacant. Full issuer declaration and current authority are included in command projections used by the common quote signature, so a changed generation, term or policy invalidates a previous review.

mobile-expedition-harbor.json independently configures a retained leader without a sponsor; mobile-expedition-dune.json configures a three-class incumbent-led expedition under a generation-3 external council warrant ending at turn 270. Both are complete ordinary JSON imports through Mods and retain different member, force and operation policies. The latter contains no local character for its external council. expedition-sponsors.spec.js covers pure invalid imports, conservation, actual consented access payment, patron request rejection, expiry, interruption, strict provenance, exact-content rejection and matching restore. Existing sponsored command and coalition examples keep their original paths.

Technology decisions for these contracts are independently none. mobile_independent_sponsorship depends on actual resources and consenting outside parties. mobile_external_commission depends on the finite warrant, exact issuer identity and ordinary command eligibility. Neither has a credible research prerequisite; normal military unit and travel gates remain in force.

Technology decisions

mobile_command_continuity is none: living political relationships, eligibility and current issuers govern appointment and recall. mobile_command_scope is independently none: explicit permissions, canonical resources and local rights govern public operations. Existing unit equipment and travel technology remain authoritative. Neither capability needs an invented discovery or bypasses an existing one.

Loading and presentation

js/mobile_command.js loads immediately after js/mobile_confederations.js, before later government extensions and UI consumers. The existing mobile sheet shows commander, effective mandate, actual available superior, public treasury and the distinct camp and host. Command forms show no majority threshold or election actions. Only declared operations are offered. Appointment and recall use the same exact-cost review, stale-confirmation checks and modal history as other mobile actions; Back, Escape, scroll, inputs and originating focus remain shared. The sheet does not promise that suspending command dissolves the following or suspends its expenses.

Member pledge and departure permissions remain personal decisions under their existing self-principal, conservation and local-settlement checks, even when public command is suspended. They cannot recommission a recalled commander. Every public operation, including paid work, still requires an effective mandate.
