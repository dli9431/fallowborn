Building water operations

Water service composes the existing physical resource owner with ordinary nonfort buildings. The bundled buildingOperationProfiles table is empty. No historical facility, source, catchment, stock, population, workforce, technology completion or income is inferred. The two complete ordinary JSON imports water-operations-river.json and water-operations-catchment.json demonstrate supplied conveyance and competing demand versus an initially dry tank requiring a separately paid fill.

Declarations and intrinsic boundaries

A building's optional operationProfile selects literal mode, amount, periodDays, workCost and effect. Mode store transfers real water into the final store and gives no effect. Mode use consumes the actual allocation once and activates exactly one existing field: tax, piety, research, populationCapacity, populationFamineProtection, populationCrisisProtection or migrationAttraction. Effect values remain on the building owner. This is no new scalar channel. Forts, military effects, construction awards, settlement drawing and faith pressure are excluded from this extension. A use policy must have a positive matching building effect; other ongoing fields must be absent or zero. Unbound existing assets retain their exact ordinary definitions, effects, upkeep and repair rules, even when a newly imported building definition offers an operation profile.

Effective bookmark/revision buildingOperationAssignments bind an exact building, county and actual physical site to a profile, source, ordered connected storage route and demand identity. Compilation uses normalized world/layout references and the resource owner's checked graph, without the active map or state. The final source/store must occupy the facility's exact site. Only a declared connected storage edge permits a transfer. At most eight stores and 256 assignments are supported. A demand identity declares one finite service at that site and effect; different effects/sites cannot alias it, and different names cannot create duplicate demand for the same site/effect. Alternative facilities compete for the one demand. Separate actual sites may receive independent service only by consuming their own allocations. Source, conveyance and storage do not multiply the benefit of the same volume.

Canonical transaction

Quote and commit serve player, realm AI and private barons. A quote stages the complete reserve, transfer and terminal consume or release sequence against a detached copy of siteResources. Each intermediate transfer spends actual source capacity, credits its connected store, and withdraws only the same amount for the next step. Terminal store releases its temporary claim and retains received stock. Terminal use consumes its claim. Failure at any edge discards the complete stage. Other people's existing reservations remain conserved by the resource owner; this owner leaves no outstanding reservation of its own.

Quotes require an actual living free adult payer, ordinary direct property authority, the existing home civil decision safeguard, an uncontested county, current rights at every source and a populated receiving county. Paid local work is an explicit cash service cost for a bounded water quantity, with no extra household action day, wage, named hire, enterprise output or production stock. Named employment and material production are separate owners. There is at most one service per asset and shared demand per policy period. This conservative contract cannot schedule a second use early when a source boundary shortens a term. Seasonal scheduling is a separate extension.

The charge is workCost plus building upkeep times the actual funded days divided by 90. The term ends at the next policy boundary or any renewable route source's next boundary, whichever comes first. Quote compares the entire resource revision, current authority/incumbent, asset binding, operation root, exact payer balance and existing reserve. Commit recomputes the quote and stages the route again before one debit and publication. Stale or repeated confirmation changes no resources, money, RNG or UID. The upkeep projection for an opted-in asset is zero because its service charge already prepaid the exact finite term. Construction protects its first prospective work/upkeep payment and existing AI reserves in addition to the construction invoice. Repairs retain property and its operation binding without allocation, automatic activation or repeated construction awards.

Projection and consumers

The shared building index uses buildingOperations.effects rather than static fields for bound records. Its cache key contains the actual receipt and live eligibility. Population capacity, famine/crisis protection, migration, local fiscal income, household piety and national research therefore see the same receipt. Count-based income ledgers still use integral active asset counts and the original matching scalar. Unbound records follow the original path. Bound storage has no ongoing effect, and bound use has none before a successful paid receipt or after its term expires. Capture, custody, changed incumbent, lost source permission, contested land, incompatible institution binding or ruin suspends that projection immediately. No read removes property, returns consumed water, refunds payments or repeats an expense. Current eligibility is derived, not saved as prose.

The annual AI pass considers at most 32 successful assigned-asset operations, in declaration order, using the exact quote/commit and existing public or baronial reserves. Player assets require explicit review. Successful operations invalidate the annual fiscal snapshot before surplus distributions. There is no automatic historical initialization, off-map supply, speculative material purchase, household work allocation or new per-day scheduler. Later seasonal consumers may call the same canonical API. Construction, ordinary autoBuild and baron purchases share the prospective physical-input and funding gate.

Only commit adds state.buildingOperations version 1 with definition, revision and receipts. Receipts are indexed by original asset id and contain assignmentId, assetId, revision, actor, authority, turn, untilTurn, period, amount, paid, sources, demandId and resourceRevision. authority binds concrete characterId, original realm generation or null, original holder and owner. Sources bind the full route's ids, profile ids and period ordinals. The structural definition fingerprint includes assignments, policies, the matching scalar and upkeep. The independent resource fingerprint covers site/source capacity, rights and connections. Exact ordinary mod signatures continue to cover all imported definitions.

Strict validation checks exact fields, stable asset and source references, unique revisions, positive finite charges, declared quantity, term and period bounds, original actor identity, matching asset revision, conserved minimum renewable usage and a surviving resource owner. Receipt history for a deliberately removed property may remain, without becoming an active benefit. A surviving asset with a positive revision requires its exact receipt. Missing legacy owners stay missing. Restore validates facts without constructing, allocating, renewing, consuming, paying, hiring or restoring a benefit outside current eligibility. There is no save-envelope or unrelated subsystem version change.
