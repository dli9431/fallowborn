Conserved production batches

js/production.js owns opt-in finite material accounting. data/production.js declares empty productionGoods and productionRecipes tables; no bundled world receives a plant, material lot or production assignment. Effective bookmark/revision productionSites bind one existing physical county/site/building to supported recipes and, where required, one co-located site-resource water source. Whole-field revision replacement and ordinary Mods file import use the same pure compiler. Geography, scenery, knowledge and cash cannot stand in for a physical asset or material input.

Goods use stable ids, explicit unit ids and positive integer conservative mass per unit. Recipes consume one to eight actual good quantities and create one to eight different good quantities. Their dimensionless output mass cannot exceed input mass. Water is a separate real site-resource allocation, never a second material stock. A paid recipe needs one to thirty real action days and a positive integral processing price. Units, ratios, fees and work are authored simulation accounts, not archaeological yields. The batch interface itself has no supplier, sale or arbitrary deposit operation. The optional finite exchange and enterprise-output adapter below composes those operations over the same stock owner.

One canonical owner

FB.production.quote is read-only. A successful reserve commit claims deterministic existing unreserved lots, reserves actual lawful water and charges the full declared processing price once. It creates a pending batch and no output. Processing costs are non-refundable, including on cancellation; they are spent processing costs rather than wages credited to a new person. Inputs remain conserved property until completion. One pending batch per asset and the same authority, original worker, ruler generation and asset identity govern work and completion. Competing, stale, repeated and insufficient-fund confirmations leave all ledgers and RNG unchanged. Output room for every pending batch is reserved against the global lot bound.

The payer is explicit in the quote and saved batch. An actual public treasury pays only its own lawful facility, preserving ordinary construction reserves and military accrual. A household crown without an independent public account pays from its original private household purse, as ordinary construction does. Public payment never falls back to private funds when an independent public account exists. The account abstraction also retains the existing private barony payment primitive; this tranche's explicit opening lots are bound to their original realm owner, without inventing a private baron inventory or a transfer.

The living original holder does the actual work locally. Enterprise assignments, contracted enterprise labor, household offices, active service, travel, custody, personal military work and a raised realm host block that worker. Raw assignment reads never repair staffing or clear careers. A worker supplies at most one production shift per campaign turn across all batches. Player work goes through productionAction in the canonical passDay boundary after ordinary blockers and replaces Daily Focus; direct commit refuses a protagonist shift. NPC callers use aiQuote and the same commit, stock, reserves, locality and work checks. There is no speculative autonomous manufacturing loop or free hired labor. Normal time progression and enterprise owners remain unchanged.

Only a fully worked pending batch can finish. Finish consumes its reserved lots and actual water claim once, then creates the exact declared output lots. Store retains finite owned goods for another declared recipe at the same asset. Use consumes the output immediately, with no passive building effect, money, people, soldier, research or other reward. No positive scalar is inferred from the existence of a receipt. Generic conservation is conserved_production_batches, mode none. Each optional advanced recipe names its exact hard technology-impact review and visible missing technologies and fallback; ordinary recipes use a none review. Existing funded batches keep their original gates when current knowledge changes. Recipes grant no research bonus or new discovery.

Interruption and assets

Capture, changed original generation, death, custody, removal, ruin and lost institutional permission block further work/completion. A withdrawn or expired source claim also blocks the batch. Nothing silently substitutes a successor, moves stock or refunds earlier processing. An unchanged lawful original actor may cancel, releasing material reservations and any still-releasable water claim, without producing output. Missing original people and authority may leave an honestly stranded batch. Original property and recorded completed work remain intact. Repair of the same stamped asset can resume it; replacement construction has no inherited production identity. No historic or restore operation enrolls a replacement.

Explicit fictional opening lots require a standing openingInfrastructure placement. Fresh boot runs production initialization after ruler adoption, infrastructure, household/site lordship and local-institution setup. It enrolls only actual declared standing assets, assigning a local productionAsset stamp and stable owner id, and creates only those declared finite opening lots. Empty explicit configurations create no goods. Production does not place buildings. No historical world opts in. This accounting surface operates explicit opening assets and same-asset recipe chains. The finite exchange adapter below admits purchased materials and actual producer output with exact provenance, without admitting an arbitrary deposit or synthesizing a new asset.

Persistence and bounds

The strict optional state.production version 1 contains definition, revision, nextId, assets, lots and batches. A building's optional productionAsset contains only version 1 and its exact local asset id. Definitions exclude translated names/descriptions, while ordinary whole-mod fingerprints retain their existing exactness. Stable asset references, original opening holders, conserved initial quantities, units, consumed input equations, output provenance, per-person work turns, exact paid prices and water period/claim identities are validated before adoption. Every created local id is unique and accounted for; ids are not global game uids. Removed assets may retain inactive historic receipts but cannot match a replacement building.

Missing legacy owners remain absent. Restore neither calls initialization, performs work, fills inventory, consumes inputs, credits output, renews a source nor changes RNG. A water-backed completed batch must finish within the actual reservation's period; storage claims have no renewal. Detached matching saves can retain interrupted pending batches without repairing permission. The save envelope and other subsystem versions stay unchanged.

Bounds are 128 goods, 128 recipes, 128 declared sites/assets, 16 recipes per site, eight inputs/outputs/opening goods, 10,000 units per recipe quantity, mass 1-1,000 per unit, 30 workdays, 10,000 processing gold, and 512 lots and batches globally. Journals are finite and stop new commitments at capacity; no unreviewed pruning drops provenance. Compilation is cached per effective bookmark/revision/mod signature. Device performance and memory acceptance require owner measurements; source review does not establish a benchmark.
