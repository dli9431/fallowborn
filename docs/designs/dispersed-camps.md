Dispersed civilian camps

An authored hosted command may partition its existing aggregate civilians and resting fighters into two to four fixed camps through mobile.civilianCamps. Each camp has a stable id, inhabited county, optional curated site, exact member allocations and finite local food. These pools replace the corresponding member pools rather than adding another population total. The original people, realm, command, military rosters and public treasury retain their existing owners. This is actual present distribution, not several potential access counties or a completed journey.

The declaration partitions every member population remaining after the opening muster exactly once. fieldFood plus all camp food equals mobile.food. A single host receives fieldFood within its ordinary carrying capacity. With columns, fieldFood must equal their existing declared food total. The ordinary constructor withdraws the fighters once; the camp initializer repartitions only the remaining people and stock. All fresh declarations and the complete existing hosting batch preflight before any initializer runs. The actual lawful holder, original living counterpart, current available capacity, effective inhabited county and curated site are required. The existing hosting contract conservatively reserves capacity for the whole following in every named county, including those with smaller actual camps.

Technology impact mobile_civilian_camps is none: locating existing people and provisions needs no discovery. Technology impact mobile_camp_transactions is separately none: local service, markets and partial settlement use existing public money, private presence and population transactions. Command permission, lawful hosting, ownership, capacity, actual stock and cash reserves remain binding. No travel, military equipment or transport gate is waived.

Local daily accounting

Each actual camp consumes its own finite provisions for its own civilians and reserves. Its starvation losses remove only its own cohorts and update the original following accounting once. Pasture production is bounded by that camp's actual people, active local concession and the existing shared county-period capacity ledger. The camp's harvestPeriod prevents another harvest through an access renewal or another daily call in the same period. A camp without access remains physically present but cannot procure or harvest through that lost right. Expiry does not move, settle or protect its people.

Nonplayer commands attempt at most one purchase per camp at the daily boundary through the same reviewed transaction as players. The quantity is the current local ration deficit, bounded by actual local market stock, current price and public funds above the existing supply-policy reserve. There is one public treasury and no new procurement balance. The normal lastDay bounds the whole daily dispatch. No seasonal single-camp AI acts on dispersed civilians.

Reviewed operations

FB.mobile.campSnapshot(state,realmId) returns detached camp ids, actual county/site, civilians, reserves, food, daily demand, current access and local market stock. It creates no people or supplies. FB.mobile.reviewCamp uses the existing governmentSelection source mobile_camps and FB.mobile.commit. Both player and AI must carry an exact living character actor; trade and settlement require the current original command leader and declared permission. Requoting includes all camp pools, military state, original command, local population, holder/owner, market, relevant obligations and actual payer funds. Refused and stale reviews spend nothing and consume no RNG or UID.

trade buys or sells only the selected camp's actual provisions at that county market. The current local owner and the following exchange the real payment through treasuryTransfer. Owned county stock pays no money to the same account. The camp receives or loses exactly the market quantity; no other camp or host receives it. Work requires an adult actually at a county-level camp and current local access. It pays 0.2 from the public treasury to that person and consumes one local food unit, once per person per day. Existing location records do not establish exact site presence, so exact-site work is explicitly unavailable.

settle transfers only the selected camp's civilians into its actual county, through populationPoolQuote and populationPoolApply. It requires the command's settlement permission, the following as both current owner and holder, and no current war or conflicting service. It transfers the departing civilians' proportional share of that camp's food to the local market. Reserves, named people, the field host and other camps stay with their existing owners and locations. It grants no county, private land, office, home move or government conversion. Exact-site settlement remains unavailable until its population and personal-presence contract exists.

Unsupported whole-following operations return camp_operation. This includes recruitment, voluntary demuster, civilian movement, reunion, admission of new aggregate members, member departure/replacement, final settlement, land awards, service-contract acceptance and the old undirected work/trade controls. Column movement carrying the court, recruitment, voluntary demuster and camp supply shortcuts are also unavailable. Ordinary field marches, combat and other permitted command operations retain their existing owners. A field host consumes only its own carried stock; it cannot automatically load a nearby or remote camp. No new civilian movement or field-procurement transaction is implied by this contract.

An ordinary forced host disband returns surviving roster people only to a county-level camp at the host's actual county that already declares their member. Other survivors disperse through the existing global accounting. A county-level field position does not prove entry into an exact-site camp. Colocated returned people and their proportional carried food enter only that actual camp; a lost remote host never injures every camp or teleports its survivors to migration.at. Complete realm extinction disperses all remaining camp pools once. No named person is killed or moved by an aggregate demographic loss.

Persistence and presentation

Optional saved mobile.camps has schema 1 and ordered entries. Each camp retains its declaration id, immutable county/site provenance, local food, harvestPeriod, member cohort pools and initial/returned/dead/settled/dispersed accounting. Its live count equals initial plus returned minus dead, settled and dispersed. Empty legacy member pools and zero legacy food prevent double ownership. The existing global conservation equation includes all camp pools and all real field hosts. Restore validates the exact declaration and bounded typed pools, counters, totals, location provenance and optional presence without initializers, payments, purchases or replacement hosts. Older declarations omit camps and retain their original single-camp behavior. No save envelope version changes.

The existing mobile command sheet lists numbered camps with actual place, people, rations, stock and current access beside the separate military hosts and one treasury. A retained Civilian camp selector targets local purchase, sale, work and partial settlement reviews. Existing confirmation, Back, Escape, Close, scroll, Details and focus behavior remain shared. Blocked old controls explain the dispersed-camp boundary. Chrome uses literal FB.T and keeps stable ids and provenance out of rendered labels.

The independent ordinary imports dispersed-camps-harbor.json and dispersed-camps-dune.json vary county/site selection, allocation, remaining access term, food, reserves, military classes and single-host versus two-column ownership. They are fictional authoring examples. No historical camp, leader guard, political constitution or headcount is supplied. A guarded personal-party binding requires its own merged contract before composition; this module does not create or interpret one.
