Access, supplies and obligations

Access, tribute and protection offers need coalition assent and a separate current outside ruler's acceptance. Acceptance rechecks the outside government's consent, ownership, capacity, generations and real payer funds. Payment occurs once on acceptance. A grantor must be the direct county holder, and the sovereign owner must be that holder or its current top superior. A superior court without direct possession cannot grant local access; foreign occupation and replacement of the holder suspend existing rights. The seasonal AI chooses the lawful direct holder as counterpart and uses the same reviewed transactions. Access never transfers sovereignty, a holder, private property or tax rights. Corridors name at most 16 inhabited counties, agreements last at most 1440 days, and a profile allows at most 12 concurrent saved agreements. Expiry, hostile relations or loss of the grantor's direct control removes access. Current routes are halted when required rights lapse.

County capacity is 15 percent of current local population. Active concessions cannot allocate more than that capacity. Seasonal pasture creates finite food from granted capacity once per agreement per period; the shared mobilePastures period ledger also limits total harvested capacity at each county, including expired and replaced concessions. Reoffering a short concession cannot harvest the same site's entire capacity repeatedly in one period. Civilians and resting fighters consume finite camp food. A host has its own conserved mobileFood inventory, filled from camp stock up to a 30-day baseline carrying capacity. It consumes that inventory once per day with ordinary supply-use modifiers. Initial carried food is withdrawn from opening camp stock; recovery receives no free stock. Demuster returns remaining carried food and military losses remove their proportional carried stock. Starvation and troop attrition reduce actual people. Local market purchases and sales exchange real provisions and real treasury money. Transfers within an owned county move stock without a payment to the same account.

Protection reserves the stated existing fighters and pays once for a finite term. It permits field-host passage through the protected realm but gives no civilian pasture right. Honoring protection opens an ordinary defensive campaign with the existing host. Member-approved offensive campaigns use the ordinary declaration preview, truce and objective checks, with the actual camp as their frontier. White peace requires a current member-approved treaty.
