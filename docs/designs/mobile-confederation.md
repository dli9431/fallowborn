Civic treaty counterparties

Personal expedition admission

Technology impact personal_expedition_admission is none. Unpaid personal consent and association require no discovery. Camp presence, host agency, finite capacity and ordinary travel and unit gates remain necessary. An admitted combatant occupies an existing counted demographic position, without adding a soldier or claiming that an aggregate loss killed that named person. Civilian capacity counts named guests separately from demographic pools. No family movement, command, membership, vow, wages or equipment follows from admission.

Acceptance of a mobile agreement is a reviewed transaction. A civic counterparty supplies its executive and constituency decision through FB.civicGovernment.decision, with the agreement's terms and its own payment burden. An access or tribute receipt is not a payment by the receiving city; protection charges the city its agreed price. The returned decision is part of the review, so changed incumbents, eligibility, ballots or diplomatic skill invalidate confirmation. Refusal leaves payments and the agreement untouched.

Mobile confederation

Personal work and admission use the protagonist's actual journey location when away from the permanent home. The canonical review stamp includes the current journey so departure or arrival invalidates a previous review. householdSnapshot is a pure detached projection of active status, civilian camp, actual court movement, finite food, thirty-day scarcity, treasury, local market stock and the current lawful access concession. Household stories observe it and delegate work through the same review and commit as the ordinary Deed. They cannot negotiate access or command the field host.

The mobile_confederation form composes ten complete strategies. Its saved coalition belongs to authority.governments[realmId].mobile. No culture, religion, date, terrain or historical identity selects the form. County holders remain ordinary public holders. A countyless coalition can retain living principals, a court, treasury, food and civilian following after its field host is lost.

People and places

Optional mobile.opening declarations bind direct bookmark opening actors to profile members and select a bound leader. An incumbent binding additionally checks the same realm's authored ruler and opening generation, preserving that generation. Unbound members keep the generated default. No second person or household is created for a bound principal. Cross-realm landed command and deferred story actors remain separate contracts. Opted-in command continuity is documented in docs/designs/mobile-command.md. Pure eligibility checks custody, liveness, age, actual origin, generation, duplicate membership and ordinary military gates before an opted-in following is created.

An authored muster partitions opening fighters by member and unit class, bounded by each member's existing population and pledged share. The ordinary migration host owns those units; its roster withdraws the exact demographic counts from the named pools. Camp food and carried provisions retain their existing conservation. The opt-in initializes after direct opening actors and before world stories for playable and observer starts. Legacy generated openings keep their original ordering and RNG behavior. The independent harbor and dune JSON examples contrast a second-member leader, two explicit people and an archery/cavalry muster with an incumbent-generation binding, one generated member and a three-class muster.

Technology impact is none independently for opening identity binding and opening composition: these declarations select existing people and allocate existing pools. Authored classes still pass the normal culture, era and discovery gates; unavailable equipment does not receive a historical-opening bypass. Separate entries are recorded in techImpactReviews.

Binding and lifecycle coverage

The existing opening_actor and same-realm realm_ruler references, member/class muster and schema-2 provenance remain the canonical authoring contract. No parallel principal registry, preparation record or initializer is needed to reuse those supported cases. Command continuity composes these bindings through schema 3. The existing mobile_opening_identity and mobile_opening_composition technology decisions remain none; this coverage adds no eligibility or saved schema.

mobile-authored-principals.spec.js complements mobile-opening-principals.spec.js and mobile-command.spec.js. It imports the independent harbor and dune JSON worlds through Mods, checks adoption of an already initialized command principal with a separate private purse, repeated initialization and matching restore, and checks original provenance after death and a lawful replacement. Its omission cases retain generated male principals aged thirty, first-member leadership, infantry composition and schema 1. The adoption cases use hidden imported scenario declarations and exercise the adoption API after household boot; they do not establish a fresh command-start boot path.

Opening initialization is for fresh campaign attachment. It creates the declared aggregate people and credits the declared opening stock once; it is not a funded later-formation transaction. Calling it later after deleting the existing row, or supplying new constant stocks, is unsupported. It reserves no future recruitment source. Cross-realm landed principals, later formation, source reservation, and independent-sponsor eligibility require their own supported contracts before content can use them. A successful opening import or adoption does not establish those capabilities.

Fresh moving hosts and recovery hosts initialize every registered unit class, including zero-count classes, through FB.hostUnits before entering the army list. Serialization and restore therefore retain the same composition without adding missing class entries; population, recruitment and casualty totals remain unchanged.

Members have stable ids, living character principals, realm scopes, weights, allegiance and explicit recruitment shares. Each member owns civilian and resting-fighter demographic pools. A deployed host has mobileFollowing, mobileCourt and mobileRoster. The roster partitions its actual men by member, culture and religion; it does not add another force total. Recruitment removes people from those pools, pays the treasury training cost and checks the existing unit-class eligibility. Casualties remove roster people once. Ordinary county reinforcement and professional replacement batches never refill a mobile roster or charge it for invented recruits. Demuster at the camp returns only surviving fighters. Generic host splitting is unavailable for this roster; reorganization requires returning fighters first.

The conserved identity is initial plus arrivals minus dead, settled and dispersed equals mobile civilians plus resting fighters plus deployed host men. Initial mobile households are separate from seeded local inhabitants. Admission and settlement use populationPoolQuote and populationPoolApply, preserving exact culture-faith cohorts and county settlement partitions. The county floor protects local inhabitants. A departing member settles its actual surviving pools and proportional camp food locally and loses its coalition voice. A final departure preserves the historical court reference in the terminal migration record. Explicit court extinction disperses remaining mobile people; it does not convert them into invented county inhabitants.

Court position has one owner, authority.migrations. A reviewed move carries the court and civilian following with the existing host. An ordinary field-host march leaves the court at its camp. Reunite the field host with the court before moving the following. A protagonist member present in the camp travels with a moving court unless already on a separate household journey. The ordinary march planner, crossing qualifications, battles and conquest transactions remain in charge. A field host away from camp consumes its carried supply and cannot withdraw food from the camp remotely.

Consent and leadership

FB.mobile.review and commit use the shared governmentSelection source coalition_leadership. quote is pure; commit re-quotes the complete current people, principals, ruler generations, host, court, finances, county control, market and relevant commitments. A stale or refused review creates no identity, charges nothing and transfers no people. Only an actual member principal can pledge or vote. A leader can submit one pending exact proposal; current principals separately assent. Changes to membership or terms clear those approvals.

Leadership candidates are living, uncaptured, non-exiled adult member principals. A vacancy uses weighted support from current living members and the profile threshold. No genealogy or fallback collateral is created. Accession updates the existing realm ruler snapshot once. A qualified existing local adult may replace a dead member principal with the leader's appointment. If every former principal is unavailable through death, an actual local adult with diplomacy of at least 3 can seek representation, then undergo the same leadership selection. Recovery does not create people or restore casualties. AI uses these transactions and current qualifications; it creates no replacement characters, waives no costs and receives no dated orders.

Access, supplies and obligations

Access, tribute and protection offers need coalition assent and a separate current outside ruler's acceptance. Acceptance rechecks the outside government's consent, ownership, capacity, generations and real payer funds. Payment occurs once on acceptance. A grantor must be the direct county holder, and the sovereign owner must be that holder or its current top superior. A superior court without direct possession cannot grant local access; foreign occupation and replacement of the holder suspend existing rights. The seasonal AI chooses the lawful direct holder as counterpart and uses the same reviewed transactions. Access never transfers sovereignty, a holder, private property or tax rights. Corridors name at most 16 inhabited counties, agreements last at most 1440 days, and a profile allows at most 12 concurrent saved agreements. Expiry, hostile relations or loss of the grantor's direct control removes access. Current routes are halted when required rights lapse.

County capacity is 15 percent of current local population. Active concessions cannot allocate more than that capacity. Seasonal pasture creates finite food from granted capacity once per agreement per period; the shared mobilePastures period ledger also limits total harvested capacity at each county, including expired and replaced concessions. Reoffering a short concession cannot harvest the same site's entire capacity repeatedly in one period. Civilians and resting fighters consume finite camp food. A host has its own conserved mobileFood inventory, filled from camp stock up to a 30-day baseline carrying capacity. It consumes that inventory once per day with ordinary supply-use modifiers. Initial carried food is withdrawn from opening camp stock; recovery receives no free stock. Demuster returns remaining carried food and military losses remove their proportional carried stock. Starvation and troop attrition reduce actual people. Local market purchases and sales exchange real provisions and real treasury money. Transfers within an owned county move stock without a payment to the same account.

Protection reserves the stated existing fighters and pays once for a finite term. It permits field-host passage through the protected realm but gives no civilian pasture right. Honoring protection opens an ordinary defensive campaign with the existing host. Member-approved offensive campaigns use the ordinary declaration preview, truce and objective checks, with the actual camp as their frontier. White peace requires a current member-approved treaty. Service commitments prevent demuster and government conversion until fulfilled or expired.

Settlement and persistence

Settlement requires member assent to the exact county and headcount, owned land or an ordinary consented award, and finished conflicting service. A land request first needs member assent to its county; the shared service owner then offers an ordinary contract. Members separately review the exact promised land, pay, duration and ruler generations before their court consents. The outside court still decides for itself. Generic federate settlement cannot bypass the coalition decision. The shared claims session and transferProvince remain the sovereignty owners. Partial settlement moves only the requested civilians and a proportional food share; mobile leadership continues. Final settlement places every remaining civilian and fighter, delivers the remaining food to the county market, retires the host and selects the declared territorial form. Treasury, local inhabitants, private household property and the same living leader remain. A protagonist becoming a territorial ruler uses the ordinary realm adoption transaction.

Fresh initialization alone consumes authored opening declarations. Restore validates compact saved data without initializing followings, replaying payments or harvests, rebuilding a host or selecting a leader. Validation rejects unknown profiles, duplicate members or demographic partitions, negative values, unbounded records, impossible references, a roster differing from host men, inconsistent court carriage, incompatible settled forms and broken conservation. RNG and UID remain the ordinary save envelope's identities. Earlier bookmarks and saves contain no new mobile records unless they were explicitly authored with this form.

Composed command policies

Already hosted openings

Optional mobile.hostedAccess records one previously accepted, finite access concession for an authored opening. It names the actual outside holder, inhabited counties including the existing camp origin, capacity for the complete following, recruitment permission and remaining duration. It initializes only at turn zero after pure batch preflight. The current living parties, lawful direct possession and combined local capacity must permit the declaration. It neither moves people nor transfers ownership, food or money. Past admission payments are not replayed, and the saved concession has no payment due. Later offers and renewals use ordinary bilateral consent and the actual public payer.

The canonical agreements array owns the concession. A versioned optional openingAccess marker records its stable identity, original turn and party generations even after expiry removes the agreement. It prevents missing or altered opening records from being repaired during restore. Changed generations, unavailable parties, hostility, loss of direct possession or insufficient capacity suspend opening access immediately. The daily boundary ends an invalid opening concession without a fine, forced departure or casualty. It cannot revive automatically when control returns. Existing negotiated agreements retain their existing rules.

Technology impact is none for mobile_hosted_opening: this is political access constrained by living authority and available capacity. It supplies no discovery, equipment, custody release or guarantee of personal safety. The ordinary ruler_agenda priority and finish choices already support costless reviews by a retained mobile leader. They preserve the original person and generation and execute no access renewal, move or protection operation. Outside rulers can still accept actual later offers through agenda_mobile_offer and its canonical consent and payment review.

Mobility is now read from coalition_court's mobileFollowing territory marker. Coalition leadership retains its existing weighted succession and consent. The independently selected retained_principal and superior_commission strategies reuse the same population, camp, treasury and host through explicit command declarations, with no member election or invented hereditary successor. command_consent revalidates public operation scope while the outside court and ordinary owners retain their checks. command_receipts grants no local or general realm income. The new schemas, private-station separation, ordinary JSON examples and none technology decisions are documented in docs/designs/mobile-command.md. Legacy generated openings and schema-1/schema-2 records keep their behavior.

Independent command followings

An authored command following may opt into schema-1 independent sponsorship with a null patron. Missing policy retains required-patron migration behavior. The actual origin, target, following, host, people, food and account retain their canonical owners. The declaration and saved migration marker must agree; no new stock or people are created during restore. Access, outside service, conquest and settlement retain their current counterpart, consent, funding and ownership requirements. Patron-based migration requests cannot run on an independent record. docs/designs/mobile-command.md defines this opt-in, finite external warrants and the two independent ordinary JSON examples.
