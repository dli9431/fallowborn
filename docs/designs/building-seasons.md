Seasonal building commitments

Seasonal policies compose actual water operations and conserved production at their existing physical assets. The bundled buildingSeasonProfiles table is empty. No historical building, farm, herd, worker, water, fuel, grazing right or material lot is initialized. Local availability reads FB.localSeason, so an imported southern summer can occur during the global winter. The climate and stock owners retain their units and identities.

An effective bookmark or complete revision binds a buildingSeasonAssignments record to exactly one existing operation assignment or production site. Its profile declares permitted local season kinds, a period of at most 90 days, a maximum operating term, optional local care, explicit per-good lifetimes and an optional stock-backed use of one existing building field. Detached compilation resolves the same-import catalogues and normalized physical layout. One physical asset has one seasonal assignment. No executable predicate, arbitrary state path or autonomous population exists.

Canonical opening and operation

buildingSeasons.quote and commit serve the player and AI. Open commits one window per assignment and period against the original actor, person, generation and canonical water-operation or production asset identity. Optional co-located water/grazing requirements are real siteResources reserve/consume transactions staged together before publication. Opening has no cash payment, output or benefit. It neither supplies fuel nor performs care or a workday. Production still consumes actual recipe inputs and funded ordinary workdays; water service still pays its canonical work cost and prorated upkeep. A water operation cannot also declare a material-stock benefit. Imported production buildings with a stock benefit expose only its one matching positive existing-field scalar; without a stock benefit they cannot expose ongoing scalars through this adapter.

The term ends at the earliest declared period boundary, maximum duration, actual calendar transition or renewable required-resource boundary. The local kind decides eligibility, never a hardcoded season index. Requiring care checks that the original living free adult remains local and outside incompatible household, enterprise, service or military work. Existing property authority, institution permission, custody and contested-county gates remain binding. Lost authority, care, resource permission, ruin or replacement immediately hides only the new benefit. The ordinary daily boundary records interruption once. An interrupted term cannot restart in its original period. Property, population and previous payments persist.

Existing canonical production quotes require the active term for reserve/work/finish; ordinary cancellation remains possible. Actual fuel and water remain recipe inputs rather than invented seasonal inventory. A water service needs the active term and funds only its remaining intersection, including a partial term's exact upkeep fraction. Ordinary construction retains its full invoice and reserves and checks current seasonal availability; it does not open or fund a term. Unbound assets retain their exact defaults. Construction and opening are separate reviews, and a term alone never produces a scalar.

Stock and committed benefit windows

stockLives attaches an absolute lifetime to specific actual production goods at the bound site. Opening lots date from their explicit original asset opening; batch lots date from their actual committed batch close; producer lots date from the canonical diverted-output receipt. An exchange uses its actual receipt date and conservatively inherits the earliest input expiry. A receiving policy may shorten that deadline and never extend it, even at an otherwise untagged buyer. The bounded original lot graph remains the sole provenance source. An expired lot has zero available quantity even before a boundary writes its loss receipt. An allocation consumes exactly the declared unreserved owned quantity from the ordinary lot, once per term. Its existing-field benefit lasts only until the earliest benefit duration, lot expiry or operating-term end. Allocation grants no cash, people, research discovery, military stock or new effect channel, and cannot be sold or used a second time. The shared building index includes the current seasonal projection, so all fiscal, demographic, migration, piety, research and settlement readers agree even between boundary writes.

The ordinary daily boundary closes pending batches whose reserved input expires, retaining completed work and non-refundable processing costs. It releases any still-releasable canonical water claim and cancels without output. It then debits each remaining expired lot once, recording the actual lost quantity. Allocated quantity already left the lot and is never lost or allocated again. Repeating the hook performs no further debit. No stock balance, profit, property, person or population is copied into seasonal state. Only the existing production lot has a remaining quantity; seasonal debits are strict provenance that participates in the material conservation equation. Ordinary stock consumers must use lotAvailable/lotExpiry when reviewing dated lots and retain debited in that equation.

Persistence and limits

The optional state.buildingSeasons version 1 stores definition, revision, terms and debits. Terms bind original asset/actor/generation, scheduled boundary and typed source-use references. endedTurn is written at most once. Debits record actual allocation or terminal expiry against a canonical production lot, never another inventory. The revision equals the number of terms, debits and closed terms. Exact fields, bounded dates, local historical kind, asset/source/lot identity, once-only allocation/loss, prior seasonal coverage of funded work, source conservation and the production equation validate before adoption. Definition fingerprints include structural assignment/policy/effect values, while ordinary exact mod fingerprints cover the full imports. Save restoration does not initialize, renew, work, consume, cancel, allocate or pay. A pending expired record may restore unchanged but has no available inputs until the normal boundary records loss.

Limits are 128 profiles and assignments, sixteen distinct allowed kinds, eight explicit stock lives and resource requirements per assignment, 1-90 day periods and maximum terms, 1-360 day stock lifetimes, 1-90 day benefit duration, 512 term markers and 1024 debit receipts. Full journals stop further commitments; they are not silently pruned. Expired inventory remains unavailable if a journal is full. AI considers at most 32 successful window openings per dispatch and uses detached canonical operation/production reviews to avoid consuming resource capacity for an unfunded or inputless plant. It may commit already owned useful stock; it never fabricates production, demand, cash or a second workday. Performance, save growth and physical-device acceptance remain owner-reviewed.
