Aristocratic monarchy

Scope and data ownership

aristocratic_monarchy is a settled court with royal descent, permanent hereditary interests and personal public commissions. data/aristocratic_courts.js declares its generic chartered profile and its dated Sasanian profile. js/government_aristocracy.js supplies reusable strategies after the common recognition and administration modules. Engine behavior names no Persian dynasty, province, office or historical outcome. The fictional acceptance fixture declares a different royal pool and provincial households through the same interfaces.

The Sasanian profile applies to fresh 476 openings, which save opening revision wider_v1. Peroz and his already declared relatives retain their existing identities. Generated Mihran and Karen principals, their adult children and a generated capital governor fill simulation roles without asserting named spring-476 incumbencies. The hidden Peroz ruler start supports owner review through the normal existing-ruler journey. Earlier openings and saved regnum courts retain their own declarations. The dated research and abstraction limits are recorded in docs/research/aristocratic-monarchy.md.

Canonical facts

The existing compact royal tree and actual character genealogy establish descent from the declared founder. A government row has an optional schema-1 aristocracy record with profileId, originRealmId, royalRealmId, one royalRoots reference, interests, regency and optional lastPeriod. Each permanent interest references one existing perpetual authority appointment and two existing customary rights for Peace and Consent. It stores a dynasty and county scope, not another family tree or territorial owner. Public remuneration uses that appointment's publicTerms. Its provincialTerms schema 1 contains interestId, levy, assent or withheld status and changedTurn. Private estates stay in the opening estate ledger and private household property keeps its established owner.

Direct sovereign holders remain the territorial baseline. A governor's personal public commission supplies scoped Peace and Revenue; a commander operates an existing host. A hereditary family keeps local jurisdiction, its public assignment and its separate private estate. Dismissing a governor cannot revoke a family's inherited rights. Family probate transfers the same appointment and rights to an existing free adult of that dynasty after the principal dies, without creating a replacement person or moving estates. Existing private estate inheritance remains a separate transaction.

Recognition and succession

Changing provincial burdens invalidates a positive royal pledge but preserves an explicit withdrawal by the same principal in the same ruler generation. Provincial withholding does not authorize the AI to choose a royal candidate for that principal. A recognized player household accession removes the new ruler from both succession orders, updates the next-heir pointer and clears any matching opening preference before the campaign can be saved.

The common governmentRecognition quote/apply transaction reads a court designation and every permanent family seat. Dead, captive, exiled, underage, dispossessed or legally ineffective principals cannot support a candidate, but their seat retains its weight in the denominator. An eligible candidate must be living, free and descended from the court's founder through actual ancestry. Nonroyal wealth, office and provincial influence never confer that ancestry. AI compact member ids and player character ids are translated at the canonical court boundary.

The profile requires more than its declared recognition threshold. A principal can back a different eligible candidate or explicitly withdraw support. A ruler can negotiate an existing family's support using accepted revenue and levy burdens, paying the declared cost from that court's real treasury. Quotes bind the candidate's life, parents, ancestry, generation, principals, local rights, obligations, source commitments and treasury. The apply path recomputes the full quote before spending or recording a vote. Recognition keeps one record per seat; succession clears the current votes and resolves once.

Accession requires death, voluntary abdication or current judicial displacement. Divided or absent recognition leaves an explicit public vacancy under the continuing administration. A supported minor requires a living adult provincial principal as steward. The steward must remain an active hereditary principal; loss of life, freedom or jurisdiction blocks reserved court decisions until a reviewed replacement is confirmed. Stewardship ends at adulthood without replacing the king or moving property. The strongest available interest and lexical tie break are simulation policies, not historical offices or a formal constitutional ballot.

Player crown absorption, an existing-ruler start and separation of a public court from an unrecognized household heir preserve one original royal tree and rekey public institution references. A political incumbent from another court retains its birth reference without becoming a new royal root. A recognized household heir continues the public crown once; an unrecognized private heir keeps household inheritance while the public court remains separate. Dormant aristocratic ancestry and rights survive an explicit change to ordinary dynastic government.

Provincial economy and consent

Revenue terms express the fraction of county receipts retained by the court before other existing public assignments. The hereditary principal receives the complementary source-backed share through ordinary public payroll. Aggregate assignments cannot exceed the same source. No transaction invents money, raises another tax pool or credits an officer during a pure projection. Provincial levy terms scale that province's ordinary recruitment capacity. Withheld or ineffective local assent halves the public revenue base and reduces provincial levy contribution to 35 percent of baseline. These numerical burdens are openly declared simulation choices.

A living principal can withhold or restore assent. Court negotiation is funded, respects declared minimum and maximum burdens and clears that interest's former positive recognition while retaining a current principal's explicit refusal. Enforcing a withheld obligation requires current scoped Peace authority and spends local popular support. Low popular support feeds AI refusal; the ordinary support/unrest and war systems retain rebellion and battlefield consequences. Ordinary public war, treaty and levy decisions need a functioning court or steward, while extraordinary taxes and new grants also require the relevant local interests' assent. Private production and household freedom retain their normal owners and rules.

Existing subject/client hierarchy, paid peace tribute and alliances/protection remain distinct external relationships. Recognition does not convert a supervised court's faith or its county communities. Territorial conquest changes existing sovereign control through the normal holder strategy; surviving local rights and private estates remain independent records. A reviewed government conversion requires the current adult court and assent of every living hereditary principal. Return uses the surviving royal and provincial facts through the Provincial continuity deed, which also reviews family probate during an intervening dynastic government. AI courts continue that same probate through the existing fiscal scheduler, without reseeding households. No restored older regnum receives new opening families or offices.

Save contract and technology

Detached save-v4 validation checks the exact founder, original/current court, declared profile, unique family jurisdictions, existing personal principals, canonical appointments and rights, bounded rates, recognition ancestry and regency references before adoption. An active aristocratic government requires its saved constituency. Dormant facts under another form remain valid. Changed or deceased participants remain historical facts until normal play reviews them; restore does not seed, replace, pay, probate, vote or resolve. All projection caches and action quotes remain transient, and the save envelope and existing subsystem schema versions are unchanged.

The technology-impact ledger has independent none decisions for royal recognition, hereditary jurisdiction, provincial bargains and government conversion. These capabilities depend on ancestry, law, assent and real resources. Baseline political authority has no credible research prerequisite. Existing equipment and fiscal improvements keep their established technology effects; this profile grants no later military division or fiscal reform.
