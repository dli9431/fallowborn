Episcopal civil rent claims

The 1204 Roman declaration exposes one bounded civil experience over the ordinary
rent of an actual delegated settlement in Rome. Begin with the ordinary Roman
baron household, or use an existing private grant in the see's temporal territory.
Visit the diocese, Hold ecclesiastical court and The Bishopric open the same
costless review for the current possessor, canonical bishop or competent ruler.
An ungranted site creates no opportunity. A church building, clerical career,
spiritual claim or piety score supplies neither property nor temporal jurisdiction.

Evidence and scope

The existing settlement grant, founder, grant turn and charter identify the exact
rent right. The claim is an assertion about that charter, never title to the
household's land. It invents no missed payment or historical litigation. The
possessor can request a review, contest the evidence, propose terms, accept the
other party's offer or decline. The bishop can demand review, negotiate or abandon
the assertion. Abandonment and refusal retain the ordinary charter. Only a
competent temporal ruler can adjudicate a composition or refuse enforcement;
the shared justice adapter checks the actual ruler, county jurisdiction, Peace
and public readiness. The declaration's historical bishop happens also to hold
temporal government. That coincidence does not extend spiritual jurisdiction.

Accounting and lifecycle

A recurring composition replaces the tax share in this holding's existing
seasonal remittance. The same projection supplies the private debit and public
receipt; there is no second periodic collection. Compensation is an alternative
single private payment which commutes that rent to zero for the declared term.
It debits the protagonist's household purse or the NPC possessor's existing barony
account and credits the see's existing public treasury. Its unique receipt lives
in authority.receipts. The bishop's private purse, any secular treasury, buildings,
land, population and military service are unchanged. Reviews and hearings charge
no fee. Existing debt and other dues remain with their original owners.

Every composition begins at the next seasonal boundary, avoiding a refund or
second charge for the current period. It lasts one to eight seasons and then
returns to the ordinary charter. Occupation or loss of the original county
jurisdiction suspends the concession while its fixed clock continues. Recovery
within that term resumes the same concession. Inheritance of the same grant
retains the agreement; a new grant or changed source charter cannot acquire it.
Accepted institutional money and agreements survive episcopal vacancy and
succession without entering the bishop's private inheritance. An unaccepted
offer binds the exact living principals and public generations; their replacement,
changed evidence, expiry or inadequate private funds prevents acceptance. A
fresh demand can renew an interrupted pending review, without creating arrears.

Supported participant coverage

Player and NPC parties share quote/apply. NPC possessors have a declared accept,
contest or decline policy; NPC bishops accept a valid funded composition offered
by the possessor. An NPC temporal court has a compose or reject policy. Responses
occur after an explicit player proposal or enforcement request. There is no
autonomous simulation of every episcopal debtor, private prosecution, religious
guilt, confiscation or campaign. The court remedy is the disclosed finite rent
composition, limited to this recorded charter and no higher than its existing
share. Contested evidence requires the actual temporal court rather than an
automatic spiritual sentence.

Persistence and reusable interfaces

FB.episcopalClaims.list/snapshot/quote are pure. apply rechecks the entire quote,
including turn, exact declaration, principals, property, political consent,
jurisdiction and both relevant balances. respond uses the same transactions.
dues is a payment-free adapter inside settlementFiscalProjection; it substitutes
only the agreed share of the same rent base. justiceCivilRentJurisdiction is the
shared justice owner's temporal court gate. These interfaces expose civil facts
and receipts for other consumers without manufacturing criminal evidence.

The optional bounded authority.episcopalClaims ledger stores ids and numeric
facts, pins the exact effective definition and cross-validates compensation
receipts. Matching restore neither initializes a claim nor replays a payment.
Malformed records, duplicate sources, changed definitions and unmatched receipts
reject on the detached save. Released saves without this ledger retain their
existing meaning; the save envelope is unchanged.

The Roman rates, four-season term and compensation are game-scale compositions,
not attested figures or an allegation that a particular historical tenant owed
arrears. The independent ordinary JSON example episcopal-river-rent.json uses a
fictional two-season Spoleto composition with different rates, price, deadline
and response policies. Both require an actual private grant before play.

Technology impact episcopal_rent_compositions is none. Property, consent,
competent jurisdiction and real funds supply the eligibility; no discovery is a
credible prerequisite. Ordinary economic and military technology gates remain.

tests/e2e/specs/episcopal-claims.spec.js covers actual grants, pure review, disputed
evidence, refused enforcement, real conserved compensation, recurring paired
remittances, finite expiry, stale/poor offers, institutional succession and
private inheritance, lost jurisdiction, exact current-content restoration,
ordinary JSON import and retained mobile review navigation.
